# Public Language Dictionary

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses the **Public Language Dictionary** to keep public, investor, community, product, partner, website, Medium, whitepaper, FAQ, and product-page language consistent.

The dictionary defines approved terms, avoided terms, product names, risk boundaries, token/credit separation, stablecoin compensation language, product-specific boundaries, and public-safe alternatives for sensitive topics.

FUZE’s public language starts with this master position:

**FUZE is a transparency-first AI SaaS platform building practical products on top of shared infrastructure for identity, credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, and ecosystem participation.**

FUZE’s public execution order is:

**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**

FUZE’s token and credit separation is:

**FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage.**

FUZE’s compensation principle is:

**Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation.**

The Public Language Dictionary helps FUZE avoid unclear or unsafe language around token price, exchange listing, liquidity, profit, payout, public token sale, Platform Credits, games, AI products, market products, sponsored visibility, community products, and future economic frameworks.

This paper defines the approved FUZE public vocabulary and how it applies across the platform.

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE presents public language as part of public trust.

FUZE position:

**FUZE public language stays product-first, transparency-first, compliance-aware, and risk-bounded.**

FUZE uses public language to explain what products do, how platform rails support them, how Platform Credits work, how FUZE token utility is separated from product usage, and which outcomes FUZE does not guarantee.

The dictionary follows FUZE’s tokenomics principle:

**Purpose-specific allocation. Vault-based control. Controlled circulation. Product-first utility. Long-term ecosystem alignment.**

FUZE avoids language that makes the platform sound like a token speculation project, guaranteed-income product, uncontrolled AI system, casino, financial-advice service, market-manipulation product, public token sale, or unsupported compliance claim.

The dictionary supports public materials across:

- FUZE platform papers
- HerHelp.com
- ZAGA.io
- product pages
- tokenomics pages
- investor materials
- community FAQ
- Medium posts
- website sections
- public announcements
- partner materials
- whitepaper sections
- product onboarding
- Platform Credit documentation
- sponsored-placement policies
- game pages
- market-product pages
- governance pages
