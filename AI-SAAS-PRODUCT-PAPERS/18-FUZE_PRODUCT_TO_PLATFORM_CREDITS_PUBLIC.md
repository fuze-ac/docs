# FUZE Product to Platform Credits

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses Platform Credits as the product usage layer across the FUZE ecosystem.

Platform Credits are for product usage. FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. This separation is central to FUZE’s public model because it helps users, investors, community members, partners, and product customers understand the difference between software usage and token-based ecosystem participation.

FUZE is a transparency-first AI SaaS platform building practical products on top of shared infrastructure for identity, credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, and ecosystem participation. The FUZE product family includes HerHelp, SheetLayer AI, ShopOS AI, SpeakShop AI, TrainLayer AI, CommunityLayer AI, ZAGA, ZAGA Arena, ZAGA Districts, QTB, AIMM, AIE, ToolGrid, and Botmad.

Each product can use Platform Credits for different product actions: AI usage, reports, dashboards, workflows, queue systems, voice generation, data preparation, assistant validation, community summaries, game actions, event intelligence, sponsored visibility, market intelligence, liquidity-operations support, document generation, automation support, and workspace usage.

Platform Credits are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens. They are a product-usage accounting layer designed to support pricing, billing, usage control, metering, reporting, and product access across FUZE products.

This paper explains the public relationship between FUZE products and Platform Credits.

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE presents Platform Credits as the product usage rail across FUZE products.

FUZE position:

**FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage.**

FUZE uses Platform Credits to connect product actions, AI usage, feature access, workflows, reports, sponsored visibility, game-related product actions, and platform services into one shared usage model.

The Platform Credits model follows these principles:

| Principle | Public Meaning |
|---|---|
| Product usage | Credits are used for software, AI, workflow, report, tool, game, and platform service usage |
| Clear separation | Credits are separate from FUZE token |
| Usage metering | Credits help measure product consumption and service usage |
| Pricing flexibility | Credits can support subscriptions, add-ons, usage packs, workspace billing, and partner packages |
| Product consistency | Different FUZE products can share one usage rail |
| Reporting | Credit usage can create clearer product analytics and owner reports |
| Access control | Credits can support feature access, limits, and plan rules |
| Public clarity | Credits reduce confusion between software usage and token participation |

Platform Credits help FUZE build a platform company instead of disconnected products with separate billing logic.

Platform Credits are for product usage and are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

### 6.2 Token Boundary

Credit purchases, subscriptions, add-ons, refunds, expiry, invoices, tax treatment, app-store rules, payment rail rules, and enterprise billing remain subject to final product terms, payment provider rules, accounting review, tax review, and applicable law.

### 6.5 Abuse and Fair Use Boundary

FUZE can apply limits, rate controls, review gates, abuse prevention, anti-spam controls, anti-scraping controls, and account safeguards where product usage creates security, cost, fairness, or policy risk.

### 6.7 Sponsored Visibility Boundary
