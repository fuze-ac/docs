# HerHelp SheetLayer AI

## Executive Summary

SheetLayer AI is HerHelp’s AI-powered spreadsheet operating layer for real business data.

SheetLayer AI turns messy business spreadsheets into ready-to-use dashboards, role-safe views, workflows, sync systems, and LINE-ready business tools. It helps SMEs and teams keep the spreadsheets they already use while making those records safer, clearer, more useful, and easier to connect with daily operations.

Many small businesses already use Google Sheets, Excel files, CSV exports, shared folders, and daily spreadsheets as lightweight operating systems. These files often contain customer records, orders, payment status, stock movement, staff data, costs, formulas, margins, product lists, bookings, registration forms, and daily reports. The problem is not that these businesses lack data. The problem is that the data is scattered, sensitive, difficult to monitor, hard to permission safely, and difficult to turn into usable business systems.

SheetLayer AI solves this by adding an AI-assisted layer above familiar spreadsheet workflows. It maps business meaning, creates dashboards, protects sensitive fields, turns records into workflow actions, syncs daily files, and connects sheet data into HerHelp and FUZE platform rails.

SheetLayer AI is important to FUZE because it gives HerHelp a familiar data entry point for SMEs and prepares business data for ShopOS AI, CommunityLayer AI, TrainLayer AI, reporting, AI orchestration, Platform Credits, and future business automation.

## 3. Public Model

### 3.1 SheetLayer AI Product Model

SheetLayer AI is organized around five core modules:

| Module | Public Role |
|---|---|
| SheetMap | Maps columns, tabs, formulas, daily files, data types, business meaning, relationships, and sensitive fields |
| SheetView | Turns mapped data into dashboards, owner views, staff tables, customer pages, mobile views, and LINE-ready views |
| SheetFlow | Turns spreadsheet records into workflow actions such as reminders, approvals, follow-ups, status changes, and task assignment |
| SheetSync | Handles imports, exports, recurring updates, daily files, Google Drive folders, CSV files, mapping memory, and source history |
| SheetGuard | Protects confidential data through role-based access, field visibility, masking, AI access control, export control, and audit logs |

SheetLayer AI public promise:

**Keep your sheets. Let AI make them safer, clearer, and easier to use.**

### 3.2 SheetMap

SheetMap is the structure-understanding layer.

It uses AI-assisted mapping and controlled business rules to identify what spreadsheet data means. It can map:

| Sheet Area | SheetMap Output |
|---|---|
| Column names | Standardized business fields |
| Tabs | Business sections, reporting periods, departments, branches, products, or workflows |
| Formulas | Business logic, calculations, totals, margins, and dependencies |
| Data types | Text, number, date, phone, address, currency, status, quantity, category, ID, note |
| Sensitive fields | Phone numbers, addresses, costs, margins, staff records, restricted notes, payment details |
| Relationships | Customer-to-order, product-to-stock, staff-to-task, order-to-payment, booking-to-status |
| Daily files | Recurring imports, daily sales files, staff files, event files, campaign files |
| Business meaning | The real operational purpose of each field and record |

Example mapping:

| User Sheet Column | SheetLayer Field | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Name | customer_name | Customer identity label |
| Phone | phone_number | Customer contact field |
| Product | product_name | Product ordered or tracked |
| Payment Status | payment_status | Paid, unpaid, pending, refunded, or verified state |
| Quantity | quantity | Unit count |
| Cost | product_cost | Sensitive cost field |
| Note | order_note | Staff or customer note |

SheetMap helps turn informal spreadsheet data into structured business context.

### 3.3 SheetView

Public view types include:

| View Type | Public Role |

SheetView makes spreadsheet data usable for different audiences without exposing unnecessary fields.

### 3.5 SheetSync

SheetSync handles spreadsheet update, import, export, and continuity.

Many SMEs do not have one perfect master spreadsheet. They often create files by day, week, campaign, branch, staff member, event, product line, or platform export.

SheetSync supports:

| Sync Area | Public Role |
|---|---|
| Google Sheets connection | Connects existing live business sheets |
| Excel import | Brings existing files into the mapping workflow |
| CSV upload | Handles exports from POS, delivery, forms, ads, communities, or other systems |
| Google Drive folder sync | Watches recurring files and folder-based workflows |
| Daily file detection | Recognizes repeated daily or weekly sheet patterns |
| Mapping memory | Reuses known mappings across similar files |
| Change tracking | Tracks new rows, changed fields, and updates |
| Conflict handling | Flags mismatched values and review needs |
| Source history | Preserves where data came from and when it changed |
| Export | Sends cleaned views or reports back to sheets or CSV where applicable |

SheetSync helps businesses move from scattered daily files into a more continuous operating system.

### 3.6 SheetGuard

SheetGuard is the privacy, permission, and control layer.

It protects sensitive business data through:

| Control | Public Role |
|---|---|
| Role-based access | Owners, managers, staff, reviewers, and viewers see different views |
| Field-level visibility | Sensitive fields can be hidden, masked, limited, or owner-only |
| Sensitive field detection | AI-assisted and rule-based detection of restricted or high-risk fields |
| Masked fields | Phone numbers, addresses, costs, margins, payment notes, and personal data can be partially hidden |
| AI access control | AI tasks can be restricted from using sensitive fields by default |
| Export control | Downloads, copies, and exports can be limited by role |
| Audit logs | Tracks important views, edits, exports, AI actions, and permission changes |
| Workspace separation | Prevents business or client data from mixing across contexts |

Example field visibility:

| Field | Owner | Manager | Staff | AI Usage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Customer name | Visible | Visible | Visible where needed | Allowed where approved |
| Phone number | Visible | Visible | Masked | Restricted by default |
| Address | Visible | Limited | Limited or hidden | Restricted by default |
| Product cost | Visible | Hidden or limited | Hidden | Restricted by default |
| Margin | Visible | Hidden or limited | Hidden | Restricted by default |
| Staff note | Visible | Visible where approved | Limited | Restricted where sensitive |
| Payment note | Visible | Limited | Limited | Restricted by default |

SheetGuard makes SheetLayer AI safer for real business data.

## 6. Risk Boundaries and Safeguards

SheetLayer AI uses risk boundaries around business data, privacy, spreadsheet accuracy, AI usage, permissions, exports, and workflow automation.

### 6.1 Spreadsheet Accuracy Boundary

SheetLayer AI helps map and organize spreadsheets, but source files can contain errors, missing data, inconsistent formulas, duplicate records, outdated fields, or unclear business logic. Users remain responsible for reviewing critical outputs before business use.

### 6.2 AI Output Boundary

SheetLayer AI supports data mapping, summaries, dashboard setup, workflow preparation, cleanup, and insight generation. AI outputs require review where business decisions, customer communication, legal requirements, financial records, staff action, or operational commitments are involved.

### 6.3 Privacy and Sensitive Data Boundary

SheetLayer AI can process personal data, customer records, staff data, costs, margins, payment status, addresses, notes, and business-confidential information. Access control, sensitive field detection, masking, role-based permissions, export limits, audit logs, and retention settings are important safeguards.

### 6.4 Permission Boundary

Role-safe views reduce unnecessary exposure, but business owners and workspace admins remain responsible for assigning roles, reviewing permissions, checking shared links, and controlling exports.

### 6.5 Workflow Boundary

SheetFlow can help turn rows into tasks, reminders, approvals, and status updates. Businesses remain responsible for confirming actions, staff instructions, customer communication, payment checks, and final operational decisions.

### 6.6 Platform Credits Boundary

Platform Credits are for product usage and are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

Credits can support sheet mapping, AI usage, reports, workflows, sync actions, dashboards, and automation. They are product usage credits, not financial instruments.

### 6.7 Development Standards Boundary

FUZE uses standards-inspired or standards-aligned processes. FUZE does not claim formal certification unless certification has been completed.

FUZE applies structured documentation, requirements, testing, security review, privacy review, and release controls around SheetLayer AI because it handles business data.

### 6.8 Token Boundary

Normal SheetLayer AI usage does not require users to understand FUZE token systems. FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage.

FUZE does not guarantee token price, liquidity, listing, profit, return, payout, or market performance.
