# FUZE Product Language Dictionary

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses the Product Language Dictionary to keep public product communication clear, consistent, and risk-aware across the full FUZE ecosystem.

FUZE is a transparency-first AI SaaS platform building practical products on top of shared infrastructure for identity, credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, and ecosystem participation. The FUZE product family includes HerHelp, SheetLayer AI, ShopOS AI, SpeakShop AI, TrainLayer AI, CommunityLayer AI, ZAGA, ZAGA Arena, ZAGA Districts, QTB, AIMM, AIE, ToolGrid, and Botmad.

A broad platform needs a shared language system. Without a shared dictionary, public readers can confuse product usage with investment exposure, Platform Credits with tokens, game resources with financial returns, AI support with full automation, sponsored visibility with organic output, and future participation systems with immediate public offers.

The FUZE Product Language Dictionary defines approved public wording, product names, product descriptions, terms to avoid, safer replacement wording, risk-boundary language, and public explanation patterns. It helps investors, community members, strategic partners, product users, SMEs, Web3 communities, and public readers understand FUZE without unsafe claims or internal-style wording.

This dictionary supports one core FUZE execution principle:

**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE presents product language as part of public trust.

FUZE position:

**FUZE uses clear product language to separate practical software, Platform Credits, token utility, AI assistance, game mechanics, sponsored visibility, and market intelligence.**

The FUZE product language model follows these rules:

| Language Rule | Public Meaning |
|---|---|
| Use official product names | Product names stay consistent across public papers, website pages, investor materials, and community FAQ |
| Use product-first wording | FUZE explains what each product does before discussing broader ecosystem participation |
| Separate token and credits | FUZE token is for ecosystem participation; Platform Credits are for product usage |
| Separate games and finance | Game resources, Net Worth, roles, drops, and scoring remain gameplay concepts unless final policy states otherwise |
| Keep AI bounded | AI products support users, teams, workflows, and review; they do not remove human responsibility |
| Keep sponsored content labeled | Sponsored visibility is clearly labeled and reviewed |
| Avoid hype and unsafe claims | FUZE avoids price, return, revenue, liquidity, listing, payout, trading, growth, or outcome-assurance wording |
| Use public-ready language | Public papers use finished public documentation language rather than operational planning language |

FUZE uses the dictionary to make product communication safer, clearer, and more reusable.

## 6. Risk Boundaries and Safeguards

FUZE applies product language safeguards across public materials.

### 6.1 Token Language Boundary

FUZE does not guarantee token price, liquidity, listing, profit, return, payout, or market performance.

### 6.2 Platform Credits Language Boundary

Platform Credits are for product usage and are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

### 6.3 Seed Round Language Boundary

FUZE’s Seed Round discussions are private strategic fundraising conversations. Full details, structure, and terms are shared privately with qualified interested parties. This is not a public token sale.

### 6.4 AI Language Boundary

AI outputs can be incomplete, outdated, incorrect, or sensitive to source quality. Human review remains important for sensitive or consequential uses.

### 6.5 Game Language Boundary

Game resources, scoring systems, quests, city economies, and digital-asset utility are product and gameplay systems. Public communication separates gameplay from financial return promises.

### 6.6 Market Product Language Boundary

QTB and AIMM use bounded intelligence language. QTB does not provide financial advice, autonomous trading execution, or profit assurance. AIMM does not guarantee liquidity, trading profit, listing performance, market outcome, or autonomous market action without controlled permissions.

### 6.7 Sponsored Visibility Language Boundary

ToolGrid sponsored placements are subject to advertiser review, destination checks, sponsored-labeling rules, prohibited-claim rules, scam and phishing screening, takedown controls, and public trust safeguards.

### 6.8 Botmad Language Boundary

Botmad supports supervised work sessions, reviewable artifacts, and execution evidence. It does not remove human review, expose secrets publicly, or operate as uncontrolled automation.

### 6.9 Development Standards Boundary

FUZE uses standards-inspired or standards-aligned processes. FUZE does not claim formal certification unless certification has been completed.
