# QTB

## Executive Summary

QTB is FUZE’s AI-powered trading intelligence and market interpretation product for crypto participants.

QTB helps traders, analysts, founders, operators, and crypto-native teams move from raw market data, fragmented signals, noisy narratives, and scattered alerts toward clearer interpretation, structured research, and reviewable decision-support workflows.

Crypto markets are fast-moving, fragmented, noisy, and cognitively expensive. Most users do not suffer from lack of information. They suffer from lack of structured intelligence: the ability to understand what matters, compare signals, summarize market context, document reasoning, and separate signal from noise under time pressure.

QTB addresses this gap by acting as an intelligence layer for market understanding. It is not positioned as financial advice, a guaranteed trading result system, a black-box autonomous trading agent, or a profit-assurance product. QTB provides AI-assisted market intelligence, interpretation, research support, and decision-support workflows.

Inside FUZE, QTB is strategically important because it gives FUZE a crypto-native AI intelligence product that connects to shared rails for identity, workspaces, Platform Credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, privacy, security, and governance. QTB strengthens the FUZE platform by showing how AI can support high-value workflows while staying bounded, reviewable, and risk-aware.

| Problem | Public Meaning |

| Information overload | Users face more data than they can process |

| No review trail | Users cannot easily preserve why a view or thesis was formed |

## 6. Risk Boundaries and Safeguards

QTB uses risk boundaries around financial-market interpretation, AI outputs, data quality, decision support, user responsibility, Platform Credits, and public communication.

### 6.1 QTB Boundary

QTB provides AI-assisted market intelligence, interpretation, research support, and decision-support workflows. QTB does not provide financial advice, guaranteed trading results, autonomous trading execution, or profit assurance.

### 6.2 User Responsibility Boundary

Users remain responsible for their own trading, investment, research, risk management, asset custody, compliance, and financial decisions. QTB outputs are informational and decision-support oriented.

### 6.3 AI Output Boundary

AI-generated summaries, interpretations, alerts, reports, and research notes can be incomplete, outdated, incorrect, or sensitive to source quality. Users need to review important outputs before relying on them.

### 6.4 Market Data Boundary

Market data can be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, unavailable, or limited by provider terms. QTB output quality depends on available data, source coverage, timestamps, and integration readiness.

### 6.5 No Autonomous Execution Boundary

QTB does not execute trades by default. Any future action-supporting workflow requires explicit user control, permissioning, security review, auditability, legal review, and product-policy readiness.

### 6.6 Platform Credits Boundary

Platform Credits are for product usage and are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

Credits can support market summaries, signal interpretation, watchlist intelligence, reports, alerts, research workflows, and workspace usage. They are product usage credits, not financial instruments.

### 6.7 Token Boundary

Normal QTB usage does not require users to understand FUZE token systems. FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage.

FUZE does not guarantee token price, liquidity, listing, profit, return, payout, or market performance.

### 6.8 Development Standards Boundary

FUZE uses standards-inspired or standards-aligned processes. FUZE does not claim formal certification unless certification has been completed.
