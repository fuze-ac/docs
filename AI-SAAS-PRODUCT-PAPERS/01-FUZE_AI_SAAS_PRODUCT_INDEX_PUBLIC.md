# FUZE AI SaaS Product Index

## Executive Summary

FUZE products address practical work across business data, shops, training, communities, games, market research, liquidity operations, events, utility discovery, and supervised AI assistance.

This index helps a reader choose the right product paper. It groups products by audience and job, shows how their scopes differ, and links directly to the detailed workflow for each one. Product availability and readiness should be checked separately from the approved public definition.

Shared platform capabilities can support these experiences, but the product remains the reader's starting point. Each detailed paper owns its users, features, data controls, Platform Credit use, reporting, and concise boundary.

## 2. HerHelp AI SaaS Family

HerHelp groups products that turn recurring business and community tasks into guided AI-supported workflows.

| Product | Primary users | Core job | Typical output |
|---|---|---|---|
| HerHelp | Individuals, teams, and SMEs | Access practical AI assistance across supported modules | Draft, summary, plan, report, or workflow artifact |
| SheetLayer AI | Spreadsheet users and operators | Understand, map, and act on business data | Mapping, formula, dashboard, or data summary |
| ShopOS AI | Shops and local businesses | Coordinate customer and back-office operations | Menu, order, queue, stock, staff, loyalty, delivery, or report record |
| SpeakShop AI | Shops, booths, markets, events, and promoters | Prepare spoken promotional content | Script, announcement, localized variant, or sound-pack copy |
| TrainLayer AI | Trainers, employers, educators, and communities | Turn source knowledge into reusable learning | Guide, lesson, quiz, onboarding, or training module |
| CommunityLayer AI | Community owners, administrators, moderators, and support teams | Operate communities with structured assistance | Summary, queue, support draft, safety record, or admin report |

These products can be used independently. A business can also connect them where the workflow benefits, such as using SheetLayer AI with ShopOS AI data or TrainLayer AI for staff onboarding.

## 3. ZAGA Game Family

ZAGA is the game and token-utility context for two distinct game products.

### ZAGA Arena

ZAGA Arena is designed around quick battle sessions, survival, bosses, progression, competition, and shareable community activity. Readers looking for the fast game experience should use the Arena paper.

### ZAGA Districts

ZAGA Districts is the persistent Telegram-native cyberpunk MMORPG where communities develop districts, organize participants, manage game systems, and compete over time.

The two products should retain separate gameplay, progression, and community descriptions. ZAGA provides the shared brand and wider game-ecosystem route rather than merging them into one mode.

## 4. Market and Liquidity Operations

QTB and AIMM serve different professional workflows.

| Product | Main purpose | User decision supported |
|---|---|---|
| QTB | Organize market information, narratives, watchlists, and research | How to interpret and review available market context |
| AIMM | Monitor and report on liquidity-operations information | How an authorized operator reviews operational conditions and records |

QTB is a research and interpretation surface. AIMM is an operations-support surface. Their reports and AI assistance require source, permission, review, and product-specific market controls.

---

## 5. Events, Discovery, and Work Assistance

### AIE

AIE supports event discovery, planning, briefings, participant or partner context, recaps, and reporting. Its intended users include organizers, communities, sponsors, and teams coordinating event activity.

### ToolGrid AI

ToolGrid AI helps users find and compare AI utilities. Tool owners or sponsors can use approved, clearly labeled visibility services, while users receive context about listings and sponsorship.

### Botmad

Botmad is a permission-controlled AI desktop work assistant. It can help with research, documents, reports, task preparation, and selected tool or file workflows within an authorized scope. Human owners remain responsible for sensitive actions and final approval.

## 7. Shared Product Experience

Products can use common FUZE services where those services improve delivery:

- accounts, workspaces, roles, and permissions;
- AI task routing and review;
- Platform Credit usage for defined actions;
- supported payment and settlement paths;
- product data and retention controls;
- wallet-aware references for approved features;
- operational histories and reporting.

The [FUZE Platform Overview](../CORE-PLATFORM-PAPERS/01-FUZE_PLATFORM_OVERVIEW_PUBLIC.md) explains the shared experience. [FUZE Core Platform Rails](../CORE-PLATFORM-PAPERS/04-FUZE_CORE_PLATFORM_RAILS_PUBLIC.md) defines the service layer.

Not every product needs every shared capability. Integration follows product evidence and operational readiness.

## 8. Platform Credits in Product Papers

Each product paper should identify credit use through a concrete action. Examples include generating a report, processing an AI task, preparing a training module, or using another defined service.

Actual amounts, packages, subscriptions, grants, expiry, and refund treatment depend on approved product policies. The [FUZE Platform Credits Usage Examples](../CORE-PLATFORM-PAPERS/06-FUZE_PLATFORM_CREDITS_USAGE_EXAMPLES_PUBLIC.md) shows common user and workspace patterns.

FUZE token utility is covered separately in [FUZE Product to Token Utility Bridge](19-FUZE_PRODUCT_TO_TOKEN_UTILITY_BRIDGE_PUBLIC.md) and the tokenomics collection.

## 9. Product Data and Reporting

A detailed product paper should explain:

- information the workflow receives;
- roles allowed to access or change it;
- AI context and provider boundaries where relevant;
- outputs, records, and review steps;
- product reporting available to users or operators;
- public-safe evidence appropriate to the product.

Personal identity, customer content, private partner information, credentials, and sensitive operational records stay within permissioned processes. Public wallet references do not make those records public.

Platform-wide handling appears in [FUZE Data Privacy and AI Data Handling](../CORE-PLATFORM-PAPERS/07-FUZE_DATA_PRIVACY_AND_AI_DATA_HANDLING_PUBLIC.md).

## 11. Status and Selection Boundary

An index entry confirms the approved product name and public role. It does not establish live availability, access, pricing, customer adoption, revenue, or a release date.

Readers should consult the [FUZE Public Status and Roadmap Matrix](../PUBLIC-INDEX/02-FUZE_PUBLIC_STATUS_AND_ROADMAP_MATRIX.md) and the product's current release information when assessing operational status.

AI assistance, game activity, market interpretation, liquidity-operations support, event intelligence, sponsored visibility, and work automation depend on their own inputs and controls.
