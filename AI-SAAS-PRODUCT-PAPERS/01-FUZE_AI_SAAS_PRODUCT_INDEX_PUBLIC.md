# FUZE AI SaaS Product Index

## Executive Summary

The FUZE AI SaaS Product Index introduces the product layer of FUZE.

FUZE is a product-first AI SaaS and Web3 ecosystem. The platform starts with practical products, connects them through shared platform rails, and supports broader ecosystem participation through FUZE token, Platform Credits, wallet-based records, reporting, and public documentation.

This index helps readers understand what FUZE is building before they move into tokenomics, investor materials, wallet-based records, market-access topics, or whitepaper papers.

The FUZE product layer includes:

- HerHelp as the practical AI SaaS suite
- SheetLayer AI as the spreadsheet and business data layer
- ShopOS AI as the AI-powered shop operating system
- SpeakShop AI as the voice script and speaker announcement layer
- TrainLayer AI as the learning and training layer
- CommunityLayer AI as the community operations layer
- ZAGA as the game and token-utility ecosystem
- ZAGA Arena as the fast battle arena game
- ZAGA Districts as the Telegram-native cyberpunk MMORPG
- QTB as AI trading intelligence and market interpretation support
- AIMM as AI market-making and liquidity operations support
- AIE as event intelligence
- ToolGrid AI as the AI utility network with sponsored visibility
- Botmad as the AI Desktop Employee under permission controls

Each product has its own role.

Some products serve small businesses. Some serve communities. Some serve games. Some serve crypto-native workflows. Some serve events, utility discovery, or AI work assistance.

Together, they make FUZE more than one app or one token. They create a product ecosystem that can connect through shared rails for identity, Platform Credits, payments, AI orchestration, wallet records, reporting, privacy, and controls.

The product layer makes FUZE practical.

## 5. Core Features

### 5.1 HerHelp

HerHelp is the practical AI SaaS suite under FUZE.

It is the umbrella for products that help real users use AI in business, shop, spreadsheet, training, voice, and community workflows.

HerHelp products are designed to make AI practical, not abstract.

### 5.2 SheetLayer AI

SheetLayer AI is the spreadsheet and business data layer.

It supports workflows such as:

- mapping sheet columns
- explaining spreadsheet structure
- creating dashboards
- generating formulas
- turning sheet records into workflow actions
- syncing supported sheet data
- preparing report drafts
- organizing business data

### 5.3 ShopOS AI

ShopOS AI is the AI-powered shop operating system.

It supports practical shop workflows such as:

- QR menu
- checkout
- queue
- payment
- loyalty
- stock
- staff
- delivery
- reports
- TrustCheck
- device support
- Google Sheets sync
- AI assistance

ShopOS AI is designed for small shops that need simple operations, not complicated enterprise systems.

### 5.4 SpeakShop AI

SpeakShop AI supports promotional voice scripts, sound packs, and speaker announcements.

It can help shops, booths, events, malls, markets, and promoters create voice content for real customer-facing situations.

### 5.5 TrainLayer AI

TrainLayer AI supports AI learning materials, training workflows, quizzes, guides, and staff/customer education.

It can help teams turn knowledge into training materials that are easier to reuse and improve.

### 5.6 CommunityLayer AI

CommunityLayer AI supports moderation, safety, verification, support, reporting, summaries, and community operations.

It can help Telegram groups, Web3 communities, creator communities, support groups, and business communities operate with more structure.

### 5.7 ZAGA

ZAGA is the FUZE game and token-utility ecosystem.

It gives FUZE a game and community surface connected to the broader platform.

### 5.8 ZAGA Arena

ZAGA Arena is the fast battle arena game where players survive, fight bosses, collect USDT and Token Value, build Net Worth, and compete in Telegram-ready multiplayer arenas.

It is designed for quick, repeatable sessions, leaderboards, badges, share cards, and community competition.

### 5.9 ZAGA Districts

ZAGA Districts is a Telegram-native cyberpunk MMORPG where real communities build cities, manage economies, hire NPCs, and compete for power.

ZAGA Districts is separate from ZAGA Arena.

It should be explained as a community MMORPG and city-building ecosystem, not as a mode inside ZAGA Arena.

### 5.10 QTB

QTB is AI trading intelligence and market interpretation support.

It can support market notes, watchlists, public news summaries, research workflows, narrative comparison, and market context reports.

### 5.11 AIMM

AIMM is AI market-making and liquidity operations support.

It can support liquidity monitoring, operational summaries, market activity reports, exchange or pool notes, and public-safe reporting drafts where appropriate.

### 5.12 AIE

AIE supports event intelligence.

It can help users discover event opportunities, summarize event data, prepare event plans, produce event reports, and create partner or audience briefings.

### 5.13 ToolGrid AI

ToolGrid AI is the AI utility network with sponsored visibility.

It can help users discover tools, compare utilities, save lists, review descriptions, and understand sponsored visibility with clearer context.

### 5.14 Botmad

Botmad is the AI Desktop Employee under permission controls.

It can support research, documentation, artifact creation, reports, task summaries, workflow assistance, and supervised AI work.

## 6. Practical Workflows and Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Small Shop Operations

A shop uses ShopOS AI for daily operations and SpeakShop AI for promotional announcements.

The shop can manage orders, queue, stock, delivery, loyalty, and daily reports.

SpeakShop AI can help create announcement scripts such as lunch promotions, weekend campaigns, queue messages, or booth greetings.

SheetLayer AI can help sync or organize spreadsheet-based records.

### Use Case 2: Spreadsheet-Based Business Team

A business team uses SheetLayer AI to understand spreadsheet data.

The team can map columns, create dashboards, summarize records, generate formulas, and turn data into workflow actions.

This helps teams that already run important work in spreadsheets.

### Use Case 3: Community Operations

A Telegram or Web3 community uses CommunityLayer AI.

The community can summarize discussions, support moderation, organize verification, prepare safety reports, and create weekly updates.

The product helps the community operate with more structure.

### Use Case 4: Training and Learning

A team uses TrainLayer AI to turn documents, product instructions, or internal knowledge into training guides, quizzes, onboarding materials, and learning workflows.

This helps teams reuse knowledge instead of repeating the same explanation manually.

### Use Case 5: ZAGA Game Community

A community follows ZAGA Arena for fast gameplay and ZAGA Districts for city-building and group competition.

The two products serve different experiences under the ZAGA brand.

ZAGA gives FUZE an entertainment and community layer that can connect to platform rails where supported.

### Use Case 6: Crypto Market Context

A crypto-native reader uses QTB to organize public market information, research notes, watchlists, and market context summaries.

A team uses AIMM to support liquidity operations monitoring and reporting workflows.

These products help crypto users and operators work with clearer information.

### Use Case 7: Events and AI Utility Discovery

A user uses AIE to plan an event and ToolGrid AI to discover useful AI tools.

AIE helps with event opportunities and summaries.

ToolGrid AI helps with utility discovery, sponsored visibility, and tool comparison.

### Use Case 8: AI Work Assistance

A team uses Botmad as a permission-controlled AI Desktop Employee.

Botmad can help prepare documents, reports, research notes, artifacts, summaries, and task outputs while keeping human review and permission control in the workflow.

## 9. Data, AI, Privacy, and Permission Controls

FUZE products can involve different types of data.

Examples include:

- shop data
- spreadsheet data
- training content
- community messages and summaries
- game activity
- market notes
- event information
- utility listings
- Botmad work outputs
- product usage records
- wallet-based records where useful

FUZE’s public privacy direction is simple:

Use data to support product workflows, AI assistance, reporting, and public-safe transparency while keeping sensitive identity and private records permissioned.

AI supports product workflows such as generation, summaries, analysis, routing, reporting, and decision-support outputs.

Products should use permission controls where roles, sensitive data, customer records, community records, investor records, or AI output review matter.
