# ZAGA Districts

## Executive Summary

ZAGA Districts is the persistent cyberpunk MMORPG and community city-building product in the ZAGA family.

The community is the primary long-term player.

Members may organize around a district, city, faction, gang, alliance, or another supported identity and coordinate:

- membership;
- game roles;
- quests;
- NPCs;
- structures;
- simulated resources;
- district treasury decisions;
- production;
- defense;
- conflict;
- diplomacy;
- world events;
- reputation;
- seasonal campaigns;
- Telegram-ready communication; and
- long-term progression.

The product is designed around a persistent loop:

```text
form the district -> assign roles -> set priorities
-> contribute -> resolve outcomes -> review the state
-> adapt -> continue the world
```

Districts may use terms such as:

- treasury;
- salary;
- tax;
- market;
- resource;
- property;
- production;
- district wealth;
- city power;
- ownership;
- income;
- reward; and
- yield-like game language.

In the ordinary Districts context, these are gameplay, simulation, scoring, progression, governance, or narrative terms.

They do not automatically represent:

- real money;
- real USDT;
- a bank balance;
- legal ownership;
- employment;
- governmental tax;
- compensation;
- a security;
- token allocation;
- a financial claim;
- a payout;
- an investment return;
- exchange access;
- liquidity; or
- future financial value.

Any wallet-aware, FUZE-token, claim, payment, or externally redeemable mechanism must be separately defined, authorized, implemented, tested, activated, and disclosed.

ZAGA Districts remains separate from [ZAGA Arena](09-ZAGA_ARENA_PUBLIC.md). Arena runs, scores, badges, Net Worth, game USDT, or Market Roles do not automatically become Districts resources, roles, property, city progress, faction progress, or governance rights.

## Product Purpose

Many online communities organize around discussion, trading chat, announcements, or short-lived campaigns but have limited shared activity between major events.

ZAGA Districts gives a community a persistent world to build and manage together.

The product is intended to support:

- a persistent district or city identity;
- member roles and permissions;
- shared objectives;
- long-term progression;
- NPC hiring and assignment;
- structures and upgrades;
- simulated treasury and resource decisions;
- production and maintenance;
- alliances and rivalries;
- defense and conflict;
- quests and world events;
- community governance;
- Telegram-ready coordination;
- seasonal campaigns;
- public-safe city records; and
- long-term reporting.

The game should create meaningful choices at both individual and group levels while keeping the operating rules understandable.

The product should remain enjoyable and understandable without requiring wallet or token participation.
