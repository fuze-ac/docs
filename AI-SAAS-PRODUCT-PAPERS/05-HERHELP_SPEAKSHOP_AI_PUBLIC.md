# HerHelp SpeakShop AI

## Executive Summary

SpeakShop AI is HerHelp’s AI-powered front-shop voice promoter for small businesses.

SpeakShop AI turns a phone, tablet, or connected speaker into an AI front-shop voice promoter for small businesses. It helps shops create approved voice messages from product information, pricing, promotions, opening hours, stock context, and daily campaigns, then play those messages through a phone speaker, Bluetooth speaker, external speaker, or shop audio setup.

Small businesses often depend on staff to repeat the same message throughout the day: greeting customers, explaining today’s product, announcing promotions, calling attention to special items, or telling customers when an order is ready. Printed signs are passive. Staff get tired. Owners cannot always stand at the front. Existing motion-speaker devices often play fixed recorded audio without understanding products, stock, time of day, or current offers.

SpeakShop AI solves this by making front-shop voice promotion easier, cheaper, more flexible, and more connected to real shop data.

Inside HerHelp, SpeakShop AI can connect naturally with ShopOS AI for menu items, queue announcements, stock-aware messages, loyalty campaigns, product promotions, and owner reports. Inside FUZE, SpeakShop AI connects to shared platform rails for identity, Platform Credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, privacy, and controlled workflows.

## 6. Risk Boundaries and Safeguards

SpeakShop AI uses risk boundaries around message accuracy, voice output, local rules, customer experience, privacy, device setup, AI usage, and public communication.

### 6.1 General SpeakShop AI Boundary

SpeakShop AI supports AI-generated voice promotion for shops. Shop owners remain responsible for message approval, local noise rules, product accuracy, customer experience, and device placement.

### 6.2 Message Accuracy Boundary

Voice messages can describe products, prices, promotions, queue status, and shop information. Shop owners and approved staff remain responsible for reviewing messages before playback and updating inaccurate, expired, or unavailable offers.

### 6.3 Local Noise and Space Boundary

Physical locations can have rules about sound volume, promotion, public announcements, quiet hours, shared spaces, mall rules, market rules, neighborhood rules, and customer experience. Shop owners remain responsible for operating within those rules.

### 6.4 Device Boundary

SpeakShop AI can support phones, tablets, browsers, Bluetooth speakers, AUX speakers, USB-C speakers, and amplifiers where applicable. Device performance depends on hardware, network, browser, operating system, microphone/camera permission where used, battery, mounting, speaker quality, and local setup.

### 6.5 AI Output Boundary

AI assistance supports promotional text, voice generation, message variation, campaign sound packs, and reporting. Human review remains important for product claims, pricing, health claims, service promises, customer messages, and public playback.

### 6.6 Customer Privacy Boundary

SpeakShop AI can connect with customer context only where product rules, privacy terms, and approval settings allow. Customer-specific messages require careful handling because voice output can be heard in public spaces. Product design favors limited, safe, and role-controlled customer context.
