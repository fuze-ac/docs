# FUZE Approved Distributable Value Policy

## Executive Summary

FUZE Approved Distributable Value Policy defines the private policy structure for determining whether any reconciled FUZE product revenue can become approved distributable value for wallet-based participation.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE.

FUZE token supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token.

Wallet-based participation ability is not automatic for every wallet.

Wallet-based participation ability is not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

Approved distributable value is the controlled value category that may be considered for wallet-based participation only if the wallet-based participation framework becomes active and all required gates are ready.

Approved distributable value is separate from product revenue, gross revenue, net revenue, stablecoin balances, Platform Credit purchases, treasury balances, vault balances, token allocation categories, seed-round funds, investor funds, token sale proceeds, reserves, partner pass-through funds, and non-revenue transfers.

Eligible FUZE-holding wallets may participate in approved distributable value from defined FUZE product revenue pools only if the wallet-based participation framework becomes active and all required gates are complete.

Required gates may include legal, accounting, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, privacy, eligibility, operator, jurisdiction, product revenue pool, and approved distributable value gates.

Approved distributable value may be zero.

Approved distributable value may vary by period.

Approved distributable value may be withheld, reserved, delayed, restricted, corrected, restated, or not activated.

Product revenue does not automatically become approved distributable value.

Gross revenue does not automatically become approved distributable value.

Stablecoin inflows do not automatically become approved distributable value.

Platform Credit purchases do not automatically become approved distributable value.

Treasury balances do not automatically become approved distributable value.

Seed-round funds do not become approved distributable value.

Investor funds do not become approved distributable value.

Token sale proceeds, if any, do not become approved distributable value.

FUZE uses wallet-level transparency by default, but FUZE does not publish personal identity publicly.

Public records may show wallet addresses, vault activity, report hashes, audit records, snapshot records, eligibility status, and claim status where applicable.

Private or legal verification, if required, stays off-chain, permissioned, and separated from public blockchain records.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

FUZE’s public market access direction is DEX first. CEX expansion may come later when product evidence, legal review, exchange review, liquidity readiness, custody treatment, operational readiness, market conditions, and strategic timing support that path. CEX expansion is not guaranteed.

FUZE does not guarantee approved distributable value, payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, AI accuracy, user acquisition, community growth, or investment return.

## 3. Master Model

The master approved distributable value statement is:

Approved distributable value is the final reviewed value category that may be considered for wallet-based participation only after defined FUZE product revenue is identified, confirmed, classified, reconciled, adjusted, reserved where needed, approved by required gates, assigned to an approved product revenue pool, and connected to an active wallet-based participation framework.

The approved distributable value model has ten main parts.

| Model Part | Private Meaning |
|---|---|
| Defined Product Revenue Pool | A product revenue category approved for possible inclusion. |
| Confirmed Product Revenue | Revenue confirmed as settled and connected to an included product source. |
| Exclusion Review | Removal of seed-round funds, token sale proceeds, treasury transfers, non-revenue transfers, refunds, disputes, chargebacks, and excluded sources. |
| Cost Review | Review of product costs, payment fees, partner shares, infrastructure costs, service costs, and operating costs where applicable. |
| Reserve Treatment | Application of tax, legal, audit, refund, chargeback, treasury, security, dispute, and operating reserves where needed. |
| Accounting Review | Classification of recognized revenue, deferred revenue, liabilities, credits, expenses, and reserves. |
| Legal and Jurisdiction Review | Confirmation that value can be included under relevant legal and jurisdiction boundaries. |
| Audit or Verification Review | Evidence review, attestation, audit, or verification where required. |
| Reporting Preparation | Preparation of private records, public-safe summaries, report hashes, and correction process. |
| Approved Value Decision | Final approval, delay, restriction, correction, or rejection of possible approved distributable value. |

The model does not mean:

- all product revenue is distributable
- all gross revenue is distributable
- all net revenue is distributable
- all stablecoin inflows are distributable
- all Platform Credit purchases are distributable
- all treasury balances are distributable
- all seed-round funds are distributable
- all investor funds are distributable
- all token-related proceeds are distributable
- all vault balances are distributable
- all eligible wallets receive payout
- approved distributable value is guaranteed
- wallet-based participation is active by default

The model is conservative, product-first, accounting-aware, treasury-aware, privacy-aware, and compliance-aware.

## 5. Revenue Reconciliation Model

Approved distributable value begins with revenue reconciliation.

Revenue reconciliation determines whether an inflow can be classified, confirmed, adjusted, reserved, reviewed, and approved.

### 5.1 Revenue-to-Approved Value Flow

| Step | Name | Purpose | Result |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inflow Capture | Capture payment, wallet transfer, invoice, subscription, credit purchase, service fee, report purchase, event fee, or partner settlement. | Raw inflow record |
| 2 | Source Identification | Identify the source product, service, report, package, event, partner, treasury, or non-revenue source. | Source record |
| 3 | Product Revenue Classification | Determine whether the inflow is product revenue or another category. | Revenue classification |
| 4 | Excluded Source Review | Exclude seed-round funds, investor funds, token sale proceeds, treasury transfers, reserve transfers, and non-revenue movements. | Exclusion record |
| 5 | Payment Confirmation | Confirm payment settlement and remove failed, pending, reversed, disputed, or unconfirmed payments. | Confirmed revenue record |
| 6 | Platform Credit Review | Classify Platform Credit purchase, usage, bonus credit, refund, expiration, liability, and recognition status. | Credit treatment record |
| 7 | Stablecoin Classification | Classify stablecoin payment, settlement, treasury transfer, compensation, reserve, or non-revenue movement. | Stablecoin treatment record |
| 8 | Refund and Chargeback Review | Remove or reserve refunded, reversed, disputed, or chargeback-risk value. | Adjustment record |
| 9 | Cost and Reserve Review | Apply payment fees, partner shares, infrastructure costs, service costs, operating costs, taxes, and required reserves. | Cost and reserve record |
| 10 | Accounting Review | Review recognition, deferred revenue, liabilities, reserves, expenses, and period classification. | Accounting approval or block |
| 11 | Legal and Jurisdiction Review | Confirm that value can be considered under legal and jurisdiction boundaries. | Legal approval or block |
| 12 | Audit or Verification Review | Apply evidence review, audit, attestation, or verification where required. | Verification approval or block |
| 13 | Product Revenue Pool Assignment | Assign value to a defined approved product revenue pool if eligible. | Pool assignment |
| 14 | Approved Distributable Value Calculation | Calculate final approved value after exclusions, costs, reserves, and approvals. | Approved value record |
| 15 | Reporting Preparation | Prepare private reports, public-safe summaries, report hashes, corrections, and restatements. | Reporting package |
| 16 | Activation Check | Confirm whether the wallet-based participation framework and all required gates are active. | Activation approval or block |

### 5.2 Required Separation

Revenue reconciliation must separate:

- product revenue from gross inflow
- product revenue from investor funds
- product revenue from seed-round funds
- product revenue from token sale proceeds
- product revenue from treasury transfers
- product revenue from reserve transfers
- product revenue from non-revenue transfers
- product revenue from stablecoin settlement movement
- product revenue from Platform Credit liability
- product revenue from partner pass-through funds
- product revenue from refundable deposits
- product revenue from deferred revenue
- approved distributable value from product revenue
- approved distributable value from treasury balances
- approved distributable value from vault balances
- approved distributable value from token allocation balances

This separation protects the FUZE model from premature or unsafe payout expectations.

### 6.3 Gross-to-Approved Value Calculation

A conservative calculation direction is:

Confirmed product revenue from approved product revenue pools  
minus refunds, reversals, chargebacks, disputes, and fraud-risk value  
minus taxes, processing fees, partner shares, infrastructure costs, service costs, operating costs, and required reserves where applicable  
minus excluded or restricted revenue  
after legal, accounting, tax, treasury, audit or verification where required, reporting, operator, and jurisdiction review  
equals possible approved distributable value if approved.

This formula direction is not a public payout promise.

This formula direction is not a guarantee that any value will be approved.

This formula direction may produce zero approved distributable value.
