# FUZE Wallet Eligibility Model

## Executive Summary

FUZE Wallet Eligibility Model defines the private eligibility structure for wallet-based participation under the FUZE one-token model.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE.

FUZE token supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token.

Wallet-based participation ability is not automatic for every wallet.

Wallet-based participation ability is not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

This paper defines how FUZE can evaluate whether a FUZE-holding wallet may become an eligible FUZE-holding wallet if the wallet-based participation framework becomes active and all required gates are ready.

Eligibility is a private ruleset and review process.

Eligibility does not guarantee payout, claim rights, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, resale ability, exit, business revenue, game earnings, or investment return.

Eligible FUZE-holding wallets may participate in approved distributable value from defined FUZE product revenue pools only if the participation framework becomes active and all required gates are complete.

Required gates may include legal, accounting, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, privacy, eligibility, operator, jurisdiction, product revenue pool, and approved distributable value gates.

Approved distributable value is separate from gross revenue, token sale proceeds, seed-round funds, treasury balances, vault balances, stablecoin balances, Platform Credits, and token allocation categories.

FUZE uses wallet-level transparency by default, but FUZE does not publish personal identity publicly.

Public records may show wallet addresses, vault activity, report hashes, audit records, snapshot records, eligibility status, and claim status where applicable.

Private or legal verification, if required, stays off-chain, permissioned, and separated from public blockchain records.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

FUZE’s public market access direction is DEX first. CEX expansion may come later when product evidence, legal review, exchange review, liquidity readiness, custody treatment, operational readiness, market conditions, and strategic timing support that path. CEX expansion is not guaranteed.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, AI accuracy, user acquisition, community growth, or investment return.

## 5. Revenue Reconciliation Model

Wallet eligibility is separate from revenue reconciliation.

A wallet can be eligible while approved distributable value is zero.

A wallet can satisfy eligibility criteria while no participation distribution is active.

A wallet can appear in a snapshot while approved distributable value is not available.

Revenue reconciliation determines whether any value can become approved distributable value.

Revenue reconciliation may include:

| Step | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Product Revenue Identification | Identify revenue source by product, service, report, credit package, subscription, campaign, event, or partner arrangement. |
| Payment Confirmation | Confirm payment status and settlement status. |
| Stablecoin Classification | Classify stablecoin inflow where applicable. |
| Platform Credit Classification | Separate Platform Credit purchase and Platform Credit usage. |
| Refund Review | Exclude or reserve refunded value. |
| Chargeback Review | Exclude or reserve chargeback-risk value. |
| Dispute Review | Exclude or reserve disputed value. |
| Cost Review | Review product, infrastructure, partner, processing, and service costs. |
| Reserve Treatment | Apply operating, tax, legal, security, refund, treasury, or risk reserves where needed. |
| Accounting Review | Classify revenue, liabilities, credits, deferred revenue, expenses, and reserves where applicable. |
| Legal Review | Review whether value can be included in the relevant model. |
| Tax Review | Review tax treatment where needed. |
| Audit or Verification | Apply review, verification, or audit process where required. |
| Jurisdiction Review | Exclude or restrict value where required by jurisdiction. |
| Approved Pool Assignment | Assign value only to a defined product revenue pool if approved. |
| Approved Distributable Value Calculation | Calculate final approved value after required adjustments and approvals. |
| Reporting Preparation | Prepare public-safe reporting and report hashes where applicable. |

Eligibility does not replace revenue reconciliation.

Revenue reconciliation does not replace eligibility.

Both must be ready before any approved participation distribution can occur.
