# FUZE Wallet-Based Privacy and Eligibility Model

## Executive Summary

FUZE Wallet-Based Privacy and Eligibility Model defines the private structure for separating public wallet-level transparency from private identity, verification, eligibility, jurisdiction, custody, compliance, and claim-readiness records under the FUZE wallet-based participation framework.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE.

FUZE token supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token.

Wallet-based participation ability is not automatic for every wallet.

Wallet-based participation ability is not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

This model protects the difference between a public wallet record and a private person, company, investor, contributor, customer, or agreement.

FUZE uses wallet-level transparency by default, but FUZE does not publish personal identity publicly.

Public records may show wallet addresses, vault activity, report hashes, audit records, snapshot records, eligibility status, claim status, transaction hashes, approval status, deployment status, and timelock status where applicable.

Private identity verification records, legal records, tax records, accounting workpapers, treasury procedures, security controls, private agreements, private revenue data, customer records, investor identities, contributor identities, and sensitive operational records stay off-chain, permissioned, access-controlled, and separated from public blockchain records where required.

A FUZE-holding wallet may become an eligible FUZE-holding wallet only if it satisfies the active eligibility rules, the wallet-based participation framework is active, and all required gates are ready.

Eligible FUZE-holding wallets may participate in approved distributable value from defined FUZE product revenue pools only if the wallet-based participation framework becomes active and all required gates are complete.

Required gates may include legal, accounting, tax, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, privacy, eligibility, operator, jurisdiction, product revenue pool, approved distributable value, market access, custody, AI review, community audit, and public language gates.

Approved distributable value is separate from product revenue, gross revenue, net revenue, stablecoin balances, Platform Credit purchases, treasury balances, vault balances, token allocation categories, seed-round funds, investor funds, token sale proceeds, reserves, partner pass-through funds, and non-revenue transfers.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

FUZE’s public market access direction is DEX first.

CEX expansion may come later when product evidence, legal review, exchange review, liquidity readiness, custody treatment, operational readiness, market conditions, and strategic timing support that path.

CEX expansion is not guaranteed.

FUZE does not guarantee approved distributable value, product revenue, payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, AI accuracy, user acquisition, community growth, acquisition, or investment return.

## 3. Master Model

The master privacy and eligibility statement is:

FUZE uses wallet-level transparency for public records while keeping personal identity, private verification, investor records, contributor records, customer records, agreements, legal records, tax records, accounting workpapers, treasury procedures, security controls, and sensitive operational records off-chain, permissioned, access-controlled, and separated from public blockchain records where required.

The privacy and eligibility model has twelve main parts.

| Model Area | Private Meaning |
|---|---|
| Public Wallet Record | A wallet address or wallet-level status that may appear in public records. |
| Private Identity Record | A person, company, investor, contributor, customer, or legal identity record that stays private where required. |
| Off-Chain Verification | Permissioned review process for identity, jurisdiction, eligibility, or legal requirements where needed. |
| Eligible FUZE-Holding Wallet | A wallet that satisfies active eligibility rules if the framework is active and all gates are ready. |

| Custody Treatment | Separate handling for self-custody and exchange custody where required. |
| Privacy Review | Review to prevent public exposure of personal, business, legal, tax, accounting, treasury, and verification data. |
| Public Language Boundary | No public wording that makes wallet visibility look like identity exposure or guaranteed eligibility. |

## 4. Wallet-Based Eligibility Model

Wallet eligibility defines whether a FUZE-holding wallet may qualify under active rules if the wallet-based participation framework becomes active.

Eligibility is not automatic.

### 4.1 Eligibility Status Terms

FUZE may use the following private or public-safe eligibility status terms where applicable.

| Status | Meaning | Public-Safe Use |
|---|---|---|
| Not Reviewed | Wallet has not been reviewed. | Yes, if status publication is approved. |
| In Review | Wallet is under eligibility review. | Yes, if privacy-safe. |
| Eligible | Wallet meets active eligibility criteria. | Yes, if publication is approved. |
| Ineligible | Wallet does not meet active eligibility criteria. | Yes, if publication is approved. |
| Restricted | Wallet is restricted due to jurisdiction, legal, compliance, custody, abuse, fraud, Sybil, bot, manipulation, or operator review. | Yes, if privacy-safe. |
| Pending Verification | Wallet requires off-chain verification where required. | Yes, if privacy-safe. |
| Snapshot Included | Wallet appears in a relevant snapshot. | Yes, if snapshot publication is approved. |
| Snapshot Excluded | Wallet does not appear in a relevant snapshot. | Yes, if snapshot publication is approved. |

| Claim Available | Wallet may claim under active rules if the claim module is active. | Yes, if all gates are ready. |
| Claim Completed | Wallet completed claim under active rules. | Yes, if privacy-safe. |

Status terms should not expose personal identity.
