# FUZE Revenue Reconciliation Model

## Executive Summary

FUZE Revenue Reconciliation Model defines the private structure for classifying, confirming, reconciling, reviewing, reserving, approving, reporting, and separating FUZE product revenue before any value can be considered for wallet-based participation.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE.

FUZE token supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token.

Wallet-based participation ability is not automatic for every wallet.

Wallet-based participation ability is not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

Product revenue does not automatically become approved distributable value.

Gross revenue does not automatically become approved distributable value.

Stablecoin inflows do not automatically become approved distributable value.

Platform Credit purchases do not automatically become approved distributable value.

Treasury balances do not automatically become approved distributable value.

Seed-round funds do not become product revenue or approved distributable value.

Token sale proceeds, if any, do not become product revenue or approved distributable value.

Revenue reconciliation is the private control process that separates product revenue, non-revenue transfers, Platform Credit activity, stablecoin settlement, treasury transfers, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, costs, reserves, liabilities, taxes, accounting treatment, legal review, audit or verification review, and approved distributable value.

Eligible FUZE-holding wallets may participate in approved distributable value from defined FUZE product revenue pools only if the wallet-based participation framework becomes active and all required gates are complete.

Required gates may include legal, accounting, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, privacy, eligibility, operator, jurisdiction, product revenue pool, and approved distributable value gates.

FUZE uses wallet-level transparency by default, but FUZE does not publish personal identity publicly.

Public records may show wallet addresses, vault activity, report hashes, audit records, snapshot records, eligibility status, and claim status where applicable.

Private or legal verification, if required, stays off-chain, permissioned, and separated from public blockchain records.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

FUZE’s public market access direction is DEX first. CEX expansion may come later when product evidence, legal review, exchange review, liquidity readiness, custody treatment, operational readiness, market conditions, and strategic timing support that path. CEX expansion is not guaranteed.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, AI accuracy, user acquisition, community growth, or investment return.

## 3. Master Model

The master revenue reconciliation statement is:

FUZE product revenue may be considered for approved distributable value only after the revenue is identified, confirmed, classified, reconciled, adjusted, reserved where needed, reviewed by required gates, assigned to an approved product revenue pool, and approved for the active wallet-based participation framework.

The revenue reconciliation model has ten main parts.

| Model Part | Private Meaning |
|---|---|
| Revenue Identification | Identify the product, service, report, subscription, package, credit, partner, event, or platform activity that generated the inflow. |
| Payment Confirmation | Confirm that the payment settled and is not pending, reversed, failed, disputed, or refunded. |
| Classification | Classify the inflow as product revenue, Platform Credit purchase, stablecoin settlement, treasury transfer, investor funds, partner settlement, compensation, reserve transfer, or non-revenue transfer. |
| Reconciliation | Match invoices, receipts, wallet transfers, payment records, credit records, product records, and accounting records. |
| Adjustment | Remove or reserve refunds, chargebacks, disputes, taxes, processing fees, partner shares, costs, liabilities, and required reserves. |
| Review | Apply legal, accounting, tax, treasury, audit, reporting, operator, and jurisdiction review. |
| Product Revenue Pool Assignment | Assign only approved product revenue to defined product revenue pools where applicable. |
| Approved Distributable Value Calculation | Calculate final approved value after all exclusions, costs, reserves, and reviews. |
| Reporting | Prepare private and public-safe reports, report hashes, correction records, and audit references where applicable. |
| Activation Boundary | Approved distributable value can be used only if the wallet-based participation framework is active and all gates are ready. |

The model does not mean:

- all product revenue is distributable
- all gross revenue is distributable
- all net revenue is distributable
- all stablecoin inflows are revenue
- all Platform Credit purchases are distributable
- all treasury balances are distributable
- all seed-round funds are revenue
- all token-related proceeds are revenue
- every eligible wallet receives payout
- approved distributable value is guaranteed
- wallet-based participation is active by default

The model is conservative, product-first, accounting-aware, treasury-aware, privacy-aware, and compliance-aware.

## 5. Revenue Reconciliation Model

The FUZE revenue reconciliation model defines the path from inflow to reviewed product revenue to approved distributable value.

### 5.1 Revenue Reconciliation Flow

| Step | Name | Purpose | Output |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inflow Capture | Capture payment, wallet transfer, invoice, credit purchase, subscription, partner settlement, or product payment. | Raw inflow record |
| 2 | Source Identification | Identify the product, service, report, package, event, partner, or treasury source. | Source classification |
| 3 | Payment Confirmation | Confirm payment settlement and exclude failed, pending, reversed, or unconfirmed payments. | Confirmed payment record |
| 4 | Revenue Classification | Classify the inflow as product revenue or non-revenue. | Revenue classification record |
| 5 | Platform Credit Classification | Separate Platform Credit purchase, usage, bonus credits, refunded credits, expired credits, and credit liabilities. | Credit classification record |
| 6 | Stablecoin Classification | Classify stablecoin payment, settlement, treasury transfer, reserve transfer, compensation, or non-revenue movement. | Stablecoin classification record |
| 7 | Refund and Chargeback Review | Identify refunds, chargebacks, reversals, disputes, fraud, or pending risk. | Adjustment record |
| 8 | Cost and Fee Review | Apply processing fees, infrastructure costs, partner shares, service costs, taxes, and operating reserves where applicable. | Cost and reserve record |
| 9 | Accounting Review | Review revenue recognition, deferred revenue, liabilities, reserves, expenses, and period classification. | Accounting review record |
| 10 | Legal and Jurisdiction Review | Review whether the value can be included under applicable legal and jurisdiction boundaries. | Legal and jurisdiction record |
| 11 | Audit or Verification Review | Apply audit, attestation, verification, or internal review where required. | Verification record |
| 12 | Product Revenue Pool Assignment | Assign approved value only to eligible product revenue pools. | Product revenue pool record |
| 13 | Approved Distributable Value Calculation | Calculate value after exclusions, costs, reserves, and approvals. | Approved distributable value record |
| 14 | Reporting Preparation | Prepare private reports, public-safe summaries, report hashes, and correction records. | Reporting package |
| 15 | Activation Check | Confirm whether wallet-based participation framework and all gates are active. | Activation approval or block |

## 6. Approved Distributable Value Policy

Approved distributable value is the final reviewed category that may be used for wallet-based participation only if the framework is active and all gates are ready.

Approved distributable value is not guaranteed.

Approved distributable value may be zero.

Approved distributable value may vary by period.

Approved distributable value may be withheld, reserved, delayed, restricted, or not activated.

### 6.1 Approved Distributable Value Formula Direction

A conservative private formula direction may be:

Confirmed product revenue  
minus refunds, reversals, chargebacks, and disputes  
minus taxes, processing fees, partner shares, service costs, infrastructure costs, operating costs, and required reserves where applicable  
minus excluded or restricted revenue  
after legal, accounting, tax, treasury, audit, reporting, operator, and jurisdiction review  
equals possible approved distributable value if approved.

This formula direction is not a public payout promise.

This formula direction is not a guarantee that any value will be approved.

### 6.2 Approval Requirements

Approved distributable value may require:

| Approval Area | Review Purpose |
|---|---|
| Product Approval | Confirm revenue source and product category. |
| Payment Approval | Confirm settlement and payment status. |
| Credit Approval | Confirm Platform Credit treatment. |
| Stablecoin Approval | Confirm stablecoin classification. |
| Accounting Approval | Confirm recognition, deferred revenue, costs, reserves, and liabilities. |
| Treasury Approval | Confirm treasury treatment and reserve policy. |
| Legal Approval | Confirm participation and distribution legal boundaries. |
| Tax Approval | Confirm tax treatment where required. |
| Audit or Verification Approval | Confirm records and review where required. |
| Reporting Approval | Confirm public-safe reporting and report hash readiness. |
| Jurisdiction Approval | Confirm supported, restricted, and excluded regions. |
| Operator Approval | Confirm final process, support, and release readiness. |

No single approval is enough.

Approved distributable value requires coordinated review.
