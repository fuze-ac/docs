# FUZE Public Paper Index

## Executive Summary

The FUZE Public Paper Index is the authoritative inventory and navigation layer for FUZE’s 99 approved public papers.

The collection supports different readers at different depths. First-time visitors can begin with concise website summaries. Product users can review practical AI SaaS and game-product papers. Investors, partners, technical reviewers, and Web3 participants can follow dedicated paths into evidence, architecture, tokenomics, governance, market access, and risk.

FUZE is presented throughout this system as a product-first AI SaaS and Web3 ecosystem. Its products may connect through shared platform rails for identity, Platform Credits, payments, AI orchestration, permissions, wallet-based records, and reporting where those capabilities are genuinely required.

Publication of a paper confirms that the document is available for public review. It does not by itself prove that a product, contract, token mechanism, market-access route, commercial activity, or participation framework has been implemented, activated, released, adopted, or made live.

## Purpose of This Index

This index provides:

- the approved public-paper inventory;
- the exact directory and filename structure;
- recommended starting points for different readers;
- navigation between specialist paper groups;
- a consistent sequence for controlled paper creation and review; and
- clear routing to the papers that control status, terminology, evidence, and public boundaries.

The index is a navigation authority. It does not replace the specialist paper that controls a specific product, policy, number, status, mechanism, or risk.

## How to Interpret the Collection

The public-paper system separates several kinds of information that should not be treated as interchangeable:

- A **product paper** explains a user problem, product model, intended workflow, and relevant boundaries.
- A **status paper** explains whether a capability is defined, designed, prototyped, tested, released, live, paused, under review, or activation-gated.
- An **evidence paper** explains what support exists for stronger implementation, usage, commercial, or operational claims.
- A **tokenomics paper** controls the relevant token, allocation, participation, vault, circulation, or utility topic.
- A **website summary** provides an accessible introduction but does not override specialist sources.
- A **whitepaper** synthesizes the wider system without changing the facts controlled by narrower papers.
- A **risk paper** provides deeper treatment of limitations, dependencies, and uncertainty.

Where two papers discuss the same topic, use the narrower controlling specialist paper for the detailed fact, definition, number, status, or boundary.

## Status and Evidence Control

Readers should use the [FUZE Public Status and Roadmap Matrix](./02-FUZE_PUBLIC_STATUS_AND_ROADMAP_MATRIX.md) before interpreting words such as current, active, available, implemented, tested, released, live, deployed, generating revenue, listed, or activated.

FUZE distinguishes among public position, concept, research, design, prototype, internal testing, limited release, public beta, live operation, paused or under-review status, activation-gated preparation, and retired or replaced status.

A status applies only to the specific product, feature, module, contract, venue, or mechanism identified by the supporting record.

A product may be released while a token-connected feature remains activation-gated. A contract may be deployed while the mechanism it supports remains inactive. A paper may be public while the product it describes remains at design stage.

The [FUZE Product Status and Evidence Matrix](../INVESTOR-PARTNER-PAPERS/14-FUZE_PRODUCT_STATUS_AND_EVIDENCE_MATRIX_PUBLIC.md) provides the deeper framework for evaluating product definition, implementation, testing, release, usage, paid delivery, and revenue evidence.

## Public Information Boundary

All papers in this index are intended for public review.

The public collection may contain approved public information and sanitized public-safe summaries. It does not provide unrestricted access to confidential or restricted records such as private investor or customer identities, wallet-to-person mappings, negotiated transaction terms, protected financial or legal records, private exchange or custody discussions, personal identity documents, signer identities, credentials, cryptographic secrets, sensitive treasury procedures, exploitable security information, or restricted infrastructure details.

Where protected evidence is relevant, the public papers may explain the category of evidence or review process without exposing the underlying sensitive record.

Wallet-based reporting can provide useful public evidence without requiring public disclosure of personal identity.

## Maintenance and Corrections

The index should be updated when:

- a link becomes incorrect;
- a controlling paper changes;
- a reader path no longer points to the best current entry paper; or
- the approved documentation sequence changes.

A change to this index does not automatically change the underlying status or authority of another paper. Material changes should also be reflected in the specialist source that controls the affected topic.
