This FUZE Investor FAQ answers common investor, partner, and strategic supporter questions about FUZE’s platform model, product-first execution, revenue direction, token design, Platform Credits, private fundraising structure, product evidence, risk boundaries, and public reporting.

FUZE is a transparency-first AI SaaS platform building practical products on shared infrastructure for identity, credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, and ecosystem participation.

FUZE execution order is:

**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**

FUZE launch focus is:

**HerHelp.com and ZAGA.io, supported by FUZE Core Platform rails and Botmad-assisted execution.**
