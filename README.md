## 30-Second Summary

FUZE is a transparency-first AI SaaS platform building practical products on top of shared infrastructure for identity, credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, and ecosystem participation.

FUZE’s execution order is:

**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**

FUZE launch focus is **HerHelp.com** and **ZAGA.io**, supported by **FUZE Core Platform** rails and accelerated internally by **Botmad**.

FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. **Platform Credits are for product usage.**

Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation.

This index is the public front door for the FUZE master paper library.

## Public Risk Notice

FUZE public papers are educational and informational documents for product users, partners, community members, ecosystem participants, and strategic investors.

FUZE does not guarantee token price, liquidity, listing, profit, return, payout, revenue, user growth, product adoption, enterprise adoption, sponsored performance, game earnings, AI accuracy, acquisition, or market performance.

Platform Credits are product usage credits. Platform Credits are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

FUZE’s Seed Round discussions are private strategic fundraising conversations. Full details, structure, and terms are shared privately with qualified interested parties. This is not a public token sale.

Any Community Participation Round, Public Vault Access, token utility, product launch, product feature, integration, public dashboard, governance mechanism, or future economic framework remains subject to legal, compliance, jurisdiction, product, platform, technical, treasury, and operational readiness where applicable.

## FUZE Token vs Platform Credits

| Item | Public Meaning |
|---|---|
| FUZE token | Ecosystem participation asset. It supports long-term ecosystem alignment, ZAGA utility, wallet-aware access, participation systems, community participation where allowed, and product-linked utility. |
| Platform Credits | Product usage credits. They are used for software actions, AI actions, reports, workflows, sponsored visibility, Botmad artifacts, game-related product actions, and product services where applicable. |
| Stablecoins | Used to pay for work, operations, services, compensation, and practical business transactions where applicable. |
| Tokenomics | Purpose-specific allocation, vault-based control, controlled circulation, product-first utility, and long-term ecosystem alignment. |

Platform Credits are not FUZE tokens. Platform Credits are not investment assets or payout assets.

## Example Platform Credit Usage

Platform Credits can be used for product actions such as:

- mapping spreadsheet columns in SheetLayer AI
- generating a dashboard or business report
- creating a shop report in ShopOS AI
- generating an owner-approved voice promotion in SpeakShop AI
- running a community health report in CommunityLayer AI
- building or validating an assistant in TrainLayer AI
- generating a Botmad artifact
- creating a QTB research report
- creating an AIMM scenario review
- tracking AIE event opportunities
- reviewing a ToolGrid sponsored placement
- using ZAGA utility reports or product actions where applicable
- using ZAGA Arena or ZAGA Districts product actions where applicable

Exact credit pricing, package size, expiration, refunds, product rules, and feature availability are defined in product terms.
