# FUZE Tokenomics One-Page

## Executive Summary

FUZE tokenomics organizes a fixed supply of **500,000,000 FUZE** across ten approved allocation categories.

Allocation defines why tokens are reserved. It does not establish release, circulation, eligibility, ownership by a future recipient, market availability, liquidity, price, or investor return.

FUZE uses one ecosystem token framework while keeping product economics separate. Platform Credits support eligible product consumption. Stablecoins can support approved payment and settlement flows. Product revenue, fundraising proceeds, treasury activity, wallet records, and FUZE token movements retain their own classifications.

This page gives users, community members, contributors, partners, and investors a concise view of the supply structure, allocation purposes, token-state model, and public boundaries. Detailed controls remain in the dedicated tokenomics, governance, compliance, wallet, and market papers.

## What the Allocations Support

### Community Entry and Continuity

**Community Participation Allocation** supports approved community participation, product-user access, contributor recognition, and future community programs under eligibility, jurisdiction, release, and reporting rules.

**BOARD / Surfboard Migration** is a separate allocation for eligible legacy-holder continuity. Unused migration supply does not automatically become treasury or community-round inventory.

### Builders and Contributors

**Team Allocation** and **Advisors / Strategic Contributors** support long-term alignment under approved grant, vesting, service, milestone, cancellation, custody, and release controls.

### Stewardship and Reserves

**Foundation Reserve**, **Treasury Reserve**, and **Transparency / Stability Reserve** support long-horizon stewardship, strategic capacity, operating continuity, governance, infrastructure, and exceptional trust or stability needs.

A reserve is retained capacity. It is not an obligation to spend, distribute, or support market price.

### Holder and Ecosystem Programs

**Holder Incentives** and **Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships** can support approved product-use programs, loyalty, referrals, grants, integrations, distribution, campaigns, and partner milestones.

Each program should define its target behavior, budget, eligibility, duration, abuse controls, evidence, and remaining balance.

### Market Structure

**Liquidity & Market Operations** reserves supply for controlled market-structure preparation or approved operations.

This category does not establish:

- a DEX pool;
- exchange application or approval;
- market-maker commitment;
- liquidity depth or duration;
- trading volume;
- price support;
- investor exit.

## Allocation Is Not Circulation

Tokens can be assigned to a category while remaining reserved, planned, committed, locked, vesting, suspended, or otherwise outside active circulation.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Reserved | Assigned to an allocation and retained under its control |
| Planned | Included in a proposed or approved program budget but not committed |
| Committed | Subject to an approved obligation or agreement |
| Locked or vesting | Restricted by time, milestone, service, or other conditions |
| Released | Moved under an approved instruction |
| Deployed | Used in an approved program, contract, pool, or mechanism |
| Circulating | Classified as available in circulation under the reporting method |
| Returned | Moved back into approved category custody |
| Cancelled | Removed from a proposed or contingent assignment |
| Disputed or suspended | Held pending correction, investigation, or decision |

Movement can depend on:

- approved category purpose;
- source balance and custody mapping;
- recipient or program eligibility;
- release, vesting, or access conditions;
- legal, accounting, compliance, treasury, and technical review;
- multisignature, timelock, or governance approval;
- transaction execution and reconciliation;
- public-safe reporting where appropriate.

A visible wallet balance does not automatically mean the tokens are freely circulating, owned by a future recipient, or available for distribution.

The detailed classification appears in [FUZE Token Release and Circulation Clarity](../TOKENOMICS-GOVERNANCE-COMPLIANCE-PAPERS/13-FUZE_TOKEN_RELEASE_AND_CIRCULATION_CLARITY_PUBLIC.md).

## One Token, Separate Systems

FUZE tokenomics supports a product-first ecosystem rather than replacing ordinary product economics.

| System | Primary purpose |
|---|---|
| FUZE token | Approved ecosystem utility, alignment, governance, and participation functions |
| Platform Credits | Metering and access for eligible product actions, AI tasks, modules, and reports |
| Stablecoins | Approved payment, settlement, treasury, refund, reserve, vendor, or compensation flows |
| Product revenue | Commercial records governed by delivery and accounting treatment |
| Fundraising proceeds | Capital received under the applicable financing process |
| Wallet records | Address-linked evidence for approved token or participation activity |
| Treasury records | Controlled assets, obligations, transfers, reserves, and reconciliation |

A user can purchase or consume a product service without receiving FUZE token.

A Platform Credit balance is not token ownership, equity, revenue sharing, or a promise of future value.

A stablecoin transfer does not by itself establish product revenue, completed delivery, investor rights, or holder entitlement.

A treasury, pool, token, or venue balance does not by itself establish private ownership or availability for distribution.

## Utility, Participation, and Governance

FUZE token can support approved functions connected to products, ZAGA experiences, community programs, partner activity, ecosystem alignment, reporting, and governance.

Each function requires its own definition, readiness, evidence, controls, and status.

Token-related events remain separate:

```text
contract design -> reviewed build -> deployment -> verification
-> initialization -> allocation funding -> transfer activation
-> utility -> distribution -> wallet or custody support
-> market access -> wallet-based participation
```

Completion or preparation of one event does not establish the others.

Wallet-based participation, if activated for a defined scope, requires approved rules for eligibility, value, snapshots, claims, custody, correction, reporting, and jurisdiction treatment.

Governance requires documented authority, proposal and approval processes, technical readiness, execution records, and correction routes.

## Vaults, Release, and Reconciliation

Allocation, custody, approval, execution, and reporting are related but separate responsibilities.

An operational record should identify:

- allocation category;
- approved purpose;
- source wallet, vault, contract, or controlled account;
- opening balance;
- planned, committed, locked, released, deployed, returned, or cancelled amount;
- destination and continuing restrictions;
- approval and transaction references;
- closing balance;
- reporting owner and version;
- exceptions or unresolved reconciliation items.

The basic category reconciliation is:

```text
opening balance - authorized outbound movement
+ authorized returns or corrections = closing balance
```

A movement between wallets within the same category is not automatically a category reclassification. A change in purpose requires formal approval and an updated controlling allocation record.

## Transparency Direction

FUZE can use:

- the controlling allocation table;
- labeled public wallets or vaults where appropriate;
- period opening and closing balances;
- aggregate releases and returns;
- vesting, migration, program, or participation status;
- circulating-supply methodology;
- governance and transaction references;
- report hashes or signatures;
- correction and supersession history.

Public reporting should distinguish allocated, reserved, planned, committed, locked, vested, released, deployed, circulating, returned, cancelled, and suspended amounts.

Wallet-level transparency should not expose the private identity behind an address. Personal information, private agreements, signer details, custody accounts, credentials, and sensitive treasury procedures remain permissioned.

## Public Boundary

Token supply, allocation, utility, and governance describe system design and approved policy.

They do not guarantee:

- product adoption or revenue;
- community or migration eligibility;
- token distribution or wallet participation;
- holder incentives or approved value;
- governance influence;
- liquidity, listing, trading volume, price, resale, or exit;
- financing, acquisition, or investment performance.

FUZE's public market direction is DEX-first, with possible CEX consideration later. This is a sequence for evaluation and communication, not an announcement of a pool, application, approval, listing, liquidity level, market-maker arrangement, or price outcome.

Token, wallet, custody, market, and participation mechanisms can be delayed, changed, narrowed, paused, restricted, or discontinued.

## Key Takeaways

- FUZE has a fixed supply of 500,000,000 tokens across ten approved allocation categories.
- Allocation defines purpose; it does not establish circulation, ownership, eligibility, or market availability.
- Reserved, committed, locked, released, deployed, and circulating are different states.
- Platform Credits, stablecoins, product revenue, fundraising proceeds, treasury records, wallet records, and FUZE token movements remain separate systems and classifications.
