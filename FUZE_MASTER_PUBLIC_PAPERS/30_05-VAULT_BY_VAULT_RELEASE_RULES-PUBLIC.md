# Vault-by-Vault Release Rules

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses vault-by-vault release rules to define how each token allocation behaves before tokens enter active circulation.

The vault-by-vault model supports FUZE’s Controlled Circulation Policy by separating token supply into purpose-specific vaults, each with its own release logic, access rule, control model, lockup direction, reporting requirement, and public risk boundary.

FUZE does not treat every allocation as a simple unlock schedule. Each vault has a different role in the ecosystem:

| Vault Type | Public Role |
|---|---|
| Community Participation Vault | Eligibility-controlled community participation and product-user participation |
| BOARD / Surfboard Migration Vault | Eligible legacy-holder migration continuity |
| Team Vesting Vault | Long-term founder, builder, operator, and team alignment |
| Foundation Reserve Vault | Long-horizon stewardship and mission continuity |
| Treasury Reserve Vault | Strategic platform reserve and controlled treasury flexibility |
| Holder Incentives Vault | Earned holder, product-user, loyalty, referral, and participation programs |
| Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships Vault | Partner, grant, integration, milestone, and ecosystem expansion activity |
| Liquidity Operations Vault | Approved market-structure and liquidity-operations support |
| Advisor / Strategic Contributor Vault | Advisor, operator, expert, and strategic contributor alignment |
| Transparency / Stability Vault | Exceptional trust, transparency, or stability support under governance controls |

The vault-by-vault model helps investors, holders, contributors, users, and strategic partners understand how the fixed **500,000,000 FUZE** supply is governed.

FUZE applies one core principle across all vaults:

**Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation. Platform Credits are for product usage. FUZE token is for ecosystem participation.**

This paper defines the public vault release model, allocation-specific rules, release types, transparency direction, governance controls, and risk safeguards.

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE presents vault-by-vault release rules as the public operating model for token allocation control.

FUZE position:

**Each FUZE vault has one clear purpose, one release logic, one control model, and one public transparency rule.**

The vault-by-vault release model follows FUZE’s tokenomics principle:

**Purpose-specific allocation. Vault-based control. Controlled circulation. Product-first utility. Long-term ecosystem alignment.**

The model exists because FUZE allocations are not interchangeable. Community participation tokens support eligible public and product-user participation. Migration tokens support legacy-holder continuity. Team and advisor tokens support long-term alignment. Treasury and Foundation tokens support stewardship. Holder Incentives support earned participation. Ecosystem Growth supports partner milestones. Liquidity Operations supports market-structure needs. Transparency / Stability Reserve supports exceptional trust and stability functions.

FUZE uses vault separation to reduce ambiguity and improve public reviewability.

## 6. Risk Boundaries and Safeguards

FUZE applies risk boundaries around all vault release rules.

### 6.1 General Token Boundary

FUZE does not guarantee token price, liquidity, listing, profit, return, payout, or market performance.

### 6.2 Platform Credits Boundary

Platform Credits are for product usage and are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

### 6.3 Stablecoin Compensation Boundary

Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation. Token allocations are not presented as short-term cash compensation systems.

### 6.4 Public Vault Access Boundary

Public Vault Access, if implemented, is subject to eligibility, jurisdiction, compliance, platform readiness, market-aligned pricing rules, lockups, and final policy approval.

### 6.5 Community Participation Boundary

Any future Community Participation Round is subject to legal, compliance, jurisdiction, product, platform, and smart contract readiness review.

### 6.6 Listing Boundary

FUZE does not guarantee exchange listing, listing timing, liquidity, price performance, or market outcome.

### 6.7 Liquidity Operations Boundary

Liquidity & Market Operations tokens support market-structure needs under approved policy. This category does not guarantee liquidity, price stability, trading profit, listing performance, or market outcome.

### 6.8 Game Utility Boundary

ZAGA Arena uses market-inspired game resources and scoring language. Public communication separates gameplay progression, leaderboard scoring, and in-game resources from financial return promises.

ZAGA Districts uses digital assets for identity, access, utility, contribution, and reward systems where applicable. It is not positioned as a casino, pure play-to-earn scheme, or guaranteed income product.

### 6.9 Development Standards Boundary

FUZE uses standards-inspired or standards-aligned processes. FUZE does not claim formal certification unless certification has been completed.
