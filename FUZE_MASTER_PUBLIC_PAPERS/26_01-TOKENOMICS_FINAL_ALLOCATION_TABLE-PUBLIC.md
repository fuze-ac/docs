# Tokenomics Final Allocation Table

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses a fixed total supply of **500,000,000 FUZE** and organizes that supply across ten purpose-specific allocation categories.

The allocation model supports FUZE as a transparency-first AI SaaS platform, not a token-first project. FUZE uses a tokenomics structure built around purpose-specific allocation, vault-based control, controlled circulation, product-first utility, and long-term ecosystem alignment. Each allocation category has a defined role, a defined strategic purpose, and a public interpretation that connects tokenomics to product execution, platform infrastructure, community trust, and ecosystem participation.

FUZE separates the FUZE token, Platform Credits, and stablecoin compensation systems.

**FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage. Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation.**

The final allocation table is suitable for public website tokenomics pages, investor materials, public papers, community FAQ, whitepaper sections, transparency dashboards, vault documentation, and future governance materials. It gives readers one clear view of how the full FUZE token supply is structured and why each category exists.

This paper defines the final public allocation model, the strategic grouping model, the public boundary around allocation language, the risk safeguards, and the reporting direction.

### 3.3 Allocation Category Details

#### Community Participation Allocation

The Community Participation Allocation is **110,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **22.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports eligible public/community participation, real users, contributors, long-term supporters, product-aligned participation, and future community access structures where legally and operationally ready.

FUZE uses the public language **Community Participation Allocation** rather than price-first sale language. This keeps the category aligned with product participation and public trust instead of discount-based speculation.

#### BOARD / Surfboard Migration

The BOARD / Surfboard Migration allocation is **25,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **5.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation protects legacy-holder continuity and supports eligible migration from the earlier BOARD / Surfboard context into the FUZE ecosystem.

It is not public sale inventory, not team inventory, not general treasury inventory, and not liquidity inventory. It exists for migration continuity.

#### Team Allocation

The Team Allocation is **45,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **9.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports long-term alignment for founders, core team members, builders, operators, product owners, engineers, and future key hires.

FUZE uses controlled vesting logic for team alignment. The purpose is long-term builder commitment, not immediate discretionary circulation.

#### Foundation Reserve

The Foundation Reserve is **35,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **7.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports long-horizon stewardship, mission continuity, platform-level governance readiness, and reserve-backed trust architecture.

Foundation Reserve tokens are part of FUZE’s long-term stewardship design, not normal sale inventory.

#### Treasury Reserve

The Treasury Reserve is **120,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **24.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation is the largest single strategic reserve. It supports long-term platform runway, product infrastructure, ecosystem development, treasury management, operating flexibility, and controlled strategic use.

The Treasury Reserve is governed as a strategic platform reserve. It is not a scheduled public-release bucket and not a general discretionary sell bucket.

#### Holder Incentives

The Holder Incentives allocation is **55,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **11.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports holder programs, product-user incentives, loyalty, referrals, ecosystem campaigns, participation recognition, and community activation.

FUZE uses Holder Incentives to support ecosystem participation and product-linked activity under controlled rules.

#### Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships

The Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships allocation is **40,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **8.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports strategic partnerships, integrations, ecosystem grants, product distribution, co-marketing, partner campaigns, and milestone-based growth initiatives.

The allocation is designed for ecosystem expansion under controlled release logic and milestone-based or market-aligned deployment.

#### Liquidity & Market Operations

The Liquidity & Market Operations allocation is **30,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **6.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports liquidity seeding, market-structure needs, exchange support, and market operations where legally, technically, and operationally ready.

This category supports market structure. It does not create a liquidity guarantee, listing guarantee, market-performance guarantee, or price-support promise.

#### Advisors / Strategic Contributors

The Advisors / Strategic Contributors allocation is **15,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **3.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports advisor, expert, operator, contributor, strategic supporter, and partner-contributor alignment where contribution is meaningful and documented.

FUZE aligns this category with bounded vesting, contribution expectations, milestone logic, and long-term ecosystem support.

#### Transparency / Stability Reserve

The Transparency / Stability Reserve is **25,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **5.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports exceptional trust-preserving, transparency-supporting, or stability-related actions under governance controls.

It is not normal sale inventory, not team inventory, and not routine operating inventory. It exists as a trust and stability reserve.
