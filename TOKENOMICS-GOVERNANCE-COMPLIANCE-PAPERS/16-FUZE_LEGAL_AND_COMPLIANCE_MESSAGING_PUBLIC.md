# FUZE Legal and Compliance Messaging

## Executive Summary

FUZE Legal and Compliance Messaging explains the approved public language, restricted claims, jurisdiction sensitivity, token communication boundaries, investor/community messaging standards, and compliance-aware wording for FUZE.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Legal and compliance messaging matters because FUZE connects practical AI SaaS products, Platform Credits, stablecoin rails, token utility, wallet-based records, public reporting, ZAGA game mechanics, QTB market interpretation, AIMM liquidity operations support, private strategic fundraising, and possible future wallet-based participation ability.

These areas can be misunderstood if public language sounds like a guaranteed return, guaranteed payout, guaranteed listing, guaranteed liquidity, price support, dividend, passive income, financial advice, investment advice, trading instruction, legal advice, tax advice, or securities offering.

FUZE public communication uses clear boundaries.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token, not automatic for every wallet, and not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

Seed round discussions are private strategic fundraising conversations, not a public token sale, public investment offer, or public solicitation.

QTB is AI trading intelligence and market interpretation support, not financial advice or guaranteed trading-profit support.

AIMM is AI market-making and liquidity operations support, not price support, guaranteed liquidity, guaranteed listing, guaranteed market outcome, or trading-profit support.

ZAGA game values and game economies are game mechanics or controlled utility surfaces, not guaranteed financial earnings.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, trading volume, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, AI accuracy, or investment return.
