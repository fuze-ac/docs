# FUZE Platform Credits Relationship

## Executive Summary

FUZE Platform Credits Relationship explains the relationship between Platform Credits, product usage, FUZE token, stablecoins, product revenue, and wallet-based participation boundaries.

Platform Credits are product usage credits for supported FUZE products and services.

They help users access practical product workflows such as AI generation, reports, spreadsheet mapping, shop operations, training materials, community summaries, market interpretation reports, liquidity operations reports, event intelligence, sponsored visibility, game utility surfaces where supported, and Botmad work-assistance sessions.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Platform Credits are separate from FUZE token. They are not FUZE token, not equity, not debt, not investment contracts, not income rights, not participation rights, not profit-share rights, not yield products, not governance rights, and not wallet-based participation claims.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails. Stablecoins may be used to buy product usage, settle payments, compensate contributors, or support treasury operations where supported, but stablecoin flows do not automatically create participation rights or approved distributable value.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token, not automatic for every wallet, and not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

Buying Platform Credits, spending Platform Credits, receiving bonus credits, or using FUZE products does not create automatic wallet-based participation eligibility.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, or investment return.

## 9. Platform Credits and Stablecoins

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

A user may use a supported payment method, including stablecoins where supported, to pay for product usage or Platform Credits.

A stablecoin payment flow may look like this:

1. User pays for a supported FUZE product, package, or credit balance.
2. Payment is confirmed through the supported payment rail.
3. Platform Credits may be recorded to the user, account, or workspace where supported.
4. User spends Platform Credits on supported product actions.
5. FUZE records usage, support events, reports, and product service activity where appropriate.
6. Payment records may be reconciled for accounting, refund, fee, tax, support, and treasury purposes.

Stablecoins remain payment rails.

Platform Credits remain product usage credits.

FUZE token remains the single ecosystem token.

Boundary:

Stablecoin payment does not automatically create wallet-based participation rights or approved distributable value.
