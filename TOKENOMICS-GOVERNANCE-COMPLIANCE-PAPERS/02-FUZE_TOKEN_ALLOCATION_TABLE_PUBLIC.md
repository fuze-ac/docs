# FUZE Token Allocation Table

## Executive Summary

FUZE Token Allocation Table defines the fixed token supply and final public allocation model for FUZE token.

FUZE uses one ecosystem token: FUZE token.

FUZE uses a fixed total supply of **500,000,000 FUZE** organized across ten purpose-specific allocation categories.

The allocation model supports FUZE as a product-first AI SaaS and Web3 ecosystem, not a token-first project.

The allocation model follows five core tokenomics principles:

**Purpose-specific allocation. Vault-based control. Controlled circulation. Product-first utility. Long-term ecosystem alignment.**

FUZE separates:

| System | Public Role |
|---|---|
| FUZE token | Ecosystem participation, alignment, governance direction where applicable, product-connected utility, and wallet-based participation ability where activated |
| Platform Credits | Product usage credits for FUZE products and services |
| Stablecoins | Payment, settlement, treasury, and operational compensation rails |

The allocation table gives public readers a clear view of how the full FUZE token supply is structured.

It defines the role of community participation, legacy-holder migration continuity, team and contributor alignment, treasury discipline, foundation stewardship, holder incentives, ecosystem growth, liquidity-related operations, advisor alignment, and transparency reserves.

This paper is not a token sale document, investment recommendation, public solicitation, listing plan, liquidity assurance, price forecast, payout policy, legal opinion, tax opinion, accounting policy, or financial-return promise.

### 4.4 Allocation Category Details

#### Community Participation Allocation

The Community Participation Allocation is **110,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **22.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports eligible community participation, public/community access structures, product-user participation, contributor recognition, future community programs, and long-term ecosystem participation.

FUZE uses the public language **Community Participation Allocation** and **Community Participation Round** instead of price-first sale language.

This keeps the category aligned with product participation and public trust instead of discount-based speculation.

This allocation is separate from BOARD / Surfboard Migration.

Boundary:

Community Participation Allocation is not a dividend pool, yield pool, guaranteed payout pool, public investment offer, public solicitation, guaranteed token access for every user, guaranteed liquidity source, or market-outcome promise.

#### BOARD / Surfboard Migration

The BOARD / Surfboard Migration allocation is **25,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **5.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports eligible legacy-holder continuity from the earlier BOARD / Surfboard context into the FUZE ecosystem.

It is not public sale inventory, not team inventory, not general treasury inventory, not liquidity inventory, and not Community Participation Allocation.

Boundary:

BOARD / Surfboard Migration does not guarantee market value, exchange value, token price, liquidity, listing, or claim eligibility for every public reader.

#### Team Allocation

The Team Allocation is **45,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **9.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports long-term alignment for founders, core team members, builders, operators, product owners, engineers, and future key contributors.

FUZE applies controlled vesting logic for team alignment.

The purpose is long-term builder commitment, not immediate discretionary circulation.

Boundary:

Team Allocation does not guarantee product delivery, product revenue, business result, token price, liquidity, listing, or investment return.

#### Foundation Reserve

The Foundation Reserve is **35,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **7.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports long-horizon stewardship, mission continuity, platform-level governance readiness, and reserve-backed trust architecture.

Foundation Reserve tokens are part of FUZE’s long-term stewardship design.

Boundary:

Foundation Reserve is not normal sale inventory, payout supply, public claim value, or automatic approved distributable value.

#### Treasury Reserve

The Treasury Reserve is **120,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **24.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation is the largest single strategic reserve.

It supports long-term platform runway, product infrastructure, ecosystem development, treasury management, operating flexibility, and controlled strategic use.

The Treasury Reserve is governed as a strategic platform reserve.

Boundary:

Treasury Reserve is not a scheduled public-release bucket, general discretionary sell bucket, payout pool, or approved distributable value by default.

#### Holder Incentives

The Holder Incentives allocation is **55,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **11.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports holder programs, product-user incentives, loyalty, referrals, ecosystem campaigns, participation recognition, and community activation.

FUZE uses Holder Incentives to support ecosystem participation and product-linked activity under controlled rules.

Holder Incentives do not guarantee token price, token demand, product adoption, community growth, game earnings, payout, income, yield, or investment return.

#### Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships

The Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships allocation is **40,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **8.00%** of the total supply.

This allocation supports strategic partnerships, integrations, ecosystem grants, product distribution, co-marketing, partner campaigns, and milestone-based growth initiatives.

The allocation is designed for ecosystem expansion under controlled release logic and milestone-based or market-aligned deployment.

Boundary:

Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships allocation does not guarantee partnership success, user growth, product revenue, token demand, market price, liquidity, listing, or exit.

| Allocation Category | Control Direction |
|---|---|
| Community Participation Allocation | Community participation contract or vault structure |
| BOARD / Surfboard Migration | Migration contract or eligibility vault |
| Team Allocation | Team vesting vault |
| Foundation Reserve | Foundation reserve vault |
| Treasury Reserve | Treasury reserve vault |

| Liquidity & Market Operations | Liquidity operations vault |

| Transparency / Stability Reserve | Transparency or stability reserve vault |

Vault-based control supports clearer release management.

It keeps allocation categories from being treated as a single loose token pool.

### 6.2 Controlled Circulation Relationship

Allocation does not equal immediate circulation.

Each allocation category can have its own release logic, vault control, eligibility conditions, vesting, milestone review, or governance control.

Controlled circulation can include:

- eligibility checks
- vesting schedules
- claim windows
- release rules
- reserve controls
- operational review
- governance review
- liquidity planning
- public reporting
- auditability where applicable

The public rule is:

Allocation defines purpose. Circulation follows controls.
