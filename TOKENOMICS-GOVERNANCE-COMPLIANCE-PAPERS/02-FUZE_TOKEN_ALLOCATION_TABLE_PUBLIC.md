## Allocation Principles

### Fixed Supply Reconciliation

The approved amounts must reconcile to:

```text
110,000,000
+ 25,000,000
+ 45,000,000
+ 35,000,000
+ 120,000,000
+ 55,000,000
+ 40,000,000
+ 30,000,000
+ 15,000,000

= 500,000,000 FUZE
```

The approved percentages must reconcile to:

```text
22.00%
+ 5.00%
+ 9.00%
+ 7.00%
+ 24.00%
+ 11.00%
+ 8.00%
+ 6.00%
+ 3.00%
+ 5.00%
= 100.00%
```

A report that does not reconcile should identify the difference and correction state rather than silently changing a category.

### Purpose-Bound Categories

Each allocation category has a defined mandate.

Tokens assigned to one category should not be treated as interchangeable with another category merely because they share:

- a wallet;
- a multisig;
- a vault provider;
- a reporting dashboard;
- a broad functional group;
- a common signer set;
- or a general ecosystem objective.

Category purpose remains controlling unless a formal reclassification is approved and recorded.

### Allocation Does Not Mean Availability

An approved allocation may remain:

- reserved;
- under custody;
- subject to vesting;
- subject to release conditions;
- subject to eligibility;
- unassigned to an end recipient;
- committed but not transferred;
- deployed to a controlled program;
- distributed but restricted;
- or otherwise non-circulating.

Readers should not infer circulating supply, available inventory, token ownership, claim rights, market liquidity, or payout capacity from the allocation table.

### Records Explain State Changes

A category-level state change should identify:

- category;
- amount;

- contract where applicable;
- authority;
- purpose;
- release condition;
- restriction state;
- transaction or program reference;

- and current status.

### Public Reporting Preserves Privacy and Security

Public reports may show approved category, address, contract, balance, movement, and status information.

They should not expose:

- private keys;
- seed phrases;
- credentials;
- recovery material;
- exploitable signer arrangements;
- private identities;
- private wallet-person mappings;
- private agreements;
- legal work product;
- tax records;

- security evidence;
- or confidential commercial terms.

## Allocation-State Model

Tokens within one category may occupy different states.

| State | Meaning | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Approved allocation | Supply is assigned to the controlling category. | Custody, release, or ownership |
| Reserved | Supply remains under category control for its approved mandate. | Commitment or release |
| Planned | Supply is included in a proposal or plan. | Approval or obligation |
| Committed | Supply is subject to an approved obligation, award, agreement, or program. | Transfer or circulation |
| Locked or vesting | Supply or recipient entitlement remains subject to time, milestone, service, or technical restrictions. | Free transferability or circulation |
| Authorized for release | The applicable authority has approved a stated movement. | Execution |
| Released | Supply is made available under an approved process. | Distribution or circulation |
| Transferred | A blockchain or custody movement occurred. | Purpose, entitlement, or circulation classification |
| Distributed | Supply reached an intended recipient, contract, pool, or program. | Free transferability, active utility, or market activity |
| Deployed | Supply is being used in an approved contract, pool, program, or operation. | Circulation or market liquidity under every methodology |
| Restricted after distribution | The recipient or program holds tokens subject to continuing restrictions. | Circulating status |
| Circulating | Supply meets the stated circulating-supply methodology. | Liquidity, demand, depth, price, or active use |
| Returned | Supply moved back into approved category custody. | Cancellation of all related obligations |
| Recovered | Supply was recovered under an approved program or correction process. | Immediate reusability |
| Cancelled | A planned, contingent, or unvested assignment ended. | Category reclassification |
| Suspended or disputed | Further action is paused pending investigation, correction, or decision. | Final cancellation or retirement |
| Corrected | A prior amount, category, address, classification, or record was corrected. | Removal of the original history |
| Closed or retired | A program or mechanism ended under the applicable process. | Removal of required records or remaining category supply |

The primary classification paper is [FUZE Token Release and Circulation Clarity](13-FUZE_TOKEN_RELEASE_AND_CIRCULATION_CLARITY_PUBLIC.md).

## Allocation, Release, Unlock, Distribution, and Circulation

These terms should not be used interchangeably.

### Allocation

Allocation assigns supply to an approved category and purpose.

### Custody Assignment

Custody assignment identifies where and under whose control allocated supply is held.

### Commitment

Commitment records an approved obligation, award, agreement, budget, or program against category capacity.

### Release

Release makes an approved amount available under a stated process.

### Unlock

Unlock removes or expires a technical or contractual restriction.

An unlock does not prove transfer, distribution, sale, or circulation.

### Transfer

Transfer moves tokens between addresses or custody accounts.

A transfer does not explain purpose or classification by itself.

### Distribution

Distribution delivers tokens to an intended recipient, contract, pool, or program.

Distribution does not necessarily establish free transferability or circulation.

### Circulation

Circulation is a reporting classification under a stated methodology.

The methodology should explain treatment of:

- reserves;
- team and advisor vesting;
- custodial addresses;
- program contracts;
- liquidity pools;
- treasury-controlled wallets;
- bridge contracts;
- locked tokens;
- migrated tokens;
- claimed and unclaimed tokens;
- and returned or recoverable tokens.

### Claim and Payout

Claim eligibility, claim submission, claim approval, payout submission, payout confirmation, settlement, and reconciliation are separate states.

An allocation does not create an automatic claim or payout right.

## Reclassification and Allocation Amendments

The approved total supply and category values remain controlling unless a formal amendment is approved and published.

### Operational Movement Within One Category

A wallet or vault movement does not constitute reclassification when:

- the category remains the same;
- the approved mandate remains the same;
- the amount remains within category authority;
- custody and reporting records are updated;
- and no recipient or program purpose is changed.

### Reclassification Between Categories

A movement that changes the controlling purpose is a reclassification.

A reclassification should require:

- written rationale;
- source category;
- destination category;
- amount;
- percentage effect;
- purpose effect;
- existing commitment review;
- recipient or program effect;
- governance approval;
- treasury and custody review;
- legal, accounting, tax, compliance, security, or market review where applicable;
- updated allocation ledger;
- updated vault mapping;
- effective date;
- public communication;
- and correction history.

The amended table must still reconcile to:

- **500,000,000 FUZE**; and
- **100.00%**.

Silent category substitution is not permitted under this public control model.

### Amendment Status

Possible amendment states include:

- proposed;
- under review;
- approved;
- rejected;
- effective;
- superseded;
- corrected;
- and withdrawn.

A proposal should not be presented as an effective allocation change.
