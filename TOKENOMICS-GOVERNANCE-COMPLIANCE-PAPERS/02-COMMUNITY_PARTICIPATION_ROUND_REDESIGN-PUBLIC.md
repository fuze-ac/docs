# Community Participation Round Redesign

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses the **Community Participation Round** as the public-facing name for its redesigned eligible community access model.

The Community Participation Round moves FUZE away from price-tier, discount-driven, token-first messaging and toward a product-aligned, compliance-aware, transparent participation system for eligible community members, real users, contributors, and long-term ecosystem supporters.

FUZE keeps the full **110,000,000 FUZE Community Participation Allocation** inside the fixed **500,000,000 FUZE** total supply. This allocation is organized for community participation, product-user participation, contributor recognition, future community programs, and long-term ecosystem alignment.

FUZE separates the Community Participation Round from legacy-holder migration. The **BOARD / Surfboard Migration** allocation is a separate **25,000,000 FUZE** allocation reserved for eligible legacy-holder continuity. It is already included in the fixed **500,000,000 FUZE** total supply and is not part of the Community Participation Round.

FUZE also separates FUZE token, Platform Credits, and stablecoin compensation systems:

**FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage. Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation.**

This paper defines the Community Participation Round redesign, the public language model, the allocation relationship, the product context, the eligibility and access boundary, the controlled circulation relationship, the investor and community relevance, and the risk safeguards.

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE uses **Community Participation Round** as the public-facing name for the redesigned community access model.

FUZE uses **Community Participation Allocation** when referring to the full **110,000,000 FUZE** allocation inside the total FUZE tokenomics structure.

**The Community Participation Round is a product-aligned, compliance-aware, eligibility-controlled participation model for eligible community members, real users, contributors, and long-term ecosystem supporters.**

**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**

This means the Community Participation Round is not presented as a price-first campaign. It is presented as a public participation structure connected to FUZE product usage, ZAGA utility, community access, contributor recognition, controlled circulation, and long-term ecosystem alignment.

FUZE retires public framing based on early price-tier language. FUZE instead uses final public terminology:

| Historical / Retired Framing | FUZE Public Language |
|---|---|

| Presale allocation language | Community Participation Allocation |

| Market-price anchoring | Market-aligned access only where final policy allows |

FUZE does not present the Community Participation Round through price multiple framing, upside framing, discount messaging, pre-listing purchase language, liquidity assurance, listing assurance, or market-performance claims.
