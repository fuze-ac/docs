# Platform Credits Relationship

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses **Platform Credits** as the product-usage layer across FUZE products and services.

Platform Credits are designed for software usage, AI usage, reports, dashboards, workflows, sync actions, shop operations, voice generation, community operations, game-related product actions, intelligence reports, sponsored visibility, supervised work sessions, and other platform services where product rules allow.

FUZE token is separate from Platform Credits.

**FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage.**

This separation protects public clarity. FUZE does not present Platform Credits as investment assets, payout assets, ownership assets, token substitutes, claim rights, governance rights, market exposure, or financial-return instruments. Platform Credits are a product accounting, billing, usage, access, and reporting layer.

The Platform Credits model helps FUZE operate as a product-first AI SaaS platform. It supports subscriptions, usage packs, add-ons, workspace billing, partner packages, AI metering, reporting, and user-facing product limits. It also helps Web2 users use FUZE products without needing crypto knowledge.

This paper defines the public relationship between Platform Credits, FUZE token, stablecoin compensation, product usage, product revenue, public vault access, community participation, and FUZE Core Platform rails.

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE presents Platform Credits as product usage credits.

FUZE position:

**Platform Credits are the product-usage rail of the FUZE platform. FUZE token is the ecosystem participation asset.**

The Platform Credits model follows FUZE’s public execution order:

**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**

Platform Credits support product usage first. They allow users, teams, shops, communities, partners, and workspaces to access product features and platform services. They do not create token exposure or investment exposure.

Credit usage gives access to product actions, AI usage, reports, workflows, sponsored visibility, game-related product usage, or platform services where product rules allow. ### 6.9 Abuse and Fair Use Boundary
