# FUZE Approved Distributable Value Model

## Executive Summary

Approved distributable value is a controlled status applied to a defined amount of value from one or more approved FUZE product revenue pools after the relevant records have completed:

- source classification;
- revenue recognition;
- period close;
- settlement verification;
- reconciliation;
- deduction and cost treatment;
- reserve treatment;
- currency or asset conversion treatment;
- accounting review;
- legal and compliance review;
- treasury and custody confirmation;
- audit or assurance review where required;
- governance approval;
- and public-report preparation.

The controlling sequence is:

```text
source receipts and commercial records
-> confirmed product revenue
-> reconciled product revenue
-> net pool value
-> reserves and period adjustments
-> candidate distributable value
-> required review and authorization
-> approved distributable value
-> assignment to an activated mechanism, retention, deferral, correction, or cancellation
-> claim and payout processing where separately activated
-> final reconciliation and closure
```

Each state is separate.

Customer payment does not automatically equal recognized product revenue.

Recognized product revenue does not automatically equal reconciled product revenue.

Reconciled product revenue does not automatically equal candidate distributable value.

Candidate distributable value does not automatically equal approved distributable value.

Approved distributable value does not automatically create:

- wallet eligibility;
- claim availability;
- claim approval;
- payout authorization;
- payout settlement;
- token release;
- token circulation;
- revenue share;
- income;
- or financial return.

The model begins with a narrow, versioned product-revenue-pool definition and a closed or controlled reporting period. It then identifies what value is included, what remains excluded, which obligations and costs apply, which reserves are required, how multiple currencies or assets are treated, and which unresolved items must remain deferred or reserved.

An approved-value record should identify:

- pool and period;
- source records;
- accounting method;
- confirmed revenue;
- reconciliation status;
- deductions;
- costs;
- reserves;
- conversion method;
- candidate amount;
- approved amount;
- denomination and payout-asset treatment;
- treasury and custody location;
- review and approval authority;
- restrictions;
- expiration or reassessment trigger;
- assignment or retention status;
- correction and restatement state;
- public-report status;
- and current-as-of date.

The same value should not be counted simultaneously as:

- unrestricted operating funds;
- reserved value;
- approved distributable value;
- assigned claim funding;
- payout funding;
- or another committed use.

The value ledger should preserve source, status, movement, assignment, use, return, correction, and closure records.

This paper owns the approved-distributable-value lifecycle, calculation structure, pool specification, period close, deductions, reserves, conversion treatment, approval packet, decision record, value ledger, assignment to active processes, corrections, restatements, public reporting, and closure.

The readiness and activation system appears in [FUZE Participation Activation Gates](08-FUZE_PARTICIPATION_ACTIVATION_GATES_PUBLIC.md).

The wallet, eligibility, claim, and payout workflow appears in [FUZE Wallet-Based Participation Model](07-FUZE_WALLET_BASED_PARTICIPATION_MODEL_PUBLIC.md).

## Public Position

Approved distributable value is a period-specific, pool-specific, versioned, reviewed, and authorized value status.

It is not a standing percentage of every FUZE receipt.

It is not an automatic consequence of product revenue.

It is not an unrestricted treasury balance.

It is not a general promise to token holders.

It is not automatically claimable or payable.

FUZE may decide that a product revenue pool should be:

- retained for product delivery;
- retained for operations;
- reserved for refunds or liabilities;
- used for growth;
- used for security, legal, accounting, audit, support, infrastructure, or continuity;
- deferred pending evidence;
- excluded from participation;
- partially approved;
- fully approved;
- or unavailable for the period.

A period may close with:

- no candidate distributable value;
- no approved distributable value;
- a deferred decision;
- a partial approval;
- an approved amount retained pending activation;
- or an approved amount assigned to an active process.

The model should never be described as a guaranteed dividend, automatic revenue share, passive-income promise, fixed return, yield commitment, or assured payout.

## Value-State Model

| State | Evidence-backed meaning | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Source receipt | A payment or settlement record exists. | Product revenue classification |
| Classified receipt | Business context and category are assigned. | Revenue recognition |
| Confirmed product revenue | The receipt is linked to an approved offer, product activity, settlement, and applicable recognition basis. | Period reconciliation |
| Reconciled product revenue | Product, payment, treasury, and accounting records are matched and controlled for the period. | Candidate distributable value |
| Net pool value | Applicable transaction adjustments, costs, liabilities, and obligations are reflected before reserves. | Approval |
| Reserved value | Value is retained for defined obligations, uncertainty, continuity, or risk. | Availability to participation |
| Candidate distributable value | A calculated amount is submitted for required review. | Approval or treasury assignment |
| Approved distributable value | The authorized decision approves a stated amount for a stated pool, period, denomination, purpose, and scope. | Claim availability or payout |
| Approved but retained | Approved value remains controlled and unassigned to an active mechanism. | Participant right |
| Assigned to active process | Approved value is committed to an activated participation scope. | Individual claim approval |
| Claim-funded amount | Value is reserved or allocated to an active claim process. | Payout settlement |
| Payout-authorized amount | Value is authorized for transfer or settlement. | Submission or confirmation |
| Payout-submitted amount | A transaction or provider instruction has been sent. | Confirmation or finality |
| Payout-confirmed amount | Network or provider confirmation exists. | Final reconciliation |
| Settled amount | Participant, treasury, accounting, and reporting records agree. | Future eligibility or future payout |
| Deferred value | Approval or use is postponed pending evidence, time, or another condition. | Availability |
| Corrected value | A later authorized adjustment changes a prior record. | Deletion of historical state |
| Cancelled value | Prior approval or assignment is withdrawn under authority. | Erasure of source records |
| Closed value | The period and related obligations are reconciled and complete or controlled. | Availability for a new period |

These states should not be collapsed into labels such as:

- revenue available;
- holder revenue;
- distributable revenue;
- claimable revenue;
- paid revenue;
- or circulating value

without the exact status and evidence.

## Core Definitions

### Source Receipt

A source receipt is a recorded payment, transfer, settlement, or other inflow through an approved channel.

A source receipt needs business context before it can be classified.

Possible classifications include:

- customer payment;
- deferred customer payment;
- refundable deposit;
- tax or pass-through amount;
- financing receipt;
- token-related receipt;
- partner funding;
- grant;
- internal transfer;
- treasury movement;
- unidentified receipt;
- fraudulent or disputed receipt;
- or another approved category.

### Confirmed Product Revenue

Confirmed product revenue is value linked to:

- an approved product or offer;
- a customer or authorized account;
- an invoice, order, contract, or other commercial record;
- payment or settlement evidence;
- fulfilled or recognized product activity;
- the applicable reporting period;
- and the applicable recognition method.

### Reconciled Product Revenue

Reconciled product revenue is confirmed product revenue after the applicable:

- refunds;
- reversals;
- chargebacks;
- failed settlement;
- duplicate records;
- timing adjustments;
- provider differences;
- treasury differences;
- currency differences;
- and correction entries

have been matched or controlled.

### Net Pool Value

Net pool value is the amount remaining after the approved treatment of:

- transaction adjustments;
- taxes and statutory liabilities;
- payment, network, and conversion costs;
- product delivery costs;
- partner and vendor obligations;
- shared allocated costs;
- and other approved obligations

before final reserve treatment and approval.

### Reserved Value

Reserved value is retained for defined obligations, uncertainty, risk, operating continuity, customer protection, or another approved purpose.

Reserved value remains controlled and unavailable to the participation mechanism unless later released and reconsidered under the approved process.

### Candidate Distributable Value

Candidate distributable value is the calculated amount submitted to the applicable reviewers and approval authority.

Candidate status does not create a participant right or treasury-movement authority.

### Approved Distributable Value

Approved distributable value is candidate value that has received the required authorization for the stated:

- pool;
- period;
- amount;
- denomination;
- purpose;
- mechanism scope;
- custody;
- restrictions;
- review period;
- and reporting treatment.

### Assigned Value

Assigned value is approved distributable value committed to a specific activated mechanism, period, claim process, or payout scope.

Assignment does not create an individual claim approval.

### Claimable Amount

A claimable amount is the value made available to an eligible wallet or participant under an activated claim process.

The claimable amount may be calculated only after eligibility, formula, minimum, rounding, reserve, and other active rules are applied.

### Settled Amount

A settled amount is value for which:

- authorization;
- transaction or provider submission;
- confirmation;
- finality;
- participant treatment;
- treasury reconciliation;
- accounting reconciliation;
- and reporting

are complete for the stated scope.

## Product-Revenue-Pool Specification

Every approved-value calculation begins with a defined and versioned product revenue pool.

| Field | Required definition |
|---|---|
| Pool identifier | Stable name and version |
| Operating entity | Entity or controlled operating scope that owns or records the revenue |
| Product scope | Included products, offers, modules, services, events, subscriptions, or transaction types |
| Excluded scope | Products, receipts, or transaction types outside the pool |
| Customer and account scope | Included customer, organization, merchant, user, or partner categories |
| Reporting period | Start, end, cutoff, timezone, and close date |
| Recognition basis | Applicable product and accounting method |
| Fulfillment basis | Required product delivery, consumption, access, milestone, or service evidence |
| Payment routes | Included fiat, card, invoice, stablecoin, bank, partner-funded, or other approved routes |
| Settlement state | Required confirmation, finality, and provider treatment |
| Platform Credit treatment | Purchase, grant, inclusion, reservation, consumption, refund, adjustment, expiry, and recognition treatment |
| Currency or asset basis | Reporting denomination and conversion method |
| Deduction policy | Applicable transaction, cost, liability, and allocation categories |
| Reserve policy | Reserve purposes, methods, owners, triggers, and release treatment |
| Treasury scope | Source accounts, wallets, providers, and subledgers |
| Owners | Product, finance, accounting, treasury, legal, compliance, audit, reporting, and approval roles |
| Evidence class | Public, permissioned, restricted, confidential, or security-sensitive treatment |
| Review trigger | New period, material change, incident, provider change, correction, or scheduled review |
| Current status | Draft, under review, approved, active for calculation, closed, corrected, or retired |
| Current-as-of date | Time to which the specification applies |

A pool should be narrow enough to reconcile.

“All FUZE revenue” is not a sufficient definition when products, entities, payment routes, recognition methods, fulfillment evidence, cost structures, and periods differ.

### One Pool or Multiple Pools

FUZE may calculate:

- one product pool;
- one product-family pool;
- one entity pool;
- one campaign or event pool;
- or multiple separately reconciled pools combined under an approved method.

Where multiple pools are combined, each pool should remain independently traceable.

### Pool Ownership

The pool should have named owners for:

- product scope;
- source records;
- fulfillment evidence;
- accounting classification;
- treasury reconciliation;
- legal and compliance review;
- cost allocation;
- reserve proposal;
- approval packet;
- public reporting;
- and correction.

## Source-Receipt Classification

Every material source receipt should receive an approved classification before entering the pool.

| Classification | Typical treatment |
|---|---|
| Customer payment for delivered or recognized product activity | May enter confirmed product revenue when all conditions are satisfied |
| Customer prepayment or refundable deposit | Deferred, restricted, or liability treatment until recognition conditions are met |
| Unpaid invoice | Excluded until payment or applicable recognition treatment is satisfied |
| Failed or reversed settlement | Excluded or corrected |
| Tax or pass-through amount | Excluded or treated as liability under the approved method |
| Partner-funded product activity | Included only under the approved commercial and recognition treatment |
| Grant or sponsorship | Separately classified and included only if the pool specification permits it |
| Equity, debt, seed, or financing receipt | Excluded from ordinary product revenue |
| Token sale or token-allocation-related proceeds | Excluded and governed by separate token and fundraising policies |
| Internal treasury transfer | Excluded as revenue |
| Liquidity or market-operations transfer | Excluded from product revenue |
| Unrealized asset appreciation | Excluded unless an approved accounting method requires separate reporting, not ordinary product revenue |
| Promotional Platform Credit grant | Excluded as a purchased receipt unless a separate recognized-revenue basis applies |
| Customer purchase of Platform Credits | Treated according to the approved recognition policy, not automatically as distributable value |
| Game score, simulated balance, Token Value, Net Worth, or internal resource | Excluded from external product revenue |
| Unidentified, disputed, restricted, or fraudulent receipt | Held outside the pool until resolved |

Exclusion from the approved-value model does not determine every legal or accounting conclusion.

It means the source cannot enter the candidate calculation without the required classification and review.

- purchase;
- allocation;
- activation;
- reservation;
- consumption;
- service delivery;
- milestone completion;
- expiry;

The records should distinguish:

- purchased credits;
- included-plan credits;
- promotional credits;
- partner-funded credits;
- refunded credits;
- reversed credits;
- expired credits;
- consumed credits;
- unused balances;
- and accounting adjustments.

Platform Credit purchase does not automatically create:

Possible events include:

- service delivery;
- subscription period earned;
- Platform Credit consumption;
- product access delivered;
- milestone acceptance;
- event completion;
- partner obligation completed;
- merchant or customer transaction fulfilled;
- or another approved basis.

The method should address:

- partial fulfillment;
- cancelled service;

## Reconciliation Model

The model should reconcile at least the following layers where applicable:

```text
product orders and fulfillment
<-> invoices and billing
<-> payments and settlements
<-> bank, wallet, or provider balances
<-> Platform Credit activity
<-> accounting records
<-> treasury records
<-> candidate-value calculation
<-> approved-value ledger
<-> claim and payout records where activated
```

Possible categories include:

- timing difference;
- provider fee;

A material unresolved difference should:

- block candidate calculation;
- reduce the candidate amount;
- enter reserve treatment;
- or be formally controlled under an approved exception

rather than being ignored.

## Calculation Structure

The control formula is:

```text
confirmed product revenue
- refunds, reversals, chargebacks, and failed settlement
- taxes, statutory liabilities, and pass-through amounts
- payment, banking, network, and conversion costs
- direct product delivery and support costs
- partner, vendor, contributor, referral, and service obligations
- allocated shared operating costs under an approved method
- approved continuity, tax, support, incident, and risk reserves
+ or - approved period adjustments and reserve releases
= candidate distributable value
```

This is a control structure, not a universal accounting rule.

The exact treatment depends on:

- product;
- entity;
- contract;
- transaction;
- asset;
- jurisdiction;
- accounting method;
- reporting period;
- and professional review.

### No Double Counting

The calculation should prevent:

- the same receipt from entering two pools;
- the same cost from being deducted twice;
- the same reserve from being applied twice;
- the same released reserve from entering multiple periods;
- the same approved value from being assigned to multiple processes;
- and the same payout from being recorded through multiple settlement states.

### Negative or Zero Outcome

A period may produce:

- negative net pool value;
- zero candidate value;
- no candidate submission;
- or no approved value.

The model should not force a positive amount.

### Multiple Pools

Where multiple pools contribute to one candidate amount, the packet should show:

- each pool's confirmed revenue;
- each pool's deductions;
- each pool's reserves;
- each pool's net result;
- inter-pool eliminations;
- shared-cost allocation;
- and combined candidate amount.

## Reserve Model

Reserves retain value for obligations, uncertainty, risk, or continuity.

Possible reserve categories include:

- refunds and chargebacks;
- tax and statutory obligations;
- service completion;
- customer support;
- infrastructure and AI usage;
- payment-provider delay;
- partner and vendor obligations;
- legal and compliance work;
- accounting and audit work;
- security and incident response;
- privacy incidents;
- contract or provider failure;
- treasury and operating continuity;
- disputed claims;
- payout failures;
- currency or asset volatility;
- and correction exposure.

### Reserve Record

Each reserve should identify:

1. reserve identifier;
2. pool and period;
3. purpose;
4. basis;
5. amount;
6. denomination;
7. source evidence;
8. owner;
9. reviewer;
10. approval;
11. custody or subledger treatment;
12. effective date;
13. review trigger;
14. release trigger;
15. usage rules;
16. correction treatment;
17. public-reporting treatment;
18. current status; and
19. current-as-of date.

### Reserve Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Proposed | Reserve is being calculated or justified |
| Under review | Evidence and method are being reviewed |
| Approved | Reserve is authorized for the stated purpose and period |
| Held | Value remains controlled for the reserve purpose |
| Partially used | Some value has been applied to the obligation |
| Released for reconsideration | The reserve is no longer required in full, but value has not automatically entered candidate value |
| Corrected | Amount or treatment changed through approval |
| Closed | Obligation and remaining treatment are reconciled |

Reserve release should enter a new or corrected review process.

It should not automatically create approved distributable value.

- approve value subject to conditions;
- return the packet for correction;
- reject the candidate amount;
- or cancel a prior approval before use.

### Ledger Reconciliation

The ledger should reconcile:

```text
opening approved value
- assigned value
- claim-funded value
- payout-authorized value not already reflected elsewhere
- settled value
- approved cancellations or returns
+ approved recoveries and corrections
= remaining controlled value
```

The exact formula should prevent double subtraction across status transitions.

The ledger should use one controlled movement model rather than treating every status as a new use of value.

### No Double Commitment

A single approved amount should not be assigned simultaneously to:

- operations;
- reserve;
- another participation period;
- another claim process;
- another payout batch;
- a partner obligation;
- market operations;
- or another committed use.

## Key Takeaways

- Approved distributable value is a period-specific, pool-specific, versioned, reviewed, and authorized value status.
- Customer payment, product revenue, reconciled revenue, candidate value, approved value, assigned value, claimable value, payout value, and settled value are different states.
- Every calculation begins with a narrow product-revenue-pool specification and a controlled period close.
- Platform Credit purchase, allocation, consumption, refund, adjustment, and expiry require their own recognition treatment and do not automatically create approved value.
- Deductions, shared costs, reserves, currency conversion, and period adjustments should be documented, reproducible, and protected against double counting.
- A candidate amount enters a formal packet covering pool evidence, reconciliation, costs, reserves, custody, review, conditions, and public reporting.
- Approval may be full, partial, deferred, conditional, rejected, retained, corrected, cancelled, or closed.
- The approved-value ledger should prevent the same value from being committed simultaneously to operations, reserves, claims, payouts, or another process.
- Approved value does not create wallet eligibility, claim availability, payout entitlement, market access, liquidity, income, or financial return.
- Corrections, restatements, recoveries, offsets, pauses, and closures should preserve prior records and use accountable authority.
