# Liquidity and Listing Policy

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses the **Liquidity and Listing Policy** to define how liquidity, exchange readiness, market-structure support, listing preparation, and public communication are handled.

The policy separates liquidity planning from listing promises. FUZE does not guarantee exchange listing, listing timing, liquidity, price performance, trading volume, market outcome, profit, return, payout, or market performance. Liquidity and listing work remain subject to product readiness, legal review, compliance review, treasury planning, market conditions, exchange standards, technical readiness, security readiness, and operational controls.

The policy connects to the **Liquidity & Market Operations** allocation of **30,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **6.00%** of the fixed **500,000,000 FUZE** total supply. This allocation supports market-structure needs, liquidity seeding, exchange support, and approved market operations under controlled policy.

FUZE also separates token allocation from liquidity capital. Stablecoin or ETH pairing capital is budgeted separately and is not assumed without treasury planning.

FUZE’s liquidity and listing model supports the broader platform direction:

**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**

FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage. Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation.

This paper defines FUZE’s liquidity and listing policy, market-structure allocation logic, listing-readiness framework, liquidity deployment controls, reporting direction, public boundaries, and risk safeguards.

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE presents liquidity and listing as controlled market-structure work, not public performance promises.

FUZE position:

**Liquidity and listing activity supports market-structure readiness, exchange-readiness preparation, and responsible public communication. It does not guarantee listing, liquidity, price, trading profit, market outcome, payout, or return.**

The Liquidity and Listing Policy follows FUZE’s tokenomics principle:

**Purpose-specific allocation. Vault-based control. Controlled circulation. Product-first utility. Long-term ecosystem alignment.**

FUZE does not use liquidity and listing language as hype. FUZE uses it as an operating policy for market-structure planning, treasury controls, exchange readiness, liquidity operations, public risk language, and transparency reporting where appropriate.

The policy also supports FUZE’s public growth position:

**FUZE is not relying on token speculation as the only exit path. FUZE is building real products, real users, revenue potential, shared platform rails, and strategic acquisition optionality.**
