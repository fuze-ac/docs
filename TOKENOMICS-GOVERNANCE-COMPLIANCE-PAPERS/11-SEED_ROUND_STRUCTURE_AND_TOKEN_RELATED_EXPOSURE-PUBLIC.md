# Seed Round Structure and Token-Related Exposure

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses the **Private Strategic Seed Round** as a private strategic fundraising path for qualified interested parties, operator-angels, strategic investors, and early-stage partners.

FUZE presents the Seed Round as private strategic execution funding, not a public token sale. Any token-related exposure is handled only as a private, legally reviewed component of the investment structure. FUZE does not publicly present Seed Round participation as a token-sale campaign, public community round, exchange-listing opportunity, liquidity event, price event, or market-return opportunity.

FUZE’s public Seed Round sentence is:

**FUZE is preparing a Private Strategic Seed Round for strategic investors, operator-angels, and early-stage partners. Full details, structure, and terms are shared privately with qualified interested parties. This is not a public token sale.**

The Seed Round supports FUZE’s movement from foundation into product-first execution, with **HerHelp.com** and **ZAGA.io** as the first major launch focus, supported by FUZE Core Platform rails and accelerated internally by **Botmad**.

FUZE separates Seed Round structure from public community participation, token utility, Platform Credits, future profit participation, stablecoin compensation, liquidity planning, and exchange listing.

**FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage. Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation.**

This paper defines FUZE’s public Seed Round structure, token-related exposure boundary, private investor process, product-first funding purpose, legal-counsel review path, reporting direction, and risk safeguards.
