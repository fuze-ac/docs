# FUZE Public Language Dictionary

## Executive Summary

This dictionary is the compact terminology reference for FUZE tokenomics, governance, wallet, treasury, market-access, funding, reporting, and compliance communication.

Its purpose is not to make every FUZE paper sound the same. Its purpose is to preserve the same meaning when different papers, product pages, investor materials, notices, interfaces, and community updates discuss related subjects.

Writers should use the narrowest accurate term, attach status to the exact subject, and route detailed mechanics to the controlling specialist paper. Consistency means preserving distinctions such as product versus token, allocation versus circulation, deployment versus activation, wallet balance versus eligibility, and discussion versus approved market access.

Product names, product descriptors, and product-writing conventions are maintained in the [FUZE Product Language Dictionary](../AI-SAAS-PRODUCT-PAPERS/17-FUZE_PRODUCT_LANGUAGE_DICTIONARY_PUBLIC.md). Cross-paper editorial routing is maintained in the [FUZE Public Language and Boundary Index](../PUBLIC-INDEX/03-FUZE_PUBLIC_LANGUAGE_AND_BOUNDARY_INDEX.md).

## 1. Purpose and Use

Use this dictionary when drafting or reviewing:

- tokenomics and governance papers;
- wallet, custody, treasury, and participation explanations;
- market-access and liquidity updates;
- fundraising and investor summaries;
- website, FAQ, deck, and one-page copy;
- interface, support, email, social, and community messages;
- AI-assisted documentation.

Each entry includes a public meaning and usage guidance. The meaning should remain stable. The surrounding sentence should be adapted to the audience and objective.

Before publication, verify names, numbers, dates, allocation labels, contract addresses, networks, venues, partners, custody arrangements, status, and links against the current controlling source.

## 2. Core Ecosystem Terms

### FUZE

**Meaning:** The product-first AI SaaS and Web3 ecosystem.

**Usage:** Use “FUZE” for the ecosystem, organization, products, platform context, or operating direction. Do not use it as shorthand for FUZE token.

### Product-first

**Meaning:** The principle that FUZE begins with useful products, users, workflows, and evidence before expanding into token, market, or ecosystem claims.

**Usage:** Demonstrate this principle through the product problem and workflow rather than repeating it as a slogan.

### Product ecosystem

**Meaning:** The connected set of FUZE products and shared platform capabilities.

**Usage:** Use when cross-product relationships are relevant. Use the specific product name when discussing one product.

### Shared platform rails

**Meaning:** Common capabilities that FUZE products may use, including identity, permissions, Platform Credits, payments, AI orchestration, wallet records, and reporting.

**Usage:** Name only the rails relevant to the workflow being described. A public platform design does not prove that every rail is implemented or live.

### FUZE token

**Meaning:** The single ecosystem token used for defined utility and participation functions where approved and activated.

**Usage:** Capitalize FUZE and use lower-case “token.” State the actual utility, user, control, and current status. Route supply, allocation, release, eligibility, custody, and market mechanics to the dedicated tokenomics papers.

### One-token model

**Meaning:** The architectural rule that FUZE uses FUZE token for ecosystem-level token functions instead of introducing another public ecosystem token.

**Usage:** Use where token architecture or classification is central. It does not need to appear in ordinary product or partner copy.

Primary source: [FUZE One-Token Model](03-FUZE_ONE_TOKEN_MODEL_PUBLIC.md).

### Product-connected utility

**Meaning:** A defined use that connects FUZE token to a product or platform action.

**Usage:** Name the product, action, participant, authority, dependency, and status. Product proximity alone does not establish utility.

### Platform Credits

**Meaning:** Usage credits for supported FUZE product actions and services.

**Usage:** Use the exact capitalization “Platform Credits.” Describe the product action they enable. Keep pricing, grants, reservations, consumption, reversals, refunds, and balances separate from FUZE token ownership.

### Stablecoins

**Meaning:** External digital payment instruments that may support approved payment, settlement, treasury, refund, or compensation workflows.

**Usage:** State the operational role, relevant network, custody, and status where approved. A stablecoin balance is not product usage, token utility, revenue, or participant entitlement by itself.

## 3. Product and Token Separation

### Product availability

**Meaning:** The ability of intended users to access a product or feature under current operating conditions.

**Usage:** Product availability does not establish token utility, wallet eligibility, market access, customer adoption, or revenue.

### Product revenue

**Meaning:** Revenue recognized or classified from delivered product or service activity under the applicable accounting process.

**Usage:** Distinguish offer, payment, fulfillment, refund, paid delivery, and reconciled revenue. Do not classify fundraising receipts or treasury transfers as product revenue.

### Token utility

**Meaning:** A defined function performed with or through FUZE token under an approved mechanism.

**Usage:** State whether the function is designed, implemented, tested, activation-gated, or active. Avoid describing broad ecosystem association as utility.

### Token ownership

**Meaning:** A recorded FUZE token position held directly or beneficially under a custody arrangement.

**Usage:** Token ownership does not automatically establish company ownership, revenue rights, wallet eligibility, governance authority, approved value, claimability, liquidity, or market access.

### Corporate ownership

**Meaning:** Ownership rights arising from company shares, membership interests, or another valid corporate instrument.

**Usage:** Keep corporate ownership separate from FUZE token ownership and Platform Credits.

## 4. Supply, Allocation, Release, and Circulation

### Fixed supply

**Meaning:** The total FUZE token supply defined by the approved token model.

**Usage:** Publish the figure only from the current controlling source. State the fixed-supply basis and avoid confusing fixed supply with circulating supply.

### Allocation

**Meaning:** A defined token quantity assigned to an approved category, purpose, or mandate.

**Usage:** Allocation establishes intended control and use. It does not prove commitment, transfer, sale, release, circulation, or distribution.

### Allocation capacity

**Meaning:** The amount remaining available within an approved allocation mandate.

**Usage:** Capacity does not mean the amount has been approved for a specific use or moved from its controlling vault.

### Vault

**Meaning:** A controlled wallet or contract structure associated with an approved allocation, reserve, or operating purpose.

**Usage:** Identify the vault label, mandate, authority, and reporting status where public. A visible balance is not automatically released, accessible, liquid, or distributable.

### Reserve

**Meaning:** Tokens or other assets held for a defined future, contingency, liquidity, operating, or strategic purpose.

**Usage:** Name the purpose and controlling authority. Avoid treating a reserve as uncommitted spending capacity.

### Committed

**Meaning:** An approved amount assigned to a specific transaction, agreement, deployment, or obligation but not necessarily transferred or released.

**Usage:** Identify the governing approval and distinguish commitment from execution.

### Vested

**Meaning:** The applicable time or service condition for a grant has been satisfied according to the governing vesting record.

**Usage:** Vesting does not necessarily equal release, transfer, unlock, or circulation.

### Unlocked

**Meaning:** A time, contract, vesting, or policy restriction has ended for the identified amount.

**Usage:** Distinguish technical unlock, policy release, custody movement, and circulation.

### Released

**Meaning:** The applicable release condition has been satisfied and the amount has moved or become available under the governing record.

**Usage:** State which authority and evidence establish release. Release does not necessarily equal circulation or market availability.

### Transferred

**Meaning:** Tokens have moved from one blockchain address or custody account to another.

**Usage:** A transfer does not by itself prove sale, distribution, circulation, beneficial-ownership change, or participant receipt.

### Custody transfer

**Meaning:** A change in the wallet, account, or party holding operational control of tokens.

**Usage:** Do not infer economic ownership or sale without supporting evidence.

### Circulating

**Meaning:** Tokens classified as available outside restricted allocation control under the approved reporting method.

**Usage:** State the report date and classification method. Transfers among controlled addresses may leave circulation unchanged.

### Controlled circulation

**Meaning:** The policy of moving tokens into broader use through recorded purpose, authority, release conditions, custody treatment, and reporting.

**Usage:** This term describes governance discipline. It does not imply control over price, demand, trading behavior, or resale.

Primary sources: [FUZE Token Allocation Table](02-FUZE_TOKEN_ALLOCATION_TABLE_PUBLIC.md), [FUZE Controlled Circulation Policy](12-FUZE_CONTROLLED_CIRCULATION_POLICY_PUBLIC.md), and [FUZE Token Release and Circulation Clarity](13-FUZE_TOKEN_RELEASE_AND_CIRCULATION_CLARITY_PUBLIC.md).

## 5. Wallet, Custody, and Eligibility Terms

### Wallet address

**Meaning:** A blockchain account identifier.

**Usage:** A wallet address is not necessarily a person, customer, or legal owner. It may represent an individual, institution, multisignature, exchange, contract, treasury, or omnibus account.

### Wallet record

**Meaning:** A record associated with a blockchain address, such as balance, category, status, event, eligibility result, or transaction reference.

**Usage:** A wallet record is not a public identity record. Personal, account, jurisdiction, and verification information remains permissioned or restricted.

### Wallet-level transparency

**Meaning:** Public or controlled visibility into relevant address-level records without publishing the person behind the address.

**Usage:** Publish only the fields needed for the reporting purpose. Address visibility is not consent to publish identity.

### Self-custody

**Meaning:** A custody arrangement in which the user controls the signing authority for the wallet.

**Usage:** A valid signature can demonstrate address control but does not by itself prove legal identity, jurisdiction, beneficial ownership, or eligibility.

### Exchange custody

**Meaning:** A custody arrangement in which an exchange controls the on-chain wallet and records customer balances internally.

**Usage:** Exchange trading, custody, deposits, withdrawals, snapshot support, and wallet participation are separate capabilities.

### Omnibus custody

**Meaning:** A structure in which one or more on-chain addresses represent balances belonging to multiple beneficial accounts.

**Usage:** Public chain data cannot allocate an omnibus balance among customers. User-level support requires approved custody evidence and reconciliation.

### Beneficial holder

**Meaning:** The person or entity with the economic interest in a position held through another controlling wallet or account.

**Usage:** Use only where beneficial ownership is relevant and supported by permissioned evidence.

### Eligible FUZE-holding wallet

**Meaning:** A wallet that holds FUZE token and satisfies the rules for a particular activated process.

**Usage:** Name the process, criteria, record time, custody treatment, exclusions, and status. Token holding alone is not universal eligibility.

### Eligibility

**Meaning:** A process-specific decision that required rules and evidence have been satisfied.

**Usage:** Eligibility for one process does not establish eligibility for another. Distinguish eligible, in review, restricted, ineligible, expired, paused, and corrected.

### Snapshot

**Meaning:** A time-specific record of wallet or custody data used for a defined process.

**Usage:** State the source, block or timestamp, included population, custody treatment, exclusions, and correction method.

### Claim

**Meaning:** A request submitted under an activated process by or for an eligible participant.

**Usage:** A claim exists only where submission, evidence, timing, review, and completion rules are active. Do not use “claim” for a general expectation.

### Correction

**Meaning:** A controlled update to a wallet, eligibility, snapshot, claim, allocation, or distribution record based on verified evidence.

**Usage:** Preserve audit history and protect personal information.

Primary sources: [FUZE Wallet-Based Privacy and Eligibility](26-FUZE_WALLET_BASED_PRIVACY_AND_ELIGIBILITY_PUBLIC.md) and [FUZE Exchange Custody and Wallet Participation](27-FUZE_EXCHANGE_CUSTODY_AND_WALLET_PARTICIPATION_PUBLIC.md).

## 6. Participation and Approved-Value Terms

### Wallet-based participation ability

**Meaning:** The controlled framework under which eligible FUZE-holding wallets may participate when the framework and all required gates are activated.

**Usage:** Describe it as a framework or ability until an approved active process establishes a more specific status.

### Activation gate

**Meaning:** A legal, technical, treasury, accounting, privacy, governance, custody, security, operational, or reporting condition that must be satisfied before a mechanism can operate.

**Usage:** “Activation-gated” means readiness depends on defined approvals and evidence. Design, implementation, review, or deployment alone is insufficient.

### Activated

**Meaning:** The identified mechanism has completed its required gates and is operating within the approved scope.

**Usage:** Name the exact process, effective time, population, and current conditions. Activation of one function does not activate the wider ecosystem.

### Product revenue pool

**Meaning:** A defined accounting scope used to assess product revenue under an approved process.

**Usage:** A revenue pool is a calculation and governance boundary. It does not itself establish distributable value.

### Approved distributable value

**Meaning:** A specifically approved amount available under an activated participation process after applicable accounting, reserve, governance, legal, technical, privacy, and operating review.

**Usage:** Keep it distinct from gross revenue, cash balance, stablecoin balance, treasury assets, token-sale proceeds, forecasts, and unapproved calculations.

### Approved participation amount

**Meaning:** The amount assigned to an eligible participant under the active method after applicable calculation and review.

**Usage:** Distinguish the amount from eligibility, claim submission, payment, and completed reconciliation.

### Completed distribution record

**Meaning:** Evidence that the approved action was completed and reconciled for the stated participant or aggregate process.

**Usage:** A transaction hash alone may be insufficient where custody or intermediary allocation is involved.

Primary sources: [FUZE Wallet-Based Participation Model](07-FUZE_WALLET_BASED_PARTICIPATION_MODEL_PUBLIC.md), [FUZE Participation Activation Gates](08-FUZE_PARTICIPATION_ACTIVATION_GATES_PUBLIC.md), and [FUZE Approved Distributable Value Model](09-FUZE_APPROVED_DISTRIBUTABLE_VALUE_MODEL_PUBLIC.md).

## 7. Treasury, Governance, and Technical Terms

### Treasury

**Meaning:** The controlled operating function and records used to manage approved assets, obligations, reserves, budgets, and movements.

**Usage:** Identify the asset, purpose, authority, and reporting scope. Treasury control does not make every balance publicly available or distributable.

### Governance

**Meaning:** The rules, authority, decisions, records, and controls used to direct an approved process.

**Usage:** State which process is governed and who has authority. Avoid implying that token ownership creates unrestricted governance power.

### Governance direction

**Meaning:** Input or preference that may inform an ecosystem decision where an approved process supports it.

**Usage:** Direction is not final corporate, legal, treasury, technical, or operational authority.

### Multisignature

**Meaning:** An authorization method requiring more than one approved signer for an action.

**Usage:** State the governed action and threshold where public and appropriate. Multisignature alone does not resolve legal, security, operational, or conflict risk.

### Timelock

**Meaning:** A programmed delay between an approved action and its execution.

**Usage:** Describe the affected action, delay, exception route, and monitoring where material.

### Administrative role

**Meaning:** A permission that can configure, pause, upgrade, transfer, approve, or otherwise affect a system.

**Usage:** Use the narrow role name where available. “Admin” should not conceal materially different powers.

### Pause

**Meaning:** A temporary controlled state that restricts identified functions while an issue, review, or transition is handled.

**Usage:** State the affected scope and responsible authority. A pause does not necessarily stop every system function.

### Smart-contract readiness

**Meaning:** The evidence state showing that a contract has completed the technical reviews required for its intended next stage.

**Usage:** Readiness and activation are separate. A reviewed, verified, or deployed contract may remain inactive pending other gates.

### Deployed

**Meaning:** Code or configuration has been placed in an identified environment or network.

**Usage:** Deployment does not establish activation, availability, eligibility, adoption, revenue, or legal approval.

### Verified contract

**Meaning:** A contract whose network, address, bytecode or source relationship, and publication reference have been checked under the stated method.

**Usage:** Verification does not guarantee security or active utility.

Primary sources: [FUZE Governance, Multisig, and Timelock Model](24-FUZE_GOVERNANCE_MULTISIG_TIMELOCK_MODEL_PUBLIC.md), [FUZE Smart Contract Readiness and Activation Gates](25-FUZE_SMART_CONTRACT_READINESS_AND_ACTIVATION_GATES_PUBLIC.md), and [FUZE Development Governance and Standards](31-FUZE_DEVELOPMENT_GOVERNANCE_AND_STANDARDS_PUBLIC.md).

## 8. Market-Access and Liquidity Terms

### Market access

**Meaning:** The practical ability to interact with FUZE token through an identified venue or route under current conditions.

**Usage:** Name the venue type, exact status, network, custody, jurisdiction limits, and current availability where approved.

### DEX-first

**Meaning:** FUZE's cautious direction in which decentralized exchange access may be considered before centralized exchange access.

**Usage:** Pair the term with verified status. Direction does not establish a pool, date, pair, depth, routing, or live access.

### CEX consideration

**Meaning:** Possible later evaluation of centralized exchange access.

**Usage:** Use “researching,” “in discussion,” “submitted,” “under review,” “approved,” “scheduled,” or “live” only when evidence supports that exact stage.

### Listing

**Meaning:** A venue's completed approval and live availability of the identified token or pair under that venue's rules.

**Usage:** Technical preparation, application, due diligence, integration, or third-party mention is not a listing.

### Pair

**Meaning:** The two assets available for exchange through an identified market or pool.

**Usage:** Name the venue, network, and status. A planned pair is not a live pair.

### Liquidity

**Meaning:** Available market capacity to execute transactions at a defined time, size, venue, spread, and price impact.

**Usage:** State the measurement context. Pool value, token balance, or reported volume alone is not a complete liquidity description.

### Market depth

**Meaning:** Available orders or pool capacity across price levels for a defined venue and time.

**Usage:** Depth changes and should be dated. It does not establish continuous execution quality.

### Spread

**Meaning:** The difference between relevant buy and sell prices under a stated venue and method.

**Usage:** State the observation time and method. Spread is not a guarantee of execution.

### Slippage

**Meaning:** The difference between an expected execution price and the resulting execution price for a specified transaction.

**Usage:** Identify the venue, size, route, and time where measured.

### Market-support operation

**Meaning:** An approved activity related to liquidity planning, execution, monitoring, reconciliation, or reporting.

**Usage:** Describe the exact operation and authority. Avoid wording that implies price protection, assured buyers, guaranteed liquidity, or market control.

### Price observation

**Meaning:** A recorded market price from an identified source and time.

**Usage:** An observation is not a valuation, promise, supported price, or forecast.

Primary sources: [FUZE Liquidity and Listing Policy](21-FUZE_LIQUIDITY_AND_LISTING_POLICY_PUBLIC.md), [FUZE Market Price and Demand Boundary](22-FUZE_MARKET_PRICE_AND_DEMAND_BOUNDARY_PUBLIC.md), and [FUZE Exchange and Market Access Boundary](../INVESTOR-PARTNER-PAPERS/19-FUZE_EXCHANGE_AND_MARKET_ACCESS_BOUNDARY_PUBLIC.md).

## 9. Funding and Investor Terms

### Community Participation Round

**Meaning:** The defined round structure for approved community, product-user, and contributor participation.

**Usage:** Use the complete name. State its current stage, access process, allocation source, jurisdiction treatment, and controls from the dedicated paper.

### Community Access and Participation Allocation

**Meaning:** The approved token allocation associated with the Community Participation Round and related access purposes.

**Usage:** Preserve the exact allocation name. Allocation does not establish that access is open or that an applicant qualifies.

### Private Strategic Seed Round

**Meaning:** A private fundraising process for selected strategic investors or partners under separately provided terms.

**Usage:** Public materials may describe purpose and strategic context. Terms, rights, eligibility, commitments, and closing evidence come from authorized private records.

### Investor interest

**Meaning:** An investor or potential investor has expressed interest or entered a discussion.

**Usage:** Interest is not a commitment, signed instrument, funded closing, partnership, or endorsement.

### Commitment in process

**Meaning:** A proposed investment is progressing through documentation, approval, transfer, or closing steps.

**Usage:** Do not describe it as funded or closed until the required evidence exists.

### Funded closing

**Meaning:** The required agreement and transfer conditions have been completed and reconciled for the stated investment.

**Usage:** State the exact scope. A signed agreement without completed funding is not a funded closing.

### Use of funds

**Meaning:** The approved intended application of financing proceeds.

**Usage:** Distinguish planned allocation from actual expenditure and reporting.

### Token-related exposure

**Meaning:** A defined economic, allocation, or participation relationship involving FUZE token under the relevant terms.

**Usage:** Explain the exact instrument and conditions in authorized materials. Do not imply guaranteed price, liquidity, listing, return, or exit.

Primary sources: [FUZE Community Participation Round](06-FUZE_COMMUNITY_PARTICIPATION_ROUND_PUBLIC.md) and [FUZE Seed Round and Token-Related Exposure](23-FUZE_SEED_ROUND_AND_TOKEN_RELATED_EXPOSURE_PUBLIC.md).

## 10. Privacy, Data, and Reporting Terms

### Public

**Meaning:** Approved for unrestricted public release.

**Usage:** Public blockchain visibility does not make enriched identity, account, support, or verification records public.

### Public-safe reporting

**Meaning:** Reporting prepared for public release after personal, restricted, security-sensitive, privileged, or commercially confidential information has been removed or withheld.

**Usage:** Identify the subject, period, method, status, and limitations.

### Permissioned

**Meaning:** Available only to approved roles for a defined purpose.

**Usage:** State the applicable access or review context where necessary.

### Restricted

**Meaning:** Subject to stronger access, handling, retention, and disclosure limits because of identity, legal, financial, security, or commercial sensitivity.

**Usage:** Do not reproduce restricted evidence in public explanations.

### Aggregated reporting

**Meaning:** Reporting that combines records into totals, categories, ranges, or trends.

**Usage:** Use aggregation when individual records add privacy exposure without improving verification.

### Pseudonymous reporting

**Meaning:** Reporting that uses wallet or account references without publishing personal identity.

**Usage:** Consider re-identification risk created by combining fields or external data.

### Authoritative record

**Meaning:** The approved source used to determine the status, value, authority, or outcome of a defined process.

**Usage:** Identify whether authority rests with accounting records, contract state, signed approval, custody evidence, release register, operating logs, or another controlled source.

### Evidence reference

**Meaning:** A traceable pointer to the source supporting a claim or status.

**Usage:** The reference should identify scope, date, environment, and limitations where material.

### Preliminary

**Meaning:** A result subject to reconciliation, review, approval, correction, or finalization.

**Usage:** Keep the label visible wherever the value or conclusion appears.

### Final

**Meaning:** A result completed and approved under the stated process and purpose.

**Usage:** Final applies to the identified scope and period. Later corrections should remain traceable.

### Reconciled

**Meaning:** Relevant source records have been matched and differences resolved or recorded under the stated method.

**Usage:** Name the records, period, and reviewer where material. Reconciliation does not automatically equal audit.

### Audited

**Meaning:** Reviewed under a defined audit engagement by an identified qualified party for a stated scope and period.

**Usage:** Do not use for internal checks, automated scans, code review, reconciliation, or informal consultant feedback.

## 11. Status Vocabulary

Use the narrowest status supported by current evidence.

| Status | Public meaning | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Public position | Approved explanation or principle | Current approved public paper |
| Concept | User problem, intended audience, and idea are identified | Concept brief or product paper |
| Research | Material questions are under investigation | Research record, interviews, comparison, or review notes |
| Design | Scope, workflow, controls, or architecture are defined | Specification, flow, data model, or architecture decision |
| Prototype | A partial implementation demonstrates named behavior | Working prototype or sandbox evidence |
| Internal testing | Authorized internal evaluation is underway | Test scope, participants, results, and defects |
| Limited release | A controlled user or partner group can access the scope | Access, terms, onboarding, support, and monitoring |
| Public beta | Public access exists with known limitations | Public route, version notes, feedback, and issue process |
| Live | The intended service is available under current operating terms | Production access, support, monitoring, and operating history |
| Activation-gated | Operation requires defined approvals or readiness conditions | Gate checklist and incomplete or completed evidence |
| Under review | Scope, evidence, treatment, or operation is being reassessed | Review owner and next-decision criteria |
| Paused | The identified function is temporarily unavailable or restricted | Pause notice, scope, owner, and review route |
| Closed | The defined round, claim, window, or process has ended | Closure record and final status |
| Retired or replaced | The feature, policy, or record is no longer current | Status notice and replacement reference |

Attach status to the exact product, module, contract, venue, round, process, or environment. One component can be live while another remains in design or activation-gated.

Do not use “live” merely to mean published, deployed, announced, demonstrated, or discussed.

Primary source: [FUZE Public Status and Roadmap Matrix](../PUBLIC-INDEX/02-FUZE_PUBLIC_STATUS_AND_ROADMAP_MATRIX.md).

## 12. Market and Transaction Stage Vocabulary

### Market-access stages

Use these states precisely:

1. **Researching** — assessing venue fit and requirements.
2. **Preparing** — documentation, technical, custody, or operating work is underway.
3. **In discussion** — communication with a venue has begun.
4. **Submitted** — required materials have been delivered.
5. **Under review** — the venue is evaluating the request or integration.
6. **Technically ready** — FUZE-side technical conditions are complete for the stated stage.
7. **Approved** — the venue has provided approval suitable for publication.
8. **Scheduled** — an approved activation time may be announced.
9. **Live** — verified market access is operational.
10. **Paused or ended** — the identified venue function is interrupted or closed.

### Corporate-transaction stages

Use these states precisely:

1. **Internal consideration** — no external process is established.
2. **Exploration** — strategic fit is being tested.
3. **Preliminary contact** — high-level information is exchanged.
4. **Structured evaluation** — scope, authority, and diligence are defined.
5. **Indicative proposal** — preliminary structure or economics are recorded.
6. **Due diligence** — material claims, assets, obligations, and risks are being validated.
7. **Definitive agreement** — binding terms exist, subject to stated conditions.
8. **Closing** — required transfers, consents, and actions are completed.
9. **Integration or transition** — post-closing changes are being implemented.

Discussion, interest, proposal, signing, closing, and integration are not interchangeable.

## 13. Claim Vocabulary

### Supports

Use when a product or process helps perform a task while outcomes depend on inputs, users, controls, or external conditions.

### Enables

Use when the system provides the practical ability to perform the stated action.

### Assists

Use for bounded support where a person remains responsible for review or decision-making.

### May

Use for a real conditional possibility. State the material condition where it affects interpretation.

### Can

Use only when the capability exists for the stated scope or when the sentence clearly describes a designed possibility rather than current availability.

### Intended

Use for approved purpose or direction that has not been presented as completed operation.

### Planned

Use when the item is included in current planning and still requires execution.

### Expected

Use only where a documented basis exists and the statement remains an expectation rather than a completed fact or guarantee.

### Target

Use for an objective with a defined measure and period. A target is not a forecast, commitment, service level, or guarantee unless separately established.

### Approved

Name the approving authority, venue, policy, or source when ambiguity matters.

### Verified

State what was verified, by whom or under which method, and the relevant date or scope.

### Eligible

Name the rules, population, record time, custody treatment, and process. Eligibility for one process must not be generalized.

### Available

Use only when the intended user can access the identified function under current conditions.

### Guaranteed

Use only for a narrowly defined obligation that FUZE is legally and operationally authorized to guarantee. Do not use for price, return, yield, profit, liquidity, listing, adoption, AI accuracy, security, game earnings, growth, or transaction outcomes.

## 14. AI, Automation, and Product-Boundary Terms

### AI-assisted

**Meaning:** AI contributes to a workflow while users, reviewers, or operators retain defined authority.

**Usage:** Identify the generated output, input source, permissions, review, and limitations where material.

### AI-generated

**Meaning:** Content or output produced by an AI model or tool.

**Usage:** Do not imply completeness, authority, originality, or correctness without review evidence.

### Human approval

**Meaning:** An authorized person reviews and approves an action or output before the relevant consequence occurs.

**Usage:** State the action requiring approval rather than using the phrase as a generic reassurance.

### Permission-controlled

**Meaning:** Access and actions follow defined roles, scopes, approvals, and logging.

**Usage:** Use for Botmad and other systems only where the relevant permission model is implemented or clearly described as design.

### Decision support

**Meaning:** Information, analysis, structure, or recommendations provided to support a human or authorized operational decision.

**Usage:** Do not present decision support as autonomous legal, financial, trading, treasury, employment, or governance authority.

### Market intelligence

**Meaning:** Organized market information, research, observations, and analytical context.

**Usage:** QTB provides market interpretation and research support; do not describe it as autonomous trading.

### Liquidity-operations intelligence

**Meaning:** Analysis, monitoring, planning, controls, and reporting for authorized market-operation workflows.

**Usage:** AIMM should not be described as price protection, autonomous trading, or guaranteed liquidity.

### Sponsored visibility

**Meaning:** Paid or otherwise sponsored placement presented to users with clear identification.

**Usage:** ToolGrid AI sponsored placement must remain distinguishable from neutral assessment, editorial judgment, or utility ranking.

### Game reward

**Meaning:** A value, point, item, access benefit, or other outcome defined by an active game system.

**Usage:** State the in-game or external status. Do not imply active real-value withdrawal, earnings, or token conversion without evidence and activation controls.

## 15. Legal, Compliance, Audit, and Security Terms

### Compliant

**Meaning:** Meeting identified requirements for a defined scope, jurisdiction, and time.

**Usage:** Avoid universal claims such as “fully compliant” unless current specialist evidence supports the exact wording.

### Licensed

**Meaning:** Holding a specific authorization issued by an identified authority for a stated activity and scope.

**Usage:** Name the entity, license, jurisdiction, and relevant limitation where publication is approved.

### Legal review

**Meaning:** Review by an authorized legal professional for a defined question, scope, and jurisdiction.

**Usage:** Legal review is not a universal legal conclusion or regulatory approval.

### Certified

**Meaning:** Formally certified under an identified standard, scope, assessor, and effective period.

**Usage:** Do not use for self-assessment, internal controls, framework inspiration, or preparation for certification.

### Standards-aligned

**Meaning:** Selected practices are informed by or mapped to a recognized framework.

**Usage:** Name the selected practices where useful. Alignment does not equal assessment, audit, or certification.

### Secure

**Meaning:** Protected by identified controls against defined threats within a stated scope.

**Usage:** Avoid absolute wording. State implemented controls and material limitations where relevant.

### Audited smart contract

**Meaning:** A specific contract version was reviewed under a defined audit engagement by an identified reviewer.

**Usage:** State contract, version, date, reviewer, scope, findings status, and limitations where approved. Audit does not guarantee absence of defects.

## 17. Sensitive Claim Routing

Route a statement for specialist review when it:

- publishes a supply, allocation, circulation, price, revenue, treasury, reserve, or approved-value figure;
- announces a token contract, wallet, vault, pool, pair, venue, listing, market date, or custody route;
- links token ownership to eligibility, payment, value, governance, claim, return, or transaction outcome;
- describes fundraising terms, commitments, investor identity, closing, valuation, or token-related exposure;
- describes legal, tax, regulatory, accounting, licensing, audit, certification, or compliance conclusions;
- names an investor, customer, partner, custodian, exchange, market maker, auditor, regulator, or service provider;
- exposes wallet or account information that could identify a person;
- presents AI, security, game, market, product, growth, or business outcomes as certain;
- describes an acquisition, merger, exit, sale, or liquidity event;
- changes the public status of a product, contract, round, market route, or participation mechanism.

The [FUZE Legal and Compliance Messaging](28-FUZE_LEGAL_AND_COMPLIANCE_MESSAGING_PUBLIC.md) paper governs claim review. Token-specific risks are maintained in [FUZE Token Risk Boundaries](29-FUZE_TOKEN_RISK_BOUNDARIES_PUBLIC.md).

## 18. Editorial Rules

1. Lead with the subject, reader, and practical purpose.
2. Use a definition only where it helps the paper's objective.
3. Prefer a contextual sentence over a copied master paragraph.
4. Match status to the exact scope and current evidence.
5. Keep FUZE, FUZE token, Platform Credits, stablecoins, product revenue, treasury, and approved distributable value distinct.
6. Keep allocation, commitment, vesting, unlock, release, transfer, and circulation distinct.
7. Keep deployment, verification, technical readiness, activation, availability, usage, and revenue distinct.
8. Keep wallet records, beneficial ownership, eligibility, claims, and completed actions distinct.
9. Keep discussion, application, approval, listing, and live market access distinct.
10. Keep investor interest, commitment, funded closing, product revenue, and customer adoption distinct.
11. Protect personal identity, confidential terms, restricted records, credentials, private keys, and security-sensitive detail.
12. Route detailed mechanics to the controlling specialist paper.
13. Verify every internal link, title, path, status, number, date, and external name.
14. Remove duplicated introductions, generic disclaimer blocks, and repeated master-position language.
15. Recheck published language whenever the underlying status, evidence, mechanism, or relationship changes.

## Key Takeaways

- FUZE is the product ecosystem; FUZE token is its ecosystem token.
- Platform Credits, FUZE token, stablecoins, product revenue, treasury assets, and approved distributable value are separate concepts.
- Allocation, release, circulation, deployment, activation, eligibility, claims, and completion require distinct language.
