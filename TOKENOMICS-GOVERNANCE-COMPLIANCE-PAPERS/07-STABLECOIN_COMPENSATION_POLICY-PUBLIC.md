# Stablecoin Compensation Policy

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses the **Stablecoin Compensation Policy** to separate work payment from long-term token alignment.

FUZE compensates work through USDT, USDC, fiat-equivalent payments, milestone payments, product revenue, approved treasury operations, or approved Public Vault Access proceeds where applicable. FUZE token allocations remain focused on long-term ecosystem alignment, contribution recognition, governance readiness, product utility, and approved ecosystem participation.

The policy supports FUZE’s Controlled Circulation Policy:

**Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation. Platform Credits power product usage.**

This separation reduces forced token selling, improves contributor sustainability, supports investor confidence, strengthens community trust, and keeps FUZE tokenomics aligned with a product-first AI SaaS platform.

Stablecoin compensation does not mean token-holder payout, profit sharing, investment-yield framing, market return, investment return, or guaranteed income. It is a work-payment policy for team members, advisors, contributors, contractors, service providers, selected operators, and selected partners where approved.

This paper defines the stablecoin compensation model, its relationship to FUZE token allocations, Platform Credits, controlled circulation, treasury operations, product revenue, Public Vault Access proceeds, contributor alignment, reporting, and risk safeguards.

**FUZE compensates work through USDT, USDC, fiat-equivalent payments, milestone payments, product revenue, or approved treasury operations. FUZE token allocations are used primarily for long-term alignment, contribution recognition, ecosystem participation, governance readiness, and product utility.**

This policy helps prevent FUZE token allocations from becoming short-term cash compensation pressure.

## 6. Risk Boundaries and Safeguards

FUZE applies risk boundaries around stablecoin compensation.

### 6.1 Stablecoin Compensation Boundary

Stablecoin compensation is for approved work payment, contractor payment, contributor payment, advisor payment, vendor payment, service payment, or partner deliverable payment where applicable. It is not token-holder income, investment return, profit participation, investment-yield framing, or market-performance payment.

### 6.2 General Token Boundary

FUZE does not guarantee token price, liquidity, listing, profit, return, payout, or market performance.

### 6.3 Platform Credits Boundary

Platform Credits are for product usage and are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

### 6.4 Profit Participation Boundary

Profit participation is a long-term design direction and is not immediate or guaranteed. Any future framework requires legal, accounting, treasury, technical, and transparency readiness.

### 6.5 Tax and Accounting Boundary

Stablecoin compensation can create accounting, tax, payroll, contractor, vendor, reporting, and jurisdiction-specific obligations. Treatment depends on final legal, accounting, tax, and jurisdiction review.

### 6.6 Payment Rail Boundary

Stablecoin, fiat-equivalent, bank, app-store, exchange, wallet, or payment provider rails can have restrictions, delays, failures, fees, compliance requirements, account limits, or geographic constraints.

### 6.7 Treasury Boundary

Compensation funded by treasury operations, product revenue, Public Vault Access proceeds, partner payments, or strategic funding remains subject to budget approval, treasury controls, accounting review, and policy rules.

### 6.8 Public Reporting Boundary

FUZE can report compensation policy and high-level categories while protecting private personal data, individual compensation terms, contracts, tax records, and sensitive business information.

### 6.9 Development Standards Boundary

FUZE uses standards-inspired or standards-aligned processes. FUZE does not claim formal certification unless certification has been completed.
