# FUZE Seed Round and Token-Related Exposure

## Executive Summary

FUZE Seed Round and Token-Related Exposure explains how FUZE communicates private strategic fundraising, token-related exposure, product-first execution, investor boundaries, and public no-guarantee language.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Seed round discussions are private strategic fundraising conversations. They are not a public token sale, not a public investment offer, not a public solicitation, and not a public promise of token price, listing, liquidity, payout, income, yield, profit, exit, or investment return.

Token-related exposure, if discussed in private investor context, must be separated from public token marketing. It may relate to strategic alignment, future ecosystem participation, token utility context, controlled circulation, wallet-based participation boundaries, and long-term platform direction where legally and contractually reviewed.

FUZE does not describe seed round participation as guaranteed token profit.

FUZE does not describe FUZE token as guaranteed investment return.

FUZE does not describe wallet-based participation ability as active public payout rights.

FUZE does not describe product revenue as automatic holder payout.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token. Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails. Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token, not automatic for every wallet, and not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

This paper gives investors and the community a clear boundary between private strategic fundraising and public token communication.
