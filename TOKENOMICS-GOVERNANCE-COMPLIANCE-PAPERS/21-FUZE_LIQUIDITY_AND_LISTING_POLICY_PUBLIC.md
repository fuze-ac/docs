# FUZE Liquidity and Listing Policy

## Executive Summary

FUZE treats liquidity, trading access, custody integration, venue onboarding, and market operations as controlled market-structure work rather than automatic consequences of token issuance.

The approved **Liquidity & Market Operations** allocation contains **30,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **6.00%** of the fixed **500,000,000 FUZE** supply.

This allocation is controlled operating capacity for approved:

- decentralized exchange liquidity;
- market-making inventory;
- venue onboarding and custody preparation;
- exchange or custodian deposits;
- routing and market-access support;
- liquidity-provider arrangements;
- market-structure testing;
- withdrawal, return, and recovery;
- and related market-operation purposes.

Holding FUZE inside this allocation does not establish:

- a committed deployment;
- an active pool;
- a market-maker mandate;
- a venue application;
- a venue approval;
- a listing;
- deposits or withdrawals;
- live trading;
- usable liquidity;
- market depth;
- a narrow spread;
- trading volume;
- token demand;
- price support;
- income;
- revenue share;
- or financial return.

The public direction is **DEX-first where ready**.

A decentralized exchange deployment can proceed only when its token, contract, pool, paired-asset, custody, treasury, security, technical, legal, operational, monitoring, reconciliation, withdrawal, and public-reporting conditions are approved and ready.

Centralized exchange access may be evaluated later.

The following remain distinct states:

```text
research
-> preparation
-> confidential discussion
-> application or submission
-> venue review
-> technical integration
-> venue approval
-> scheduling
-> deposit and withdrawal activation
-> trading activation
-> monitored live operation
-> pause, withdrawal, delisting, termination, or archive
```

A discussion is not an application.

An application is not an approval.

An approval is not a listing until the venue-approved market is live and verified.

A token deposit is not proof that trading is live.

A live pair does not guarantee usable liquidity, depth, volume, demand, price, or continued venue support.

Token inventory and pairing capital are separate.

Deploying FUZE to a pool, venue, custodian, or market maker requires an approved release from the Liquidity & Market Operations allocation.

Stablecoins, ETH, native assets, fiat, collateral, venue fees, service fees, gas, and other market-operation resources require their own:

- treasury source;
- budget;
- custody;
- authority;
- accounting treatment;
- risk controls;
- reconciliation;
- and return treatment.

Platform Credits remain product-consumption units and cannot be used as token-market inventory or pairing capital.

The controlling lifecycle is:

```text
approved market-structure purpose
-> venue, protocol, pool, provider, custodian, or market-maker assessment
-> complete deployment or onboarding proposal
-> readiness and specialist reviews
-> source-allocation and pairing-capital approval
-> custody and authority setup
-> technical testing and security verification
-> final pre-deployment review
-> authorized transfer, pool creation, deposit, integration, or provider funding
-> public verification only after evidence is complete
-> continuous monitoring, reconciliation, and market-integrity review
-> rebalancing, additional deployment, pause, withdrawal, recovery, delisting, or termination
-> final reconciliation, public-safe reporting, closure, and archive
```

Every material deployment should identify:

- stable deployment or venue identifier;
- purpose;
- current status;
- venue, protocol, pool, network, custodian, provider, or market maker;
- FUZE source allocation and source vault;
- FUZE amount;
- paired asset and treasury source;
- parameters and limits;
- custody and beneficial-control treatment;
- authority and signer model;
- monitoring and alerting;
- market-integrity controls;
- fees and cost treatment;
- withdrawal and recovery rights;
- incident and suspension rules;
- allocation, vault, treasury, custody, and circulation effects;
- public-reporting treatment;
- current-as-of date;
- and closure conditions.

This paper owns the market-structure, liquidity, venue, provider, custody, monitoring, integrity, incident, withdrawal, delisting, reporting, and archive framework.

Allocation amounts and mandates remain governed by [FUZE Token Allocation Table](02-FUZE_TOKEN_ALLOCATION_TABLE_PUBLIC.md).

Source-vault release authority remains governed by [FUZE Vault-by-Vault Release Rules](15-FUZE_VAULT_BY_VAULT_RELEASE_RULES_PUBLIC.md).

Market-price and demand communication remains governed by [FUZE Market Price and Demand Boundary](22-FUZE_MARKET_PRICE_AND_DEMAND_BOUNDARY_PUBLIC.md).

## Public Position

FUZE should communicate market-structure status using exact evidence-backed states.

FUZE should not state or imply that a token is listed, liquid, supported, or price-stabilized merely because:

- the token contract exists;
- a DEX pool was drafted;
- a pool address exists;
- a venue wallet exists;
- a deposit was sent;
- a market maker was contacted;
- a venue requested documents;
- due diligence is underway;
- technical integration is underway;
- a venue approved confidentially but has not authorized publication;
- an interface displays an unofficial pair;
- an aggregator found a route;
- or a community member created a market.

Public status should distinguish:

- official and unofficial pools;
- approved and unapproved venues;
- project-controlled and third-party liquidity;
- committed and deployed inventory;
- deposits and withdrawals;
- scheduled and live trading;
- venue-reported and independently verified status;
- and current versus archived support.

FUZE cannot guarantee a third party's:

- venue decision;
- onboarding time;
- continued support;
- custody availability;
- deposit or withdrawal uptime;
- order-book quality;
- trading volume;
- market demand;
- or delisting decision.

## Asset and Ledger Separation

Market operations can involve several distinct asset, custody, and accounting records.

| Component | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| FUZE inventory | Drawn from the approved Liquidity & Market Operations allocation |
| Stablecoin pairing capital | Separately budgeted and approved treasury asset |
| ETH or native-asset pairing capital | Separately budgeted with network, custody, and volatility treatment |
| Fiat or provider collateral | Separately approved, custodied, and reconciled |
| Venue, listing, integration, or service fees | Separately approved treasury expense or deducted asset |
| Gas and network fees | Separately tracked operating cost |
| Market-maker inventory | Governed by an approved agreement, custody, limits, and return rights |
| Liquidity-provider position | Governed by pool, position, fee, range, custody, and withdrawal records |
| Fee income | Separately classified and reconciled; not automatically distributable value |
| Withdrawn or recovered assets | Returned to the correct liquidity, treasury, reserve, or recovery custody |
| Platform Credits | Product-consumption units; never token-market inventory or pairing capital |

### No Implied Pairing Capacity

A visible FUZE allocation balance provides no evidence that matching stablecoin, ETH, native asset, fiat, or collateral capacity exists.

Reports should identify token-side and pairing-side amounts separately.

### Valuation Boundary

Different assets should not be combined into one value without an approved:

- valuation source;
- measurement time;
- conversion method;
- fee treatment;
- and limitation statement.

### Approved Distributable Value Boundary

Pool fees, provider rebates, exchange rebates, or market-operation income are not automatically approved distributable value.

They require separate recognition, expense, reserve, accounting, and approval treatment.

## Key Takeaways

- FUZE reserves 30,000,000 FUZE, or 6.00% of fixed supply, for controlled liquidity and market operations.
- The allocation is operating capacity, not proof of deployment, listing, circulation, liquidity, or price support.
- FUZE's public direction is DEX-first where ready, with possible later CEX evaluation under exact evidence-backed status language.
- Research, discussion, application, review, technical readiness, approval, scheduling, deposits, withdrawals, trading, pause, delisting, and closure are separate states.
- FUZE inventory, pairing capital, collateral, fees, gas, provider funds, and Platform Credits require separate treatment and should not be combined without an approved method.
- Every venue, pool, custodian, provider, and market maker requires its own due diligence, proposal, custody, authority, monitoring, integrity, and exit controls.
- A permissionless pool can exist without official FUZE support; public records should distinguish official and unofficial markets.
- AIMM can support authorized liquidity-operation intelligence and QTB can support market research, but neither creates autonomous authority or guaranteed outcomes.
- Market-integrity controls should prohibit wash trading, spoofing, deceptive orders, artificial volume, false liquidity, undisclosed price support, and misleading venue statements.
- Rebalancing, additional deployment, venue changes, and provider expansion require new or renewed authority and available asset capacity.
- Incidents, withdrawals, delisting, provider termination, asset returns, and final closure should preserve custody evidence and complete reconciliation.
- A pool, venue approval, listing, market maker, liquidity deployment, or live pair does not guarantee continuous access, liquidity, depth, spread, volume, demand, price support, income, revenue share, or financial return.
