# FUZE Vault and Reserve Policy

## Executive Summary

FUZE Vault and Reserve Policy explains how FUZE treats vault separation, reserve categories, treasury controls, public reporting, and the boundary between visible balances and approved distributable value.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Vaults and reserves are control structures. They help FUZE separate token allocations, treasury assets, stablecoin flows, product revenue records, operating funds, ecosystem reserves, liquidity-related resources, team or partner allocations, and possible future participation-related records where applicable.

A vault balance is not automatically approved distributable value.

A reserve balance is not automatically payout supply.

A treasury wallet is not automatic public distribution value.

A public vault view does not give unrestricted public control over FUZE operations, treasury, private agreements, investor records, contributor records, or confidential business information.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token. Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails. Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token, not automatic for every wallet, and not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

Vault and reserve policy supports transparency, separation, and discipline. It does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, or investment return.

## 2. FUZE Vault and Reserve Position

FUZE’s public position is:

Vaults and reserves are used to separate purposes, strengthen controls, improve public understanding, and support reporting discipline.

Vault and reserve policy has five core ideas.

| Idea | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Purpose separation | Assets should be organized by role and use where practical. |
| Treasury discipline | Sensitive movements should use review, approvals, and controls. |
| Public-safe visibility | Some vault activity may be visible without exposing private identity or confidential records. |
| Reserve protection | Reserves protect operations, obligations, risk coverage, and future platform stability. |
| No automatic payout | Vault balances and reserves are not automatically approved distributable value. |

Vaults and reserves help FUZE avoid mixing product usage, token circulation, operating treasury, stablecoin payments, team allocations, ecosystem reserves, and future participation-related records.

The policy is designed to make the system clearer, not to promise financial outcomes.

## 3. What a Vault Means in FUZE

A vault is a wallet, contract, account, ledger category, or controlled record used to separate assets or records by purpose.

A vault may hold or track:

- FUZE token
- stablecoins
- product revenue records
- reserve balances
- treasury balances
- ecosystem allocation
- team allocation
- advisor allocation
- partner allocation
- liquidity-related resources
- community allocation
- game utility allocation where defined
- report hashes
- conversion proof records
- audit records
- eligibility records where applicable
- claim-related records only if an activated framework requires them

A vault should have a clear purpose.

A public vault label should help readers understand why the vault exists.

Boundary:

A vault is a control and record structure. It is not automatically a payout pool, approved distributable value pool, liquidity guarantee, or public treasury access right.

Reserves can protect the platform from operational risk, support long-term planning, and prevent premature distribution or release.

Possible reserve categories include:

- operating reserve
- treasury reserve
- tax reserve

- legal and compliance reserve

- infrastructure reserve

- incident response reserve
- stablecoin reserve
- token reserve
- liquidity-related reserve
- ecosystem reserve
- community reserve
- partner reserve
- game operations reserve
- future participation readiness reserve where appropriate

A reserve can be important even when it is not immediately used.

Boundary:

A reserve is not approved distributable value unless a defined policy later releases it through proper review and approval.

## 5. Why Vault Separation Matters

Vault separation matters because FUZE includes products, Platform Credits, stablecoin flows, token allocations, ecosystem reserves, wallet records, possible participation readiness, and public reporting.

Without vault separation, public readers may confuse:

- treasury assets with approved distributable value
- stablecoin payments with participation distributions
- token allocations with circulating supply
- locked tokens with available tokens
- reserves with payout pools
- product revenue with gross receipts
- game values with real-world distributable value
- liquidity-related wallets with price support promises
- team allocation with community allocation
- Platform Credit records with token rights

Vault separation reduces this confusion.

It also helps FUZE explain token movement, product revenue records, reserves, circulation, and public reporting with more discipline.

Boundary:

Vault separation improves clarity, but it does not guarantee product success, revenue, token price, liquidity, payout, or investment return.

## 18. Vaults and Approved Distributable Value

Vault balances and approved distributable value are different.

A vault may hold assets for many purposes, including treasury, reserves, operations, ecosystem development, product utility, team vesting, partner allocation, or liquidity-related operations.

Approved distributable value is a reviewed and approved amount that may be considered for eligible-wallet participation only if the framework becomes active.

A vault balance may be excluded from approved distributable value because of:

- reserve policy
- operating needs
- tax obligations
- refund obligations
- chargeback risk
- legal restrictions
- audit issues
- jurisdiction limits
- product revenue pool rules
- treasury policy
- lockup or vesting restrictions
- liquidity-related use
- team or partner allocation purpose
- incomplete reconciliation
- private agreement terms
- activation gate status

Boundary:

A visible vault balance should not be described as approved distributable value unless the required process confirms it.

## 36. Vault and Reserve Development Direction

FUZE vault and reserve policy can develop through phases.

### Phase 1: Define Vault Categories

Define treasury, reserve, ecosystem, product utility, team, advisor, partner, community, liquidity-related, stablecoin, product revenue record, report hash, and distribution vault categories.

### Phase 2: Label Vault Purposes

Assign public-safe vault labels and explain each vault’s purpose.

### Phase 3: Establish Reserve Categories

Define operating, tax, refund, chargeback, legal, audit, security, AI cost, infrastructure, treasury stability, incident response, and ecosystem reserves.

### Phase 4: Add Treasury Controls

Use multisig, timelock, role separation, approval workflows, emergency controls, and transaction records where appropriate.

### Phase 5: Build Reporting Rails

Publish public-safe vault and reserve reports, report hashes, movement summaries, and status updates where appropriate.

### Phase 6: Separate Product Revenue Records

Separate product revenue records from treasury balances, reserves, Platform Credit records, and approved distributable value.

### Phase 7: Prepare Participation Readiness Rails

Prepare eligibility, snapshot, reporting, and distribution vault concepts only as readiness infrastructure until activation gates are complete.

### Phase 8: Maintain Public Boundary Review

Continue reviewing language so vaults and reserves are not described as guaranteed payouts, income, yield, liquidity, price support, or investment returns.

These phases are development directions, not guaranteed launch dates.
