# FUZE FPPU Profit Participation Unit
## Executive Summary
FUZE defines **FPPU — FUZE Profit Participation Unit** as a separate profit participation instrument connected to approved distributable net profit from the FUZE platform.
FUZE is a transparency-first AI SaaS platform building practical products on shared infrastructure for identity, Platform Credits, payments, AI orchestration, reporting, privacy, security, governance, and ecosystem participation.
FUZE execution order is:
**Product usage first. Platform rails second. Broader ecosystem participation after that.**
FUZE separates:
| System | Public Role |
|---|---|
| Platform Credits | Product usage |
| FUZE token | Ecosystem utility, ZAGA participation, wallet-aware access, community participation, and governance-aware direction where applicable |
| FPPU | Eligible profit participation unit connected to approved distributable net profit |
| USDT | Distribution currency for approved FPPU distributions and one supported product-payment rail where applicable |
| Stablecoins / fiat / app-store proceeds | Work payment, revenue collection, treasury, or conversion sources depending on the payment channel |
FPPU is designed to support a Web3-native distribution model:
**FUZE products generate revenue → revenue is reconciled → costs, reserves, refunds, fees, taxes, and operating needs are deducted → approved distributable net profit is calculated → AI-assisted audit reviews the report → community audit reviews the public-safe records → multisig and timelock controls approve the distribution → USDT is deposited into the FPPU Distribution Smart Contract → eligible FPPU holders at the record-date snapshot can connect their wallet and claim their proportional USDT share.**
FPPU does not guarantee quarterly income, yearly income, fixed yield, dividend amount, passive income, payout amount, distribution frequency, token price, liquidity, resale opportunity, or exit.
This paper defines the public model for FPPU before FUZE updates existing tokenomics, investor, legal, roadmap, and product papers.
## 1. FUZE Position
FUZE position:
**FUZE uses FPPU as a separate profit participation unit for eligible holders, while keeping FUZE token as the ecosystem utility layer and Platform Credits as the product usage layer.**
FPPU is not designed as a normal unrestricted utility token. FPPU is a profit participation unit and therefore uses eligibility, reporting, audit, treasury, smart contract, and transfer-control logic.
FUZE uses FPPU to create a transparent relationship between platform performance and eligible holder participation, while keeping the following boundaries clear:
| Boundary | FUZE Position |
|---|---|
| No automatic payout | FPPU holders receive distributions only when approved distributable net profit exists and the process is completed |
| No fixed yield | FPPU does not promise fixed APY, dividend rate, or fixed income |
| No guaranteed frequency | Quarterly review does not mean quarterly payout |
| No unlimited access | FPPU may require eligibility, verification, wallet rules, jurisdiction controls, and transfer restrictions |
| No one-person control | Revenue-to-distribution movement uses reconciliation, AI-assisted audit, community audit, multisig, and timelock controls |
| No platform-money shortcut | Revenue does not become distribution until costs, reserves, refunds, taxes, and operating needs are handled |
| No confusion with Credits | Platform Credits are product usage credits, not profit participation units |
| No confusion with FUZE token | FUZE token is the ecosystem utility and participation layer; FPPU is the profit participation unit |
FUZE’s public-company-inspired principle is transparency, reporting discipline, record dates, approved distribution, and holder communication. FUZE applies this idea in a Web3-native structure through smart contracts, vaults, dashboards, AI-assisted audit, community audit, and wallet-based claims.

## 3. Public Model
### 3.1 What FPPU Is
FPPU means **FUZE Profit Participation Unit**.
FPPU is designed as an eligible participation unit connected to approved distributable net profit.
| FPPU Element | Public Meaning |
|---|---|
| Name | FUZE Profit Participation Unit |
| Short name | FPPU |
| Main role | Profit participation unit for eligible holders |
| Distribution currency | USDT |
| Distribution source | Approved distributable net profit from FUZE platform activity |
| Claim method | Eligible holders connect wallet and claim through smart contract |
| Snapshot logic | Eligible holders at the record-date snapshot participate in that distribution period |
| Audit logic | AI-assisted audit and community audit review the distribution report before final approval |
| Control logic | Multisig, timelock, vaults, and public-safe reporting support the process |
| Public boundary | No guaranteed payout, amount, frequency, liquidity, resale, token price, or exit |
### 3.2 What FPPU Is Not
FPPU is not a simple product credit and not an unrestricted utility token.
| FPPU Is Not | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not Platform Credits | FPPU is not used to consume AI actions, reports, dashboards, shop tools, game actions, or product workflows |
| Not normal FUZE utility token | FPPU is separate from the FUZE ecosystem utility token role |
| Not fixed yield | FPPU does not promise APY, fixed income, passive income, or guaranteed dividend |
| Not automatic revenue share | Revenue requires reconciliation, deductions, reserves, audit, and approval before any distribution |
| Not unrestricted transfer by default | FPPU can use eligibility rules, transfer restrictions, jurisdiction controls, and verified wallet rules |
| Not guaranteed liquidity | FPPU does not guarantee buyer availability, resale path, listing, market access, or exit |
| Not legal avoidance | FPPU may require legal, tax, eligibility, compliance, and reporting structure where applicable |
### 3.3 FUZE Token, FPPU, Platform Credits, and USDT
FUZE uses separate layers.
| Layer | Role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Platform Credits | Product usage | Not FUZE token, not FPPU, not equity, not investment contract, not payout asset |
| FUZE token | Ecosystem utility and participation | Supports access, ZAGA utility, wallet-aware participation, governance-aware direction where applicable, and long-term alignment |

This separation prevents confusion between product usage, ecosystem utility, work payment, revenue collection, and profit participation.
### 3.4 FPPU Distribution Logic
FPPU distribution follows a controlled process.
```text
1. FUZE products generate revenue
2. Revenue sources are tagged and reconciled
3. Refunds, chargebacks, payment fees, taxes, operating costs, AI costs, infrastructure costs, team/vendor costs, legal/compliance costs, security/audit costs, product reinvestment, and reserves are deducted
4. Approved distributable net profit is calculated
5. FPPU distribution percentage is applied
6. AI-assisted audit reviews revenue, costs, vault flows, conversion records, and distribution calculations
7. Community audit window opens for public-safe review
8. Corrections or disputes are resolved
9. Multisig approves the distribution
10. Timelock gives time for final review
11. USDT is deposited into the FPPU Distribution Smart Contract
12. Record-date snapshot identifies eligible holders
13. Eligible holders connect wallet and claim proportional USDT

3.5 Distributable Net Profit

FUZE does not treat gross revenue as distributable profit.

A public-safe calculation model:

Gross Platform Revenue
- Refunds / Chargebacks
- Payment Processing Fees
- Platform Fees
- AI Model Costs
- Hosting / Infrastructure Costs
- Team / Contractor / Vendor Costs
- Product Development Costs
- Marketing / Sales / Support Costs
- Legal / Compliance / Accounting Costs
- Security / Audit Costs
- Tax Reserve
- Refund / Chargeback Reserve
- Product Development Reserve
- Treasury Reserve
= Approved Distributable Net Profit

Then:

FPPU Distribution Pool = Approved Distributable Net Profit × Approved FPPU Distribution Percentage

The approved percentage can be defined by policy and can be adjusted through the governance and reporting process. FUZE does not guarantee that any period will have approved distributable net profit.

FPPU uses record and claim concepts similar to public-company dividend processes, adapted to Web3.

9. Conclusion

FPPU gives FUZE a clearer and more transparent way to describe profit participation without confusing it with normal product credits or ecosystem utility.

The FUZE model becomes:

Platform Credits are for product usage. FUZE token is for ecosystem utility and participation. FPPU is for eligible profit participation. USDT is used for approved FPPU distributions.

The FPPU model is Web3-native because it uses smart contracts, vaults, snapshots, wallet claims, public dashboards, AI-assisted audit, community audit, audit bounties, multisig approval, timelock controls, and on-chain distribution records.

The model also remains risk-aware because it does not promise a fixed distribution amount, fixed distribution frequency, fixed yield, passive income, liquidity, listing, resale opportunity, token price appreciation, or exit.

FPPU creates a bridge between FUZE platform performance and eligible holder participation only when approved distributable net profit exists and the complete audit, reserve, approval, and smart contract funding process is completed.
