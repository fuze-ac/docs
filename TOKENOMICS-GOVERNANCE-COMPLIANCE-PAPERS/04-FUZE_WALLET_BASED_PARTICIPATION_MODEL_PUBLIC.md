# FUZE Wallet-Based Participation Model

## Executive Summary

FUZE Wallet-Based Participation Model explains the public model for wallet-based participation ability inside the FUZE one-token system.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Wallet-based participation ability is a possible FUZE token-related framework where eligible FUZE-holding wallets may participate in approved distributable value from defined FUZE product revenue pools if the framework becomes active.

This ability is not a second token. It is not a public FPPU token. It is not automatic for every wallet. It is not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

FUZE avoids public FPPU language because it can confuse investors, community members, product users, and partners. The public model uses wallet-based participation ability, eligible FUZE-holding wallet, approved distributable value, activation gates, wallet-level transparency, and no public identity exposure.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token. Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails. Product revenue, if generated, requires reconciliation and does not automatically become approved distributable value.

Wallet-based participation ability may activate only after legal, accounting, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, eligibility, privacy, and jurisdiction gates are ready.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, or investment return.

## 11. Activation Gates

Wallet-based participation ability requires activation gates.

Activation gates protect FUZE, users, community members, investors, contributors, and product customers.

Required gates may include:

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Legal gate | Legal status, public wording, eligibility rules, and jurisdiction review are ready. |
| Accounting gate | Revenue, cost, reserve, and approved distributable value policy are defined. |
| Treasury gate | Vaults, reserves, controls, approvals, and payment rails are ready. |
| Audit gate | AI audit, internal review, community review where applicable, and dispute windows are ready. |
| Reporting gate | Public-safe reports, report hashes, records, and dashboard direction are ready. |
| Smart-contract gate | Contracts, registries, vaults, snapshots, claim modules, and security review are ready where applicable. |
| Eligibility gate | Eligible wallet rules, verification requirements, custody treatment, and transfer rules are defined. |
| Jurisdiction gate | Country, residency, securities, tax, exchange, custody, and compliance considerations are reviewed. |
| Privacy gate | Private identity review stays off-chain, permissioned, and separated from public wallet records. |
| Operator gate | No single person can approve or move sensitive funds alone. |

No live participation claim logic should activate before required gates are ready.

## 16. Self-Custody Wallets and Exchange Custody

Wallet-based participation must treat custody carefully.

A self-custody wallet is controlled directly by the wallet holder.

An exchange custody wallet may be controlled by an exchange or platform. Users may have exchange account balances, but the public blockchain may show the exchange’s omnibus wallet rather than each user’s personal wallet.

This creates questions such as:

- Can the beneficial holder be identified?
- Can the exchange support snapshots?
- Can the exchange support eligibility records?
- Can the exchange support claim distribution?
- Can the exchange provide required records?
- Can transfer restrictions be applied if needed?
- Can jurisdiction rules be handled?
- Can private verification be completed where required?

FUZE should not imply that exchange-held tokens automatically receive all wallet-based participation features.

Boundary:

Holding FUZE through an exchange does not guarantee eligibility, claim support, liquidity, exit, or payout.

Wallet-based participation may develop through phases.

Use wallet-based participation ability as public language and avoid public FPPU wording.

Define wallet-level transparency and off-chain private verification rules where required.

Define which product revenue pools may be considered and which values are excluded.

### Phase 6: Smart Contract Readiness

Build transparency and compatibility rails such as vault records, report hash registry, multisig, timelock, eligibility registry, snapshots, and claim modules where appropriate.

Define eligible wallet rules, exchange custody treatment, OTC transfer treatment, jurisdiction rules, and verification requirements.

Activate claim logic only if all legal, accounting, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, privacy, eligibility, and jurisdiction gates are ready.
