# FUZE Controlled Circulation Policy

## Executive Summary

FUZE Controlled Circulation Policy explains how FUZE governs token release, token movement, allocation vaults, reserve categories, vesting, lockups, community access, migration claims, ecosystem incentives, liquidity-related deployment, treasury controls, and public circulation reporting.

FUZE uses one ecosystem token only: **FUZE token**.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, community participation structures, and wallet-based participation ability where activated under required controls.

Controlled circulation means FUZE token movement is governed by allocation purpose, product readiness, eligibility, vesting, lockups, treasury policy, market-structure policy, governance controls, vault separation, release logic, and public reporting direction.

The purpose of controlled circulation is to reduce public confusion, avoid uncontrolled supply shocks where possible, separate allocation purposes, support public reporting, and help readers understand what is circulating, what is locked, what is reserved, what is vested, what is released, what is claimable, what is operationally deployed, and what remains under control.

FUZE uses a fixed total supply of **500,000,000 FUZE** across ten purpose-specific allocation categories.

Each allocation category has a different public role and release logic.

Community Participation Allocation does not behave like Team Allocation.

BOARD / Surfboard Migration does not behave like Liquidity & Market Operations.

Treasury Reserve does not behave like Holder Incentives.

Transparency / Stability Reserve does not behave like normal circulating inventory.

FUZE separates:

**Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation. Platform Credits power product usage.**

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

Wallet-based participation ability is a separate activation-gated framework inside the FUZE token model.

Controlled circulation supports supply clarity and disciplined release. It does not guarantee token price, token demand, liquidity, listing, market support, payout, income, yield, profit, exit, or investment return.

## 4. Core Model

### 4.1 Controlled Circulation Definition

Controlled circulation means that FUZE tokens enter active circulation only through a defined purpose, approved release logic, and relevant policy controls.

| Control Area | Public Meaning |
|---|---|
| Allocation purpose | Each token category has a defined reason to exist |
| Vault control | Tokens remain in category-specific vaults, contracts, or controlled accounts before release where practical |
| Eligibility | Some releases require user, contributor, holder, partner, community, or legacy-holder eligibility |
| Vesting / lockup | Some releases occur over time rather than immediately |
| Product readiness | Some releases depend on product, utility, or platform readiness |
| Compliance readiness | Some releases depend on legal, jurisdiction, compliance, and policy review |
| Governance control | Some releases require approval, timelock, multisig, policy review, or reporting |
| Public reporting | Releases can be shown through public-safe dashboards, updates, or documents where appropriate |
| Risk boundary | Releases do not create token price, liquidity, listing, payout, or return promises |

Controlled circulation is a public trust policy.

It does not convert every allocation into immediate liquid supply.

### 4.2 Controlled Circulation and Market Boundary

Controlled circulation must be understood as supply discipline, not market control.

Controlled circulation can include:

- lockups
- vesting
- release schedules
- vault separation
- supply reports
- treasury controls
- unlock reporting
- allocation boundaries
- public circulation clarity
- purpose-based release logic
- eligibility-controlled claims
- milestone-controlled partner releases
- strategic reserve deployment
- liquidity-related operational controls

Controlled circulation avoids public promises that FUZE will hold a token price, defend a price level, guarantee a floor, guarantee buy pressure, guarantee exchange liquidity, or control the market.

Approved public language:

**Controlled circulation supports supply clarity and disciplined release. It does not guarantee market price, liquidity, listing, market support, or exit.**

### 4.3 Total Supply, Circulating Supply, and Locked Supply

FUZE public communication separates supply categories.

| Supply Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total supply | The full fixed FUZE token supply of 500,000,000 FUZE |
| Circulating supply | Tokens available in market or user circulation under the reporting method used |
| Locked supply | Tokens restricted by lockup, vesting, contract, policy, or custody rule |
| Reserved supply | Tokens held for future ecosystem, treasury, product, liquidity, partner, reserve, or defined purpose |
| Vested supply | Tokens that have passed vesting rules but may still require policy, transfer, or release control |
| Unvested supply | Tokens not yet available under vesting rules |
| Treasury supply | Tokens held for treasury, reserve, ecosystem, or operational purposes |
| Liquidity-related supply | Tokens allocated or used for liquidity-related purposes, subject to policy and boundary controls |
| Claimable supply | Tokens available for claim by eligible parties under defined rules where active |
| Non-circulating hold | Tokens held under policy, reserve, lockup, or governance control and not treated as active circulation |

Public reporting identifies which category is being discussed.

Boundary:

A supply category label explains status. It does not create market guarantees.

### 4.5 Release Type Framework

FUZE uses release types to make token movement easier to understand.

| Release Type | Public Meaning | Best-Fit Categories |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent Lock / Non-Circulating Hold | Tokens remain locked or held and do not enter circulation unless final policy changes under governance controls | Foundation Reserve, Transparency / Stability Reserve where applicable |
| Claim-Based Release | Tokens become claimable by eligible parties under defined rules | BOARD / Surfboard Migration, selected Community Participation programs |
| Vesting Release | Tokens release over time to support long-term alignment | Team Allocation, Advisors / Strategic Contributors |
| Earned Release | Tokens release after participation, contribution, campaign completion, product usage, or eligibility rules | Holder Incentives, Community Participation Allocation |
| Milestone Release | Tokens release when partner, ecosystem, integration, or contribution milestones are approved | Ecosystem Growth & Partnerships |
| Strategic Reserve Deployment | Tokens move only for approved platform, treasury, or strategic purposes | Treasury Reserve, Foundation Reserve |
| Controlled Operational Deployment | Tokens move for market-structure or operational purposes under policy controls | Liquidity & Market Operations |
| Exceptional Reserve Use | Tokens move only for trust, transparency, or stability needs under governance controls | Transparency / Stability Reserve |

This framework helps public readers understand that token movement is not one uniform unlock schedule.

Vault-based control helps prevent allocation misuse and supports public transparency.

Boundary:

A vault balance explains controlled holding status. It does not automatically represent circulating supply, approved distributable value, payout supply, price support, liquidity assurance, or public treasury access.
