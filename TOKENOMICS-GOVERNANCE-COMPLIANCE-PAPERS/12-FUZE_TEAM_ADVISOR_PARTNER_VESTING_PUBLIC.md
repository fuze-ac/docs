# FUZE Team Advisor Partner Vesting

## Executive Summary

FUZE Team Advisor Partner Vesting explains how FUZE treats team, advisor, partner, contributor, builder, operator, and strategic allocation vesting, lockups, release controls, wallet records, and public communication boundaries.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Team, advisor, partner, and contributor allocations exist to support long-term ecosystem alignment, product delivery, strategic cooperation, technical contribution, operational contribution, and platform growth where properly defined.

Vesting and lockups help reduce confusion around token release. They also help show that allocations are not automatically circulating, not automatically sold, not automatically liquid, and not automatically available for unrestricted use.

A vested token is not automatically a sold token.

An unlocked token is not automatically market circulation.

A team allocation is not community payout supply.

A partner allocation is not a guaranteed partnership outcome.

An advisor allocation is not a guaranteed business result.

A contributor allocation is not a guaranteed product delivery result.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token. Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails. Stablecoin compensation may pay for work or services, but it is separate from FUZE token allocation and wallet-based participation.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token, not automatic for every wallet, and not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, or investment return.

FUZE’s public position is:

Team, advisor, partner, contributor, builder, operator, and strategic allocations should be tied to defined purpose, vesting or lockup conditions where appropriate, release controls, wallet records, and public-safe reporting.

The vesting position has several parts.

| Area | FUZE Position |

| Long-term alignment | Allocations should support long-term contribution and ecosystem alignment. |
| Purpose-specific allocation | Each allocation category should have a clear role. |
| Vesting discipline | Tokens may release over time instead of becoming available all at once. |

## 6. Vesting, Lockup, Unlock, and Release

FUZE should use vesting terms carefully.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vesting | A schedule or condition that makes tokens available over time or after defined milestones. |
| Lockup | A restriction that prevents transfer or release during a defined period or condition. |
| Unlock | The end or change of a lockup or vesting restriction. |
| Release | The movement or availability of tokens under defined rules. |
| Circulation | Tokens considered available in market or user circulation under the reporting method used. |
| Transfer | Movement from one wallet or vault to another. |
| Sale | A market or private sale transaction. |

These terms are not interchangeable.

An unlock is not automatically a sale.

A release is not automatically market circulation.

A transfer is not automatically a public distribution.

Boundary:

Vesting and release terms should explain status, not predict price or market behavior.

## 25. Vesting and Circulating Supply

Vesting affects circulating supply, but the relationship must be explained carefully.

Unvested tokens are usually not treated as freely circulating.

Vested tokens may or may not be circulating depending on:

- whether they are released
- whether they remain locked
- whether they remain in a controlled vault
- whether they are transferable
- whether legal restrictions apply
- whether custody rules apply
- whether the reporting method counts them
- whether transfer restrictions apply

FUZE should clearly explain whether a vesting event affects circulating supply.

Boundary:

Vesting completion does not guarantee market sale, liquidity, price movement, or market stability.

## 37. Vesting Development Direction

FUZE team, advisor, partner, and contributor vesting can develop through phases.

### Phase 1: Define Allocation Categories

Define team, advisor, partner, contributor, strategic contributor, service provider, and ecosystem allocation categories.

### Phase 2: Define Purpose and Boundaries

Define vesting, lockup, unlock, release, and transfer conditions where applicable.

Use purpose-specific vaults or wallet categories for allocation tracking where practical.

### Phase 5: Add Release Controls

Use approval workflows, multisig, timelock, vesting contracts, and transfer restrictions where appropriate.

### Phase 6: Build Public-Safe Reporting

Publish category-level allocation reports, vesting status, lockup status, release status, and movement summaries where appropriate.

### Phase 7: Clarify Circulating Supply Impact

Explain whether vesting, unlocks, releases, or transfers affect circulating supply.

Keep stablecoin compensation and Platform Credits separate from FUZE token allocations.

### Phase 9: Clarify Participation Boundaries

State that allocations do not automatically create wallet-based participation eligibility or claim rights.

These phases are development directions, not guaranteed launch dates.
