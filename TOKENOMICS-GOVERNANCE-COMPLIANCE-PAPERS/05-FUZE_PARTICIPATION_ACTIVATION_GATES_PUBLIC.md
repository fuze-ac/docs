# FUZE Participation Activation Gates

## Executive Summary

FUZE Participation Activation Gates explains the readiness gates required before any wallet-based participation claim logic can become active.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Wallet-based participation ability is a possible FUZE token-related framework where eligible FUZE-holding wallets may participate in approved distributable value from defined FUZE product revenue pools if the framework becomes active.

This ability is not a second token. It is not a public FPPU token. It is not automatic for every wallet. It is not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

Activation gates are required because wallet-based participation touches sensitive areas: product revenue, accounting, treasury controls, approved distributable value, wallet eligibility, privacy, jurisdiction rules, smart contracts, reports, audits, and public claims.

FUZE can build transparency and readiness rails early, including vault records, report hash registries, multisig controls, timelocks, conversion proof records, audit records, eligibility concepts, and snapshot logic where appropriate. However, live participation claim logic should not activate until legal, accounting, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, eligibility, privacy, and jurisdiction gates are ready.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token. Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails. Product revenue, if generated, requires reconciliation and does not automatically become approved distributable value.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, or investment return.

## 4. Readiness Is Different From Activation

FUZE may build readiness infrastructure before participation is active.

Readiness means FUZE prepares systems, records, contracts, reporting, and controls.

Activation means claim logic or participation distribution becomes live under approved rules.

These are different.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Concept defined | The public model is explained. |
| Readiness infrastructure | Transparency, records, vaults, reports, and compatibility systems may be prepared. |
| Private review | Legal, accounting, treasury, audit, and jurisdiction review may occur. |
| Testnet or prototype | Smart-contract logic may be tested without public economic activation. |
| Gate-ready | Required gates are ready for review. |
| Activated | Eligible-wallet participation claim logic becomes active under approved rules. |
| Paused | Activation or claims may be paused because of legal, technical, treasury, audit, or reporting issue. |

FUZE can be transparent about readiness without saying participation is active.

Boundary:

Readiness does not mean activation. Activation does not mean guaranteed ongoing claim availability.

## 29. Activation Gate Development Direction

Participation activation gates can develop through phases.

### Phase 1: Public Language Gate

Define wallet-based participation ability as public language and avoid public FPPU terminology.

### Phase 2: One-Token Alignment Gate

Keep participation inside the FUZE token model and confirm that Platform Credits remain separate.

### Phase 3: Revenue and Accounting Gate

Define product revenue pools, excluded values, reconciliation, reserves, costs, refunds, fees, taxes, and approved distributable value policy.

### Phase 4: Treasury and Control Gate

Build vault separation, multisig, timelock, treasury approvals, and reserve policy.

### Phase 5: Reporting and Audit Gate

Build report hashes, review process, audit records, public-safe dashboards, dispute windows, and correction process.

### Phase 6: Smart-Contract Readiness Gate

Build and test readiness contracts, then review activation contracts only when required gates are ready.

### Phase 7: Eligibility, Privacy, and Jurisdiction Gate

Define eligible wallets, custody treatment, OTC treatment, private verification, public wallet records, and jurisdiction rules.

### Phase 8: Activation Review Gate

Activate claim logic only if all gates are ready, reviewed, documented, and approved.

### Phase 9: Ongoing Monitoring Gate

Continue monitoring legal, accounting, treasury, audit, reporting, smart-contract, eligibility, privacy, jurisdiction, and operator conditions after activation.

These phases are development directions, not guaranteed launch dates.
