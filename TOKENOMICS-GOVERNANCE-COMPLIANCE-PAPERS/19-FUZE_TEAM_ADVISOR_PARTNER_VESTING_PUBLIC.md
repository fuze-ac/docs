# FUZE Team Advisor Partner Vesting

## Executive Summary

FUZE Team Advisor Partner Vesting explains how FUZE treats team, advisor, partner, strategic contributor, builder, operator, service provider, and private seed-related token exposure through vesting, lockups, milestone release, contribution records, revocation controls, vault separation, stablecoin compensation separation, and public-safe reporting.

FUZE uses one ecosystem token only: **FUZE token**.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, community participation structures, and wallet-based participation ability where activated under final policy.

Team, advisor, partner, and strategic contributor allocations exist to support long-term ecosystem alignment, product delivery, strategic cooperation, technical contribution, operating contribution, public reporting, governance readiness, and platform growth where properly defined.

FUZE applies the principle:

**Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation. Platform Credits power product usage.**

This means contributors can be paid for real work through USDT, USDC, fiat-equivalent payments, milestone payments, product revenue, approved treasury operations, or approved Public Vault Access proceeds where applicable. FUZE token allocations remain focused on long-term alignment, contribution recognition, ecosystem participation, governance readiness, and product utility.

Vesting and lockups help reduce confusion around token release. They also show that allocations are not automatically circulating, not automatically sold, not automatically liquid, and not automatically available for unrestricted use.

A vested token is not automatically a sold token.

An unlocked token is not automatically market circulation.

A team allocation is not community payout supply.

A partner allocation is not a guaranteed partnership outcome.

An advisor allocation is not a guaranteed business result.

A contributor allocation is not a guaranteed product delivery result.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails.

Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token, not automatic for every wallet, and not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

FUZE does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, approved distributable value, wallet eligibility, or investment return.

## 5. How the System Works

### 5.1 Vesting, Lockup, Unlock, Release, Transfer, Sale, and Circulation

FUZE uses vesting terms carefully.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vesting | Schedule or condition that makes tokens available over time or after defined milestones |
| Lockup | Restriction that prevents transfer or release during a defined period or condition |
| Unlock | End or change of a lockup or vesting restriction |
| Release | Movement or availability of tokens under defined rules |
| Transfer | Movement from one wallet, vault, contract, or account to another |
| Sale | Market or private sale transaction |
| Circulation | Tokens considered available in market or user circulation under the reporting method used |
| Claim | Token access by an eligible party under active claim rules |
| Revocation | Cancellation, return, pause, freeze, or reassignment of unvested or unearned allocation under policy |

These terms are not interchangeable.

An unlock is not automatically a sale.

A release is not automatically market circulation.

A transfer is not automatically a public distribution.

A vested token is not automatically sold.

Boundary:

Vesting and release terms explain status. They do not predict price or market behavior.
