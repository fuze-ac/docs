# FUZE FPPU Risk Disclosure
## Executive Summary
This paper provides the public risk disclosure for **FPPU — FUZE Profit Participation Unit**.
FUZE defines FPPU as a separate profit participation unit connected to approved distributable net profit from the FUZE platform. FPPU is separate from FUZE token, Platform Credits, stablecoin work payment, product usage, and general ecosystem participation.
FUZE separates:
| System | Public Role |
|---|---|
| Platform Credits | Product usage |
| FUZE token | Ecosystem utility, ZAGA participation, wallet-aware access, community participation, and governance-aware direction where applicable |
| FPPU | Eligible profit participation unit connected to approved distributable net profit |
| USDT | Distribution currency for approved FPPU distributions and one supported product-payment rail where applicable |
| Stablecoins / fiat / app-store proceeds | Revenue collection, work payment, treasury, or conversion sources depending on the payment channel |
FPPU is designed to support a Web3-native distribution model where eligible holders can claim USDT only when approved distributable net profit exists, the Revenue-to-Distribution Control System is completed, audit review is completed, multisig and timelock controls are satisfied, and USDT is deposited into the FPPU Distribution Smart Contract.
FPPU does not guarantee quarterly income, yearly income, fixed yield, dividend amount, passive income, payout amount, distribution frequency, liquidity, listing, resale opportunity, redemption, buyback, token price appreciation, or exit.
This paper explains the main risks connected to FPPU, including product revenue risk, distribution risk, audit risk, smart contract risk, treasury risk, payment-channel risk, conversion risk, legal and regulatory risk, tax risk, liquidity risk, eligibility risk, transfer risk, operational risk, AI risk, community audit risk, and market risk.
## 1. FUZE Position
FUZE position:
**FPPU creates a transparent profit participation framework for eligible holders, but it does not remove business, legal, technical, market, tax, treasury, product, AI, security, operational, or regulatory risk.**
FPPU is designed around transparency and controls, not guaranteed return.
| Risk Boundary | FUZE Position |
|---|---|
| No fixed distribution | FPPU does not promise fixed distribution amount or frequency |
| No fixed yield | FPPU does not promise APY, passive income, dividend rate, or fixed income |
| No automatic payout | Revenue must pass reconciliation, deductions, reserves, audit, approval, and contract funding |
| No guaranteed profit | FUZE may not generate distributable net profit |
| No guaranteed liquidity | FPPU may not have a buyer, venue, market, or resale path |
| No unrestricted access | FPPU may require eligibility, verification, wallet controls, transfer restrictions, and jurisdiction rules |
| No AI-only assurance | AI-assisted audit supports review but does not guarantee perfect accuracy |
| No community-audit guarantee | Community review improves transparency but cannot guarantee all issues are found |
| No one-person control | Multisig, timelock, vaults, reporting, and audit controls reduce but do not eliminate operational risk |
FUZE uses FPPU to make profit participation more transparent while keeping risk disclosure visible.
## 2. Public Context
FUZE products can generate revenue through multiple product and payment paths, including Platform Credits, subscriptions, add-ons, partner packages, sponsored visibility, managed services, product-specific actions, and selected payment channels such as USDT, Telegram Stars, Apple App Store, Google Play, card payments, bank transfer, and partner payments.
FPPU distributions depend on actual platform performance and the Revenue-to-Distribution Control System.
The core flow is:
```text
Platform revenue
→ source tagging
→ reconciliation
→ fee/refund/chargeback/tax/cost/reserve handling
→ approved distributable net profit calculation
→ FPPU distribution percentage
→ AI-assisted audit
→ community audit
→ dispute/correction period
→ multisig approval
→ timelock where applicable
→ USDT deposit into FPPU Distribution Smart Contract
→ eligible holder snapshot
→ wallet claim

Every step carries risk. FPPU risk disclosure explains those risks before FUZE updates wider public papers, investor papers, and tokenomics papers.

* multisig signers are unavailable,

9. Conclusion

FPPU gives FUZE a transparent profit participation model, but it also introduces specific risks that must remain visible.

The strongest FUZE position is:

Platform Credits are for product usage. FUZE token is for ecosystem utility and participation. FPPU is for eligible profit participation. USDT is used for approved FPPU distributions.

FPPU distributions depend on real product revenue, reconciliation, deductions, reserves, approved distributable net profit, AI-assisted audit, community audit, dispute resolution, multisig approval, timelock controls, contract funding, snapshot logic, and holder eligibility.

FPPU does not guarantee income, payout amount, distribution frequency, fixed yield, liquidity, listing, resale opportunity, token price appreciation, or exit.

FUZE uses this disclosure to keep the FPPU model transparent, Web3-native, public-company-inspired, and risk-aware.
