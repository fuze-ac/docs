# Team / Advisor / Partner Vesting

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses long-term vesting and milestone-based release rules for team members, advisors, strategic contributors, ecosystem partners, and any private Seed Round token-related exposure where applicable.

The purpose is to align long-term builders, advisors, contributors, and partners with the FUZE ecosystem while reducing immediate liquid sell-pressure risk. FUZE does not use team, advisor, partner, or Seed-related token exposure as short-term extraction. FUZE uses vesting, cliffs, milestone conditions, lockups, revocation rules, stablecoin compensation separation, and aggregate public reporting to strengthen trust.

FUZE applies the principle:

**Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation. Platform Credits power product usage.**

This means contributors can be compensated for real work through USDT, USDC, fiat-equivalent payments, milestone payments, product revenue, approved treasury operations, or approved Public Vault Access proceeds where applicable. FUZE token allocations remain focused on long-term alignment, contribution recognition, ecosystem participation, governance readiness, and product utility.

This paper defines the public vesting model for team, advisors, strategic contributors, ecosystem partners, and Seed-related token exposure where applicable. It explains allocation relationships, vesting logic, milestone release, revocation rules, reporting direction, public boundaries, and risk safeguards.

## 1. FUZE Position

FUZE uses long-term vesting for team members, advisors, strategic contributors, ecosystem partners, and private Seed Round token-related exposure where included.

FUZE position:

**Team, advisor, partner, and Seed-related token exposure is structured for long-term alignment, contribution accountability, controlled circulation, and public trust.**

The vesting model follows FUZE’s tokenomics principle:

**Purpose-specific allocation. Vault-based control. Controlled circulation. Product-first utility. Long-term ecosystem alignment.**

FUZE does not present team, advisor, partner, or Seed-related token exposure as immediate liquid inventory. These categories are controlled through vesting, milestone release, lockups, role documentation, contribution rules, stablecoin compensation separation, and public-safe reporting.

FUZE launch focus is **HerHelp.com** and **ZAGA.io**, supported by FUZE Core Platform rails and accelerated internally by **Botmad**.

## 6. Risk Boundaries and Safeguards

FUZE applies risk boundaries around team, advisor, partner, and Seed-related vesting.

### 6.1 General Token Boundary

FUZE does not guarantee token price, liquidity, listing, profit, return, payout, or market performance.

### 6.2 Platform Credits Boundary

Platform Credits are for product usage and are not investment assets, payout assets, or FUZE tokens.

### 6.3 Stablecoin Compensation Boundary

Stablecoin compensation is for approved work payment, contractor payment, contributor payment, advisor payment, vendor payment, service payment, or partner deliverable payment where applicable. It is not token-holder income, investment return, profit participation, investment-yield framing, or market-performance payment.

### 6.4 Seed Round Boundary

FUZE’s Seed Round discussions are private strategic fundraising conversations. Full details, structure, and terms are shared privately with qualified interested parties. This is not a public token sale.

### 6.5 Vesting Boundary

Vesting aligns long-term contributors, advisors, partners, and strategic participants. Vesting does not guarantee liquidity, price performance, market outcome, profit, payout, or future utility.

### 6.6 Partner Milestone Boundary

Partner milestone release supports approved ecosystem work. It does not guarantee partnership success, customer growth, revenue, market outcome, listing result, or token performance.

### 6.7 Revocation Boundary

Revocation rules apply to unvested or unearned allocations under final policy and agreements. Revocation handling remains subject to legal, contractual, governance, accounting, and jurisdiction review.

### 6.8 Confidentiality Boundary

FUZE can report aggregate vesting and release categories while protecting private personal information, compensation details, employment details, contractor agreements, advisor agreements, partner terms, investor terms, tax records, and sensitive business records.

### 6.9 Development Standards Boundary

FUZE uses standards-inspired or standards-aligned processes. FUZE does not claim formal certification unless certification has been completed.
