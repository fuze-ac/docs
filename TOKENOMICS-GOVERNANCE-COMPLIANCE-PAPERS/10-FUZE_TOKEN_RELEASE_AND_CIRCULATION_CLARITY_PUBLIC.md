# FUZE Token Release and Circulation Clarity

## Executive Summary

FUZE Token Release and Circulation Clarity explains how FUZE communicates token release, unlocks, vesting, circulating supply, reserved supply, locked supply, vault movement, and supply reporting.

FUZE uses one token only: FUZE token.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, and wallet-based participation ability.

Token release and circulation clarity is important because public readers, investors, community members, exchanges, partners, and product users need to understand the difference between total supply, circulating supply, locked supply, reserved supply, vested supply, treasury supply, liquidity-related supply, and tokens held in vaults.

A token release is not always a market sale.

An unlock is not always immediate circulation.

A vault transfer is not always public distribution.

A treasury balance is not approved distributable value.

A reserve balance is not payout supply.

A liquidity-related allocation is not a liquidity guarantee.

Controlled circulation and clear reporting help FUZE reduce confusion, but they do not guarantee token price, listing, liquidity, trading volume, market support, exit, payout, income, yield, profit, or investment return.

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token. Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails. Wallet-based participation ability is not a second token, not automatic for every wallet, and not active as a guaranteed public payout right.

## 3. Why Circulation Clarity Matters

Circulation clarity matters because token supply movement can affect trust and interpretation.

Without clarity, public readers may confuse:

- total supply with circulating supply
- unlocked supply with sold supply
- vested supply with circulating supply
- treasury supply with public market supply
- reserve supply with payout supply
- liquidity-related supply with guaranteed liquidity
- ecosystem allocation with immediate community rewards
- team allocation with market circulation
- vault transfer with market sale
- stablecoin movement with token release
- Platform Credit records with FUZE token supply
- wallet-based participation readiness with active payout

FUZE uses circulation clarity to reduce these misunderstandings.

The goal is not to control the market.

The goal is to explain what token movement means and what it does not mean.

Boundary:

Clear supply reporting does not guarantee market stability, token demand, token price, liquidity, listing, exit, or investment return.

## 5. Total Supply

Total supply is the full FUZE token supply created or defined.

Total supply is not the same as circulating supply.

Total supply can include:

- circulating tokens
- locked tokens
- reserved tokens
- treasury tokens
- team tokens
- advisor tokens
- partner tokens
- ecosystem tokens
- liquidity-related tokens
- community tokens
- product utility tokens
- unvested tokens
- tokens held in contracts or vaults

Public reporting should avoid using total supply as if all tokens are immediately available in the market.

Boundary:

Total supply does not represent immediate market circulation, approved distributable value, payout supply, or available liquidity.

## 6. Circulating Supply

Circulating supply is the portion of FUZE token considered available in public or market circulation under the reporting method used.

Circulating supply may include tokens that are transferable and not locked by schedule, contract, or policy.

Circulating supply may exclude:

- locked tokens
- unvested tokens
- treasury reserves
- team vesting tokens
- advisor vesting tokens
- partner locked tokens
- ecosystem reserve tokens
- tokens held in smart contracts under lock
- tokens held for future allocation
- tokens restricted by legal or jurisdiction condition
- tokens reserved for product utility
- tokens reserved for liquidity-related operations but not yet circulating

Circulating supply reporting should explain the method used.

Boundary:

Circulating supply reporting does not guarantee market liquidity, trading volume, token demand, exchange access, or price stability.

## 7. Locked Supply

Locked supply means tokens are restricted from normal movement by lockup, vesting, contract, policy, custody, legal condition, or defined release rule.

Locked supply may include:

- treasury lockups
- reserve lockups
- ecosystem allocation locks
- liquidity-related locks
- product utility locks
- vesting contracts

Locked supply is not circulating supply unless the reporting method specifically defines otherwise.

Boundary:

Locked supply does not guarantee future price stability or prevent market risk after unlock.

## 9. Vested and Unvested Supply

Vesting explains when tokens become available under a defined schedule.

Unvested supply means tokens have not yet passed the vesting condition.

Vested supply means tokens have passed the vesting condition.

However, vested does not always mean immediately sold, distributed, or circulated.

Vested tokens may still be:

- held in a vault
- subject to internal release policy
- subject to transfer review
- subject to legal restriction
- subject to lockup beyond vesting
- held by team, advisor, partner, or contributor wallets
- excluded from certain participation calculations where defined
- subject to reporting or compliance rules

Boundary:

Vesting completion does not guarantee selling, holding, price movement, liquidity, or market stability.
