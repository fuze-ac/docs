# FUZE Seed Round and Token-Related Exposure

## Executive Summary

FUZE describes its current fundraising direction as a **Private Strategic Seed Round** intended for selected strategic investors, operator-angels, early-stage partners, and other qualified parties whose capital, operating experience, distribution capability, technical expertise, or strategic relationships may support product-first execution.

The round is a private financing process.

It is not:

- a public token sale;
- a Public Vault Access Window;
- a community participation round;
- a wallet-based holder program;
- a product purchase;
- a Platform Credit program;
- a liquidity event;
- a listing event;
- or a promise of financial return.

Detailed valuation, instrument, rights, allocation, payment, token exposure, lock, vesting, transfer, governance, information, legal, tax, compliance, and closing terms belong in controlled private review and executed documents.

The controlling financing lifecycle is:

```text
approved fundraising objective and structure
-> preparation of company, product, financial, legal, token, risk, and diligence materials
-> controlled introduction
-> party qualification and confidentiality
-> diligence access
-> private term review
-> company and investor approvals
-> execution of final documents
-> settlement instruction verification
-> receipt and financing reconciliation
-> closing record
-> post-closing obligations, reporting, amendment, transfer, termination, or archive
```

Where an investment includes token-related exposure, a second and separate lifecycle applies:

```text
executed financing terms
-> approved token-exposure schedule
-> verified source allocation and available capacity
-> grant, lock, vesting, claim, custody, transfer, cancellation, and release rules
-> token-exposure record activation
-> condition and schedule monitoring
-> vesting or entitlement calculation
-> release approval
-> claim, custody transfer, lock, or staged release
-> allocation, vault, recipient, and circulation reconciliation
-> amendment, cancellation, return, correction, closure, or archive
```

The financing and token ledgers must remain separate.

A signed financing document does not automatically release FUZE.

A financing payment does not automatically activate token exposure.

Activated token exposure does not mean the full amount is vested.

Vested does not mean released.

Released does not automatically mean transferable, circulating, liquid, listed, or valuable at any particular price.

Fundraising receipts remain separate from:

- product revenue;
- Platform Credit receipts;
- community participation receipts;
- stablecoin compensation;
- approved distributable value;
- liquidity pairing capital;
- and market-operation income.

Every completed financing should identify:

- transaction identifier and version;
- qualified party;
- financing instrument;
- commitment amount and currency;
- executed documents;
- closing conditions;
- settlement route;
- verified receipts;
- ownership, contractual, governance, accounting, tax, and reporting treatment;
- continuing restrictions;
- current status;
- and current-as-of date.

Every token-related exposure record should separately identify:

- token-exposure identifier and version;
- source allocation and source vault;
- maximum approved FUZE amount;
- contractual basis;
- activation event;
- lock, vesting, milestone, claim, staged-release, or custody treatment;
- transfer restrictions;
- cancellation and termination treatment;
- release authority;
- circulation classification;
- public-reporting treatment;
- current status;
- and current-as-of date.

This paper defines the public process and boundary for private seed financing and any associated token exposure.

It does not publish offering terms, valuation, investor identity, commitment, closing amount, token amount, price, vesting schedule, claim window, release instruction, listing, liquidity, income, revenue share, or financial return.

Fundraising structure and use-of-funds communication remain governed by [FUZE Round Structure and Use of Funds](../INVESTOR-PARTNER-PAPERS/12-FUZE_ROUND_STRUCTURE_AND_USE_OF_FUNDS_PUBLIC.md).

Investor-specific public risk communication remains governed by [FUZE Investor Risk Disclosure](../INVESTOR-PARTNER-PAPERS/17-FUZE_INVESTOR_RISK_DISCLOSURE_PUBLIC.md).

Token allocation and release authority remain governed by [FUZE Token Allocation Table](02-FUZE_TOKEN_ALLOCATION_TABLE_PUBLIC.md) and [FUZE Vault-by-Vault Release Rules](15-FUZE_VAULT_BY_VAULT_RELEASE_RULES_PUBLIC.md).

| Status | Evidence-backed meaning | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|

| Open to selected private discussions | FUZE is engaging selected qualified parties. | Public availability or investor eligibility |
| Introduction in progress | Initial strategic-fit communication has begun. | Qualification or diligence access |

| In diligence | Authorized private review is underway. | Agreement on terms or commitment |

| Corrected | An authorized record fixes an error while preserving history. | New commercial terms |
| Archived | Historical records remain retained. | Current fundraising activity |

### Status Discipline

FUZE should not use:

- `funded` before verified settlement;
- `closed` before the applicable closing conditions and receipt reconciliation are complete;
- `investor` for an introduced or interested party;
- `committed` for an informal indication;
- `strategic partner` for a discussion without an approved relationship;
- or `token allocation confirmed` before the token source, schedule, and approval are complete.

## Reconciliation

### Financing Reconciliation

```text
executed commitments
- unsettled commitments
- failed or cancelled amounts
- refunds or reversals
+/- corrections
= verified financing receipts
```

### Treasury Receipt Reconciliation

```text
verified financing receipts
- approved fees and deductions
- refunds
- reversals or chargebacks
- approved transfers or conversions
+/- corrections
= closing financing-receipt balance or deployed amount
```

### Token-Exposure Reconciliation

```text
approved token exposure
= unvested
+ vested unreleased
+ claim funded or claimable under the stated method
+ released
+ cancelled or returned
+ disputed or pending final classification
+/- corrections
```

The active methodology should prevent double counting among claim-funded, claimable, released, locked, and returned states.

### Source-Allocation Reconciliation

```text
opening source-allocation balance
- confirmed token releases or claim-funding transfers
- active approved commitments not already deducted
+ cancelled commitments
+ verified returns and recoveries
+/- corrections
= closing available or controlled source balance
```

## Key Takeaways

- FUZE describes its current fundraising direction as a Private Strategic Seed Round for selected qualified parties.
- The round is a private financing process, not a public token sale, community round, Public Vault Access Window, or wallet-based participation program.
- Qualification, diligence, term review, execution, settlement, closing, and post-closing obligations are separate states.
- Detailed terms, valuation, investor rights, investor identity, token exposure, and closing information belong in controlled private review and executed documents.
- Financing receipts remain separate from product revenue, Platform Credits, compensation, community receipts, liquidity capital, and approved distributable value.
- Any token-related exposure is optional, requires its own approved source allocation and schedule, and must remain separate from the financing ledger.
- Financing execution or payment does not automatically activate, vest, claim, or release FUZE.
- Token approval, activation, vesting, claimability, release, transferability, and circulation are separate states.
- Use of funds requires separate budgets, approvals, payments, accounting, and reporting after financing is received.
- Transfers, amendments, termination, cancellation, return, and recovery should preserve both financing and token history.
- Public reporting may use approved aggregate status while protecting investor identity, private terms, beneficial ownership, bank and wallet details, negotiations, and privileged records.
- Private financing or token exposure does not guarantee product adoption, listing, liquidity, demand, price support, income, revenue share, return of capital, or financial return.
