# FUZE Vault and Reserve Policy

## Executive Summary

FUZE Vault and Reserve Policy explains how FUZE uses vaults, reserves, treasury controls, allocation separation, public-safe visibility, and governance safeguards to support controlled circulation and long-term ecosystem alignment.

FUZE uses one ecosystem token only: **FUZE token**.

FUZE token is the single ecosystem token of FUZE. It supports product-connected utility, ecosystem participation, platform alignment, governance direction where applicable, community participation structures, and wallet-based participation ability where activated under required controls.

Vaults and reserves are control structures.

They help FUZE separate token allocations, treasury assets, stablecoin flows, product revenue records, operating funds, ecosystem reserves, liquidity-related resources, team allocations, advisor allocations, partner allocations, community allocations, migration allocations, reporting records, and possible future participation-related records where applicable.

Vault and reserve policy supports:

- purpose separation
- treasury discipline
- controlled circulation
- public-safe visibility
- reserve protection
- token release clarity
- multisig and timelock readiness where appropriate
- allocation-specific release logic
- public reporting discipline
- risk boundary clarity

Platform Credits are product usage credits and are separate from FUZE token.

Stablecoins are payment, settlement, treasury, and operational compensation rails.

Wallet-based participation ability is a separate activation-gated framework inside the FUZE token model.

Vault and reserve policy supports transparency, separation, and discipline. It does not guarantee payout, income, dividend, yield, profit, token price, listing, liquidity, market support, exit, game earnings, business revenue, user growth, community growth, approved distributable value, wallet eligibility, or investment return.

## 2. Reader Problem This Paper Solves

Vault and reserve language can confuse public readers when every visible wallet, reserve, treasury balance, claim vault, product revenue record, or token allocation is treated as the same thing.

Readers may ask:

- What is a vault?
- What is a reserve?
- Does a vault balance mean tokens are circulating?
- Does a treasury vault mean public access?
- Does a reserve balance mean payout supply?
- Does a stablecoin wallet mean approved distributable value?
- Does a public vault view give community control over treasury assets?
- Which vault connects to Community Participation?
- Which vault connects to BOARD / Surfboard Migration?
- Which vault connects to team and advisor vesting?
- Which vault connects to liquidity-related operations?
- How does FUZE report vault movement without exposing private information?

This paper solves that problem by defining vaults and reserves as purpose-based control structures.

The key answer is:

Vaults organize assets and records by purpose. Reserves protect future needs and operating discipline. Public visibility helps transparency, but it does not create unrestricted access, payout rights, or market guarantees.

## 3. FUZE Public Position

FUZE’s public position is:

**Vaults and reserves are used to separate purposes, strengthen controls, improve public understanding, support controlled circulation, and improve reporting discipline.**

Vault and reserve policy has six core ideas.

| Idea | Public Meaning |
|---|---|
| Purpose separation | Assets, allocations, and records are organized by role and use where practical |
| Treasury discipline | Sensitive movements use review, approvals, role separation, and controls where appropriate |
| Controlled circulation | Token release and movement follow allocation purpose, vault logic, lockups, vesting, eligibility, and policy controls |
| Public-safe visibility | Some vault activity may be visible without exposing private identity or confidential records |
| Reserve protection | Reserves protect operations, obligations, risk coverage, treasury stability, and future platform needs |
| Public boundary | Vault balances and reserves require context before readers interpret them |

FUZE uses vaults and reserves to avoid mixing:

- product usage with token circulation
- Platform Credits with FUZE token
- stablecoin payments with wallet-based participation
- treasury assets with approved distributable value
- team allocation with community allocation
- migration allocation with public community participation
- liquidity-related resources with market-outcome promises
- reserves with payout pools
- product revenue records with gross receipts
- game values with real-world distributable value
- public visibility with unrestricted public control

The policy is designed to make the system clearer.

It is not designed to promise financial outcomes.

## 4. Core Model

### 4.1 What a Vault Means in FUZE

A vault is a wallet, contract, account, ledger category, or controlled record used to separate assets or records by purpose.

A vault may hold or track:

- FUZE token
- stablecoins
- product revenue records
- treasury balances
- reserve balances
- ecosystem allocation
- team allocation
- advisor allocation
- partner allocation
- community allocation
- migration allocation
- liquidity-related resources
- game utility allocation where defined
- report hashes
- conversion proof records
- audit records
- eligibility records where applicable
- claim-related records only if an activated framework requires them

A vault has a clear purpose.

A public vault label helps readers understand why the vault exists.

Boundary:

A vault is a control and record structure. It requires purpose, release logic, access rules, controls, and reporting context before readers interpret its meaning.

### 4.2 What a Reserve Means in FUZE

A reserve is a portion of assets, tokens, stablecoins, funds, credits, or internal records held back for a defined purpose.

Reserves protect the platform from operational risk, support long-term planning, and prevent premature spending, release, circulation, or distribution.

Possible reserve categories include:

- operating reserve
- treasury reserve
- tax reserve
- refund reserve
- chargeback reserve
- legal and compliance reserve
- audit reserve
- security reserve
- AI cost reserve
- infrastructure reserve
- product development reserve
- incident response reserve
- stablecoin reserve
- token reserve
- liquidity-related reserve
- ecosystem reserve
- community reserve
- partner reserve
- game operations reserve
- future participation readiness reserve where appropriate

A reserve can be important even when it is not immediately used.

Boundary:

A reserve supports protection, readiness, and discipline. Any future use requires the relevant policy, approval, and reporting path.

### 4.3 Why Vault Separation Matters

Vault separation matters because FUZE includes products, Platform Credits, stablecoin flows, token allocations, ecosystem reserves, wallet records, possible participation readiness, public reporting, controlled access windows, and governance controls.

Without vault separation, public readers may confuse:

- treasury assets with approved distributable value
- stablecoin payments with wallet-based participation
- token allocations with circulating supply
- locked tokens with available tokens
- reserves with payout pools
- product revenue with gross receipts
- game values with real-world distributable value
- liquidity-related wallets with market-support promises
- team allocation with community allocation
- BOARD / Surfboard Migration with Community Participation Allocation
- Platform Credit records with token rights
- public vault visibility with unrestricted public treasury control
- public vault access windows with public investment offers

Vault separation reduces this confusion.

It also helps FUZE explain token movement, product revenue records, reserves, circulation, public access windows, and reporting with more discipline.

Boundary:

Vault separation improves clarity, but it does not guarantee product success, revenue, token price, liquidity, payout, wallet eligibility, approved distributable value, or investment return.

FUZE may use several vault categories where practical.

| Vault Category | Purpose | Public Boundary |

| Liquidity-Related Vault | Supports liquidity-related operations where defined | Supports market-structure readiness without market guarantees |
| Stablecoin Treasury Vault | Holds stablecoin payment, settlement, or treasury assets | Payment and treasury rail, not FUZE token supply |

Not every vault category exists from day one.

The key policy is purpose separation.

### 4.6 Master Vault Rule Template

Each FUZE vault can be described through a shared public rule template.

| Rule Area | Public Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vault name | Public vault or allocation name |
| Allocation amount | Amount of FUZE assigned to the category where applicable |
| Allocation percentage | Percentage of the fixed 500,000,000 FUZE supply where applicable |

| Release logic | How tokens, assets, or records can move from the vault |

This shared structure makes each vault easier to understand and compare.

## 5. Token, Credits, Stablecoins, and Wallet Relationship

Vaults and reserves can hold or track different types of assets and records.

| System | Public Role | Vault / Reserve Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| FUZE token | Single ecosystem token | Token vaults separate allocation purpose, release rules, vesting, lockups, and circulation status |
| Platform Credits | Product usage credits | Credit records remain product usage records and do not become FUZE token vault balances |
| Stablecoins | Payment, settlement, treasury, and compensation rails | Stablecoin treasury vaults support payment, reserves, compensation, and settlement |
| Product revenue | Revenue from FUZE products and services | Revenue records can support accounting, treasury, and reporting review |
| Approved distributable value | Reviewed value from defined product revenue pools where an active framework exists | Requires separate approval and activation context |
| Wallet-based participation ability | Possible activation-gated token-related framework | Any related records require eligibility, activation, privacy, reporting, and jurisdiction controls |

This relationship protects public clarity.

FUZE token vaults explain allocation and circulation.

Stablecoin vaults explain payment and treasury rails.

Platform Credits explain product usage.

Wallet-based participation remains controlled by its own activation gates.

## 8. Market, Legal, or Operational Context

### 8.1 Product-First Vault Logic

Vaults and reserves support FUZE’s product-first execution model.

FUZE launch focus is HerHelp.com and ZAGA.io, supported by FUZE Core Platform rails and accelerated internally by Botmad.

Vault and reserve policy helps:

- HerHelp use product-first rails without mixing product usage with token circulation
- ZAGA organize utility, game, and community surfaces
- Community Participation Allocation remain separate from migration allocation
- team and advisor allocations remain aligned through vesting
- stablecoin compensation reduce short-term token release pressure
- treasury and reserve balances remain purpose-specific
- public reporting communicate token movement clearly
- future wallet-based participation stay activation-gated

### 8.2 Public Vault Visibility

Public vault visibility can support trust by making selected vault categories, report hashes, allocation labels, release summaries, and status records visible where appropriate.

Public visibility is useful when it explains:

- what the vault is
- what allocation it belongs to
- why the vault exists
- whether tokens are locked, reserved, vested, claimable, or controlled
- what release rules apply
- what public boundaries apply

Public visibility is not the same as unrestricted public access.

Public vault visibility should not expose private operational records, identity records, legal records, accounting records, or sensitive treasury processes.

### 8.3 Public Vault Access Windows

Public Vault Access Windows are separate from Public Vault Visibility.

Public Vault Visibility means public-safe information about selected vaults can be shown.

Public Vault Access Windows mean selected eligibility-controlled access to defined vault tokens can occur only where final policy allows.

Public Vault Access Windows may require:

- eligibility rules
- participation window rules
- jurisdiction review
- compliance review
- lockup rules
- payment rules where applicable
- public reporting
- anti-abuse controls
- governance review

Access windows should be explained carefully.

They are not unrestricted public buying, unrestricted treasury access, or guaranteed token access.

### 8.4 Market Boundary

Vault and reserve policy is a control and transparency model.

It does not guarantee:

- token demand
- token price
- exchange listing
- liquidity
- trading volume
- market-making result
- price support
- resale availability
- exit opportunity
- investment return

Market access, custody, liquidity operations, exchange support, and public trading environments require separate boundary language and review.
