# BOARD / Surfboard Migration

## Executive Summary

FUZE uses the **BOARD / Surfboard Migration** allocation to protect eligible legacy-holder continuity while keeping FUZE fair for new participants, investors, product users, and future community members.

The migration allocation is **25,000,000 FUZE**, equal to **5.00%** of the fixed **500,000,000 FUZE** total supply. This allocation is reserved for eligible legacy-holder migration. It is not public sale inventory, not treasury inventory, not liquidity inventory, and not team or advisor compensation inventory.

The migration model recognizes the earlier BOARD / Surfboard context without turning legacy migration into a public token sale, market-price promise, liquidity promise, payout promise, or financial-return plan. It is a controlled continuity allocation for eligible legacy holders under final migration rules.

FUZE separates the BOARD / Surfboard Migration allocation from the Community Participation Allocation. Community Participation is **110,000,000 FUZE** and supports eligible community participation, product-user participation, contributor recognition, and future community programs. BOARD / Surfboard Migration is **25,000,000 FUZE** and supports eligible legacy-holder migration only.

FUZE also separates migration tokens from Platform Credits and stablecoin compensation.

**FUZE token is for ecosystem participation. Platform Credits are for product usage. Stablecoins pay for work. FUZE tokens align long-term participation.**

This paper defines the public migration model, allocation relationship, eligibility direction, controlled circulation relationship, reporting direction, public boundaries, and risk safeguards.
