Product positioning

Not only a bot. A full AI operating layer for community management.

CommunityLayer AI combines moderation, onboarding, approved-knowledge AI support, engagement automation, community health analytics, event support, recognition systems, access control, and growth campaigns in one AI SaaS product.

  • Protect members from scams, spam, phishing, fake admins, and harmful behavior.
  • Reduce repetitive admin work with AI summaries, queues, and suggested actions.
  • Answer repeated questions from approved community knowledge only.
  • Create daily and weekly engagement through prompts, polls, events, and recognition.
  • Track retention, trust, member happiness, community health, and growth quality.

Platform pain points

Built for Telegram, Facebook Groups, and Discord.

Each platform has different community pain, but all need trust, clarity, activity, and growth.

Telegram

Protection-first community OS

Telegram groups need anti-scam protection, fake admin detection, official link registry, CAPTCHA, spam control, AI FAQ, member recognition, admin logs, and referral tracking.

Facebook Groups

Quality and growth copilot

Facebook Groups need post quality scoring, scam seller detection, misinformation review, comment health monitoring, recurring post calendars, member spotlight, and group growth support.

Discord

Safety, role, and engagement AI

Discord servers need raid shield, role permission audit, bot permission warnings, onboarding checklist, channel audits, moderation queues, event planning, appeals, and server health reports.
